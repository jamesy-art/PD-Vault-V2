# Textile Homeware Designer

## Company

![Ulster Weavers logo](../../../assets/companies/ulster-weavers/profile/ulster-weavers-logo-preview.webp)

## Overview

Ulster Weavers

Teptile Homeware

Designer

IN-HOUSE GREATER MANCHESTER

We're looking for a creative and detail-oriented

Textile Homeware Designer to join our growing team.

In this role, you'll design original prints and patterns for a range of homeware products, including bedding, curtains, cushions, and accessories. You'll create innovative, trend-led collections, produce customer-focused artwork, develop repeat patterns, and work closely with suppliers to ensure exceptional quality and colour accuracy. You'll also present designs to customers and internal stakeholders, incorporating feedback to deliver outstanding products.

What we're looking for:
- Degree in Textile Design, Fashion Design, or a related subject.
- 2-5 years' experience in textile or homeware design.
- Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and CAD software.
- Strong knowledge of textile printing techniques, fabric properties, and pattern development.
- Excellent eye for colour, trends, and commercial design.

If you're passionate about creating beautiful, market-leading homeware designs, we'd love to hear from you.

Send CV's to Emma Yates, emmay@uwhome.com
