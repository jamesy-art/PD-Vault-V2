# Textiles Designer

## Company

![ILIV logo](../../../assets/companies/iliv/profile/iliv-logo-preview.webp)

## Overview

LIV

CONTRACT TEXTLES K

ILIV Contract is a growing interior brand creating commercially successful textile collections for the hospitality, leisure, care and specification markets.

We have an exciting opportunity for a Textiles Designer to join a creative and ambitious team within a fast-paced and evolving business.

Full time, Permenant Position, Preston, Lancashire.

Resposiblites:
- Design and develop commercial textile collections for the Contract market from concept through to sampling, production and launch.
- Research trends, colours, materials and market direction to identify new commercial opportunities within hospitality and contract interiors.
- Manage bespoke and special product developments through the Studio+ design process from initial brief through to sampling and production.

## Requirements

- 1-3 years' experience within the soft furnishings industry, ideally with exposure to contract, hospitality or specification markets.
- Proficient in AVA and Adobe Creative Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign.
- Proactive, collaborative and detail-oriented approach.
- Genuine passion for interior textiles, hospitality and commercial design.
- Experience within the weaving or textiles industry.
- Demonstrated experience of customer or sales interaction.

If you wish to apply for the role please send your CV along side a portfolio or examples of your work to EmilyArch@iliv.co.uk

Closing Date - 17th August 2026
