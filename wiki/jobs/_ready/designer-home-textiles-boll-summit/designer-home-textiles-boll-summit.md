## Full job description

Boll & Branch, the leading designer and retailer of luxury home textiles that are managed from the source, is seeking a talented and driven Designer to join our Product Design Team.

We are seeking a talented, brand-driven Designer, Home Textiles to join our small and highly collaborative Product Design team. You will contribute to translating our brand and product vision into designs for a wide variety of home products, primarily focused on bedding (sheeting, duvet covers, quilts, blankets, decorative pillows and throw blankets), as well as bath and rug categories. As a growing company, you will have meaningful visibility - including direct exposure to our SVP, Product Creation and our Founder & Chief Product Officer. As a brand that prides itself on creating the highest quality products rooted in sustainability, our ideal candidate has an extreme attention to detail, is highly organized, self-motivated, and has a genuine passion for home design and decor.

This position reports to Senior Designer, Textiles & Home Products.

What You'll Do:
- Create unique and original designs for wovens, embroideries, prints and knits that are consistent with our brand aesthetic and company values
- Learn and maintain an ongoing understanding of Boll & Branch creative direction, brand aesthetic and design language
- Research and track trends in fashion, home, nature and architecture to inform seasonal and product design direction
- Maintain an understanding of fabrications, construction, and techniques to execute designs that uphold our commitment to quality, functionality, sustainability, and integrity at a target price point
- Execute textile and product designs through both hand drawing/painting and digital tools, demonstrating strong surface design skills and the ability to create original artwork across bedding product categories
	- Create technically accurate repeats, alternative design layouts and scales for review and factory ready specs and surface designs
 - Ensure exceptional attention to detail across all work, with precision down to construction and weave-level execution where applicable
 - Partner with the Color team to create colorway options using our Pantone & Archroma library
- Render complete bed looks using Adobe Creative Suite and generative AI tools, such as Gemini, to support design presentations and internal reviews
- Support Senior Designers in developing design specs and artwork for prototypes and production
- Demonstrate strong responsiveness and openness to feedback, with the ability to collaborate and quickly iterate on work based on direction
- Communicate spec design details to Product Development (PD) partners and factory partners where needed
- Work collaboratively with cross-functional partners in PD, Color, Materials, Merchandising and Production on product execution, revisions and cost engineering without compromising quality and integrity of design
- Partner with PD on the status of styles in development to help ensure product execution meets design specifications
- Work proactively according to the development calendar to meet timelines and work through product approvals
- Assist in building both physical and digital design presentations for internal milestone meetings and external customer meetings; maintain and update design decks
- Continually expand knowledge of textiles, techniques, color and technical skill sets

What You Bring:
- Candidates must submit a portfolio demonstrating strong design skills in textiles and surface design
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Textile Design, Surface Pattern Design, Fashion Design or a related field
- 4+ years of experience in Textile Design in home furnishings or fashion/apparel
- Demonstrates a refined aesthetic sensibility and elevated taste level in home design and decor
- Strong hand drawing and computer-generated artwork skills; highly proficient in Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator for computer-generated surface and product designs
- Skilled in using AI tools to aid in generating renderings and design mock-ups
- Strong attention to detail, especially in relation to product design specifications, quality and brand aesthetics
- Effective communicator with the ability to articulate design concepts clearly to team members and cross-functional partners
- Curiosity and desire to continuously learn from market and customer insights
- Experience using Google Suite (Gmail, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Meet, etc.) and Microsoft Excel
- Experience in and understanding of product development and production process
- Comfortable in a small, nimble team where the pace is real and priorities can shift; able to manage multiple projects at once
- Willingness and enthusiasm to help with any product design related projects
- Exposure to or experience using a Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system preferred

This role is based in our Summit, NJ headquarters.
