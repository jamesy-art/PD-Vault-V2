## About the Role

Job description:

**Designer, Textiles and Graphics**

Mixing Textiles, Prints & Graphics design knowledge, with great fashion & luxury awareness, the Designer Textiles and Graphics is responsible for designing prints and visuals at a fast pace in line with Burberry's house codes and iconic heritage. The Designer Textiles and Graphics has great understanding of luxury industry, excellent creative ability in textile and print, high taste level, very wide technical skill set (illustration, graphic design, motion design) whilst also being highly organised, reliable and with the capacity to work in team and independently.

**Responsibilities**
- Research, design and develop new concepts for a collection
- Consistently design and produce high-quality, original prints and artworks in the creative with a very high level of refinement using a range of techniques & computer programs
- Research, cultivate and push concepts to their limits to further establish the visual identity of Burberry
- Ability to enter the customer's mindset to create ideas that work as products for Burberry
- Work collaboratively with cross functional teams including Runway MW / WW and Collections MW / WW
- Ability to complete briefs at an excellent pace
- Responsible for helping ensure that project deadlines are met and timeframes worked to
- Collaborate / Liaise with other teams

**Experience**
- BA or MA in Textile Design or Graphic Design or Illustration
- High-level of creative thinking and vision
- Strong knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator specifically)
- Strong illustration and drawing skills
- Knowledge of Woven and Jersey Textiles, techniques, and processes
- Luxury fashion experience is a plus
- High-level of creative thinking and visioon
