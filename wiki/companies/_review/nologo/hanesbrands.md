# Hanesbrands

American innerwear and activewear manufacturer; headquartered in Winston-Salem, North Carolina; parent of Hanes, Playtex, Champion (sold 2024), Bali, Wonderbra, Maidenform, and Bonds; acquired by Gildan for US$2.2 billion, December 2025.

## Overview

Hanesbrands Inc. is an American publicly listed apparel manufacturer specialising in innerwear (underwear, bras, hosiery) and activewear. The company was spun off from Sara Lee Corporation in September 2006 and headquartered in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. Hanesbrands employs approximately 65,300 people internationally (as of the Wikipedia source period) and generated revenue of approximately $4.33 billion in 2010. Key brands include Hanes (US mass market innerwear), Playtex, Bali, L'eggs, Just My Size, Barely There, Wonderbra, Maidenform (acquired $575M, 2013), Berlei, and Bonds (via Pacific Brands, acquired July 2016). The Champion activewear brand was sold to Authentic Brands Group for $1.2 billion (announced June 2024). On December 1, 2025, Hanesbrands was acquired by Gildan Activewear for approximately US$2.2 billion, following shareholder approval in November 2025.

## Company Profile

Hanesbrands was created as an independent entity when Sara Lee spun off its branded apparel division in September 2006. The company's growth strategy centred on brand acquisition — most significantly Maidenform ($575M, 2013) and Pacific Brands/Bonds (July 2016). The acquisition of Alternative Apparel ($60M, 2017) added an independent-label fashion basics brand. Hanesbrands operates approximately 220 direct retail stores in the United States. The sale of Champion to Authentic Brands Group (announced June 2024, $1.2B) represented a significant strategic de-merger, removing one of the company's most recognisable activewear assets. The Gildan acquisition (December 2025) effectively ends Hanesbrands' existence as an independent public company, folding its brand portfolio into Gildan's manufacturing-led group.

## Market Position

Hanesbrands occupies the mass-market innerwear segment in the United States — a category characterised by high volume, distribution through mass-market retailers (Walmart, Target), and brand loyalty in commoditised product categories (underwear, socks, t-shirts). The company's brands span a broad price and positioning range: Hanes (value), Maidenform and Playtex (mid-market bras), Wonderbra and Berlei (shaped/fashion innerwear), Bonds (Australia). International reach includes manufacturing in Asia, Central America, and the Caribbean. Collegiate licensing — partnerships with Duke, Florida State, Michigan, Auburn, Penn State, TCU, and 24+ other universities — provides a route into branded apparel beyond basics.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Hanesbrands' product categories have distinct surface pattern relevance. Innerwear (bras, underwear, hosiery) is a category where surface print, lace pattern, graphic print, and novelty textile design are commercially significant — particularly in the Maidenform, Playtex, Wonderbra, and Bali ranges, which compete on aesthetic differentiation as well as fit and function. Collegiate licensed apparel requires graphic design and brand identity work applied to garment surfaces. The Bonds brand (Australia) operates in a consumer market known for bold, playful print and pattern in casual apparel. Alternative Apparel addresses a more design-conscious basics segment. As a mass-market company distributing across major US retailers, Hanesbrands represents a high-volume channel through which surface pattern and print reach mainstream consumers.

## Design Direction

Design activity varies by brand: Maidenform and Wonderbra compete on fashion and fit, requiring print and pattern seasonal ranges; Bonds (Australia) is known for graphic and illustrative print design; Hanes itself operates in functional basics with minimal design differentiation. The Champion brand (sold 2024), historically relevant to streetwear and performance, maintained its own design identity before its sale to Authentic Brands Group.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Hanesbrands distributes primarily through US mass-market retailers and maintains approximately 220 own-brand retail stores. Collegiate apparel licensing (Duke, FSU, Michigan, Auburn, Penn State, TCU, and others) places Hanesbrands in the licensed sports apparel sector alongside companies such as Fanatics. The sale of Champion to Authentic Brands Group ($1.2B, June 2024) was a major licensing transaction — Authentic Brands Group operates a brand-licensing model, meaning Champion will continue as a brand under a licensing structure following the acquisition. The Gildan acquisition (December 2025, $2.2B) represents a wholesale trade sale; Hanesbrands' brands will continue under Gildan ownership.
