# Kohl's

American department store chain. Largest department store chain in the United States since 2012. Private brands generate approximately half of $19 billion in annual sales. Documented designer diffusion lines from Vera Wang, Narciso Rodriguez, Peter Som, and Dana Buchman. Home assortment expanded 40% in 2024.

## Overview

Kohl's Corporation is an American department store chain operating 1,174 locations in every US state except Hawaii. Founded as a grocery business by Polish immigrant Maxwell Kohl in Milwaukee, Wisconsin in 1927, with the first department store opening in Brookfield, Wisconsin in September 1962. The company went public in 1992 on the NYSE (KSS). Kohl's became the largest department store chain in the United States in May 2012, surpassing JCPenney. In 2017, Kohl's reported $19.095 billion in revenue, ranking 157th on the Fortune 500. The company is headquartered in Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin.

## Company Profile

Maxwell Kohl positioned the original department store concept between high-end department stores and discounters. British American Tobacco Company (via Batus Inc.) took a controlling interest in 1972; a management-led investor group repurchased the company in 1986. IPO: 19 May 1992. The chain expanded from 76 Midwest stores in 1992 to a national 49-state footprint during the 2000s, entering the New York metropolitan area in 2000, Dallas-Fort Worth and California in 2003, the Pacific Northwest in 2006, and the Southeast between 2005 and 2008.

Kohl's sold its credit card division to J.P. Morgan Chase in 2006 for $1.5 billion; switched processing to Capital One in 2011. Credit card and loyalty integration into mobile payment (Kohl's Pay) launched 2016. Amazon returns partnership began 2017, expanded nationwide 2019. Kohl's has the largest solar power programme of any retailer globally (Newsweek, 2009). CEO succession: Tom Kingsbury (2022–early 2025); Ashley Buchanan (less than five months, fired for unethical conduct); Michael Bender (interim, then permanent CEO from November 2025).

Sephora partnership announced 2021: Sephora stores-within-stores opened in select Kohl's locations (replacing Sephora's previous JCPenney relationship). Haus Labs by Lady Gaga entered the Sephora at Kohl's assortment in 2024. Babies R Us partnership (2024): baby supplies, furniture, Graco, Carter's, and Fisher-Price added to approximately 200 Kohl's stores. Home assortment expanded by 40% in March 2024, despite 12 consecutive quarters of declining home goods sales through February 2024.

## Market Position

Largest department store chain in the United States (by store count, from May 2012). 23rd-largest US retailer by revenue in 2019. Positioned between higher-end department stores and discounters — a deliberate strategy from the founding positioning. Private brands account for approximately half of $19 billion in annual revenue, representing a significant own-brand design operation at scale. Simon Property Group and Brookfield Asset Management (owners of JCPenney) made an unsolicited $8.6 billion offer for Kohl's in April 2022; this did not complete. Franchise Group also considered a bid in June 2022 before withdrawing.

## Products and Categories

Men's, women's, and children's apparel, footwear, accessories, cosmetics, and home goods. Home goods include apparel (Sonoma Goods for Life), bedding, and furnishings. Babies R Us baby supplies, furniture, and branded baby goods (Graco, Carter's, Fisher-Price) added from 2024. Sephora beauty stores-within-stores operational from 2021. Kohl's uses a "racetrack" layout with a single aisle circling the entire store floor.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Kohl's has two documented SPD-relevant programmes of significant commercial scale:

**Designer diffusion lines**: Kohl's has hosted exclusive diffusion lines from high-end fashion designers, creating a documented pipeline for design-driven apparel to reach a mass mid-market retail audience:
- **Vera Wang**: Exclusive diffusion line designed for Kohl's
- **Narciso Rodriguez**: Diffusion line exclusive to Kohl's
- **Peter Som**: Diffusion line exclusive to Kohl's
- **Dana Buchman**: Diffusion line for Kohl's

These arrangements place pattern, print, and design-led apparel — from designers associated with elevated fashion — within a high-volume middle-market retail context, generating meaningful unit volume for those design directions.

**Celebrity exclusive brands**: Kohl's has sold branded clothing exclusively through the chain from multiple celebrities including Jennifer Lopez, Lauren Conrad (swimwear documented), Daisy Fuentes, Britney Spears, Marc Anthony, Avril Lavigne, and Tony Hawk. These exclusive brand relationships generate design-led product ranges — including swimwear and apparel categories where print and pattern design are primary product attributes — at mass scale.

**Private brand programme (~50% of ~$19B in annual sales)**: The scale of Kohl's private brand design operation is significant:
- **Sonoma Goods for Life**: A brand spanning apparel and home goods valued at over $1 billion; relaunched 2016
- Croft & Barrow (acquired from JCPenney, originally founded 1972)
- Apt. 9, American Beauty, So, Urban Pipeline, Jumping Beans, Tek Gear — all in-house clothing brands

**Home goods expansion**: Kohl's 40% expansion of its home assortment in March 2024, alongside the Babies R Us partnership covering nursery and home categories, signals active investment in domestic surface design at retail scale.

**Exclusive retail rights**: Kohl's has purchased exclusive retail rights to existing brands including Candie's and Mudd Jeans — brands with their own design and print vocabularies, now exclusively distributed by Kohl's.

## Design Direction

No single design signature. Kohl's operates as a multi-brand platform, with its SPD-relevant design direction expressed through the private brand programme and the inward licensing of designer diffusion lines. The Vera Wang, Narciso Rodriguez, Peter Som, and Dana Buchman lines bring elevated design thinking to the mid-market. Celebrity brands (Lopez, Conrad, Fuentes) drive trend-responsive, often print-forward product ranges aimed at specific demographic audiences. The Sonoma Goods for Life brand (over $1 billion) is the most commercially significant single design identity in the Kohl's portfolio, spanning apparel and home.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Kohl's does not operate a licensing-out programme. Inbound licensing is the model: exclusive arrangements with designers (Vera Wang, Narciso Rodriguez) and celebrities generate exclusive branded product ranges for Kohl's stores and online. Brand acquisitions (Candie's, Mudd Jeans) provide exclusive retail rights. The Amazon returns partnership (nationwide 2019) and the Aldi and Planet Fitness space-lease arrangements reflect an active strategy of third-party integration into store format. Sephora partnership represents an inbound shop-in-shop beauty anchor at scale.
