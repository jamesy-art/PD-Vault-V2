# Barneys New York

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](127100690_174609181005824_2095693795463290135_n.jpg)

American luxury fashion brand founded 1923 by Barney Pressman in Manhattan; credited with introducing Giorgio Armani, Comme des Garçons, Azzedine Alaïa, and Christian Louboutin to the American market; filed for bankruptcy in 2019; IP acquired by Authentic Brands Group; currently operated as Barneys at Saks (suspended 2026 following Saks Global bankruptcy) and through five full-line Japanese stores; ABG exploring Madison Avenue revival.

## Overview

Barneys New York is an American luxury fashion brand that operated full-line department stores from 1923 until the closure of all physical US locations in 2020 following its 2019 Chapter 11 bankruptcy. Founded by Barney Pressman in a 500-square-foot space at Seventh Avenue and West 17th Street in Manhattan, the store began as a men's discount clothing retailer before being transformed under Pressman's son Fred into one of America's most prestigious luxury fashion retailers during the 1960s and 1970s. Barneys is widely credited with introducing Giorgio Armani to the American market — stocking the full Armani line from 1976 — and with bringing Comme des Garçons, Azzedine Alaïa, Christian Louboutin, and Zegna to American retail. The brand's intellectual property was acquired by Authentic Brands Group for $271.4 million in October 2019. As of 2026, ABG is exploring a revival of the Madison Avenue flagship and a small-format store in Naples, Florida.

## Company Profile

Fred Pressman's transformation of Barneys from a discount store to a luxury retailer was driven by his conviction that the future lay in quality over price. By 1973 the store stocked 60,000 suits and carried the full lines of Bill Blass, Pierre Cardin, Christian Dior, and Hubert de Givenchy. Women's clothing arrived in 1976; housewares, cosmetics, and gift departments in 1977. A $25 million women's store in Chelsea opened in 1986, the same year the Barneys CO-OP contemporary department was established for younger shoppers. The store abandoned its Seventh Avenue flagship in 1997 after opening a 230,000-square-foot Madison Avenue flagship on East 61st Street (designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox at a cost of $267 million) — the largest new store in New York City since the Great Depression. A joint holding company with Japanese department store Isetan (formed 1989) launched the first Tokyo Barneys in November 1990 and expanded the brand across Japan. The first Chapter 11 bankruptcy came in January 1996 following a dispute with Isetan over $250 million in real estate investment. Jones Apparel Group acquired the remaining Pressman family stake in 2004, then sold to Dubai-based Istithmar PJSC for $937.4 million in 2007. Perry Capital acquired a majority in 2012, reducing debt from $590 million to $50 million. Mark Lee (former CEO of Gucci Group) was appointed CEO in September 2010, overhauling the brand's staff, advertising, and website. Daniella Vitale (also a Gucci veteran) succeeded Lee as CEO in February 2017. The second Chapter 11 filing followed in August 2019; 15 of 22 stores closed immediately. Authentic Brands Group completed the purchase in October 2019 and shuttered all remaining US locations. In January 2021, Saks Fifth Avenue opened a 54,000-square-foot "Barneys at Saks" space on the fifth floor of its New York flagship, continuing Barneys' tradition of promoting emerging designers. Both US Barneys at Saks locations were closed in early 2026 following Saks Global's own bankruptcy.

## Market Position

Barneys New York defined a specific tier of American luxury retail — curated, fashion-forward, and trade-facing — distinct from the broad-market positioning of department stores like Bloomingdale's or Saks. Its curation role was commercially decisive: brands that Barneys chose to stock gained access to the American luxury market through the store's critical imprimatur. The Barneys CO-OP format (1986, spun off to a chain of 20+ standalone stores before the name was retired in 2013) extended the brand's curatorial reach to younger consumers. The brand's current licensed form (Barneys New York Beauty, Japan retail, potential US revival) represents a shift from retail curation to brand licensing consistent with the Authentic Brands Group ownership model.

## Products and Categories

At its peak, the full Barneys New York department store format carried: men's and women's ready-to-wear, footwear, accessories, jewellery, cosmetics, fragrances, housewares, and gifts. In-store dining through Freds restaurants (Beverly Hills, Chicago, Madison Avenue, Seventh Avenue, San Francisco). The CO-OP format carried contemporary, trendier product at lower price points. Current licensed products: Barneys New York Beauty (skincare, personal care, fragrance — four-product initial launch in 2022, licensed to South Korean Gloent Group). In Japan: five full-line stores and four outlet stores (as of 2026) plus beauty and fragrance counters in department stores and hotels.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Barneys New York's relevance to surface pattern design is primarily that of one of America's most significant retail curators of international fashion, with direct connections to the display and physical environment of designed goods:

- **Introduction of major designers to the American market**: Barneys' curation function from the 1970s onwards brought some of the most significant names in fashion surface design to American consumers. Comme des Garçons (Rei Kawakubo's avant-garde Japanese fashion, notable for deconstructed and conceptual surface treatments), Azzedine Alaïa (sculpture-fitted garments with textile surface as the primary expressive medium), Giorgio Armani (minimalist Italian tailoring in refined textiles and neutral colourways), and Christian Louboutin (footwear as surface design statement) all reached the American market partly through Barneys' commercial endorsement.
- **Simon Doonan window displays**: Simon Doonan, who served as Barneys' creative director from 1988 until around 2010, was famous for the store's window displays — widely discussed in the fashion press and considered a significant retail surface design and visual merchandising practice. Windows became a distinctive Barneys signature, treating the exterior display space as a graphic and narrative design surface.
- **Spring 2011 campaign — fine art photography**: Under Mark Lee's tenure, the Spring 2011 advertising campaign used photography by William Klein, Nan Goldin, and Juergen Teller shot behind-the-scenes at New York Fashion Week — a deliberate elevation of the brand's image-making toward fine art photography and away from commercial fashion advertising conventions.
- **Barneys New York Beauty (2022)**: The brand's post-bankruptcy extension into skincare and fragrance introduces a product category where packaging design, surface graphic identity, and formulation presentation are the primary design outputs. The four-product initial launch (cleanser, serum, day cream, night cream) positioned the brand in the mass-premium beauty market via the Gloent Group licence.
- **International House (1970 expansion)**: Fred Pressman's stated commitment to "complete collections of European designers, from denim pants to $250 suits" — not a "potpourri" of items — established Barneys as a platform for the full surface design context of each designer's vision rather than edited cherry-picks.

## Design Direction

Barneys New York had no proprietary design direction — the brand's intellectual output was its curation. The store's visual identity across decades was defined by its window display practice, the aesthetic consistency of its buying decisions, and the physical retail environment (particularly the $267 million Madison Avenue flagship with wood floors, marble mosaic lobby, gold-leaf ceilings, and lacquered walls). The transition from a physical retail institution to an IP-licensed brand entity — under Authentic Brands Group from 2019 — represents a fundamental shift in how the Barneys design identity is deployed.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Barneys New York's retail operations are now entirely licensed. In the US, Saks Fifth Avenue operated "Barneys at Saks" from January 2021 until early 2026; both US locations are now closed. Five full-line and four outlet stores operate in Japan under licence; Laox Holdings acquired the Japanese stake from Seven & I Holdings in April 2023 and plans to expand across Asia. A beauty and fragrance store operates in Seoul under the Gloent Group licence. Barneys became part of the Authentic Luxury Group (joint venture between ABG and Saks Global) in October 2024. A Barneys New York Residences boutique hotel (10 units) opened in Tulum, Mexico in late 2024. ABG is reportedly exploring a Madison Avenue flagship revival and a Naples, Florida store planned to open in 2027.
