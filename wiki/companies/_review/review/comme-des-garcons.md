# Comme des Garçons

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](452986269_1133202521087213_1524221058039252658_n.jpg)

Japanese avant-garde fashion label founded Tokyo 1969 by Rei Kawakubo; independently owned by Kawakubo; anti-fashion aesthetic using unconventional materials and asymmetric silhouettes; fragrance business operated by [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/puig]]; retail expanded through Dover Street Market concept stores worldwide.

## Overview

Comme des Garçons is a Japanese fashion label founded in Tokyo in 1969 by designer Rei Kawakubo, who remains its sole owner. Her husband Adrian Joffe serves as president. The company was formally established in Japan in 1973. It launched successfully in Japan through the 1970s with womenswear, added menswear in 1978, and arrived in Paris in 1981 — where its predominantly black palette and unconventional fabrication generated significant critical controversy. Since the 1980s, the label has expanded its colour range while maintaining its experimental reputation. Comme des Garçons operates more than ten product lines, designed by Kawakubo and by designers including Junya Watanabe, Fumito Ganryu, and Tao Kurihara.

## Company Profile

The first Rei Kawakubo boutique opened in 1975 — prior to the company's formal founding. By the early 1980s, Comme des Garçons was distributed through 100+ points of sale in Japan. The Paris boutique opened in 1982; SoHo, New York in 1983; San Francisco in 1984. By the early 1990s, over 300 global stockists. A second Paris boutique opened post-2000.

Dover Street Market (DSM), the brand's premium multi-brand concept store format, has expanded to: London Mayfair (2001), Tokyo Ginza (2006), New York Lexington Avenue, and Paris Le Marais (2024; 1,100m², Hôtel de Coulanges). DSM stores sell Comme des Garçons lines alongside other luxury and streetwear brands.

Collaborations: Converse (CdG Play, reedited multiple times), Nike (Air Force 1 x Supreme x CdG; Air Vapormax), Supreme, Fred Perry (scarf series).

## Market Position

Comme des Garçons occupies the avant-garde independent luxury tier — outside the major conglomerate structures (LVMH, Kering, Richemont). Its consistent experimental positioning, unconventional material use, and institutional museum presence (Centre Georges-Pompidou 1986; Shinjuku graphics exhibition 2005; White Drama at Cité de la Mode et du Design 2012; MET 2017) have established it as a critical reference in global fashion alongside its commercial retail operation.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Comme des Garçons is not primarily a surface pattern design brand — its SPD relevance operates through:

- **Anti-fashion material surface**: The label's 1980s use of raw, deconstructed, asymmetric fabric treatments — with visible seams, irregular surfaces, and unconventional materials — constitutes an influential counter-tradition within fashion surface design.
- **Graphic identity as surface**: The Comme des Garçons Play heart logo (by Filip Pagowski), applied to Converse and branded garments, is one of the most widely recognised brand graphic marks in contemporary streetwear — a pure surface design object.
- **Fragrance packaging surface design**: The CdG fragrance range, developed with Christian Astuguevieille and produced by [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/puig]], carries distinctive conceptual packaging surface identities (Odeur 53, Odeur 71, Red series, Incense series) that are collectible objects in packaging design.
- **Dover Street Market retail environment**: DSM stores are regularly cited as examples of high-level retail environment design — curated spaces where architecture, display, and graphic identity are treated as a total surface design proposition.

## Design Direction

Rei Kawakubo's design direction has never followed conventional seasonal trend logic. Core strategies: deconstruction, asymmetry, non-standard materials, deliberate anti-conventionalism. The 1981 Paris debut was described as a revolution in dress silhouette and materiality. Multiple lines allow the brand to operate across price points without diluting the flagship's avant-garde position.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Comme des Garçons is independently owned by Rei Kawakubo. Fragrance operations managed by [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/puig]] (Spanish beauty and fashion group). Retail: flagship boutiques in Tokyo, Paris, New York, London, and other major cities; concept stores via Dover Street Market format. No parent company acquisition or conglomerate ownership structure documented.
