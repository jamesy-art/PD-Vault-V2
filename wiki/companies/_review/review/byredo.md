# Byredo

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/byredo/profile/company-logo.svg)

Swedish luxury fragrance, leather goods, and accessories brand; founded Stockholm 2006 by Ben Gorham; majority acquired by [[wiki/companies/_staging/puig]] (Spain) in May 2022; Gorham continues as Chief Creative Officer.

## Overview

Byredo is a Swedish luxury brand founded in Stockholm in 2006 by Ben Gorham. The name is a portmanteau of "by redolence." The brand launched with fragrance as its primary product category and has expanded into leather goods, accessories, clothing, jewellery, beauty, and eyewear. In 2013, Gorham sold a majority stake to Manzanita Capital (London), a firm that also invested in Diptyque, Malin + Goetz, and Space NK. In May 2022, Spanish conglomerate [[wiki/companies/_staging/puig]] acquired a majority stake; Gorham continues as shareholder and Chief Creative Officer.

## Company Profile

Byredo opened its first store in Stockholm, expanded to New York SoHo in 2015, and debuted its first handbags and accessories collection at the 2017 Paris Fashion Week. Fine jewellery was launched as a permanent category in November 2023. The brand has a documented track record of cross-category and cross-discipline collaborations: perfumer Jérôme Epinette (2016), photographers Inez and Vinoodh (2016), designer Virgil Abloh (2018, "Elevator Music" with Off-White), furniture company IKEA (2020, candle collection), makeup artist Isamaya Ffrench (2020), and Travis Scott (2021). The [[wiki/companies/_staging/puig]] acquisition placed Byredo within a portfolio that includes Carolina Herrera, Charlotte Tilbury, Rabanne, and Jean Paul Gaultier.

## Market Position

Byredo occupies the niche luxury segment: premium pricing, restrained Nordic aesthetic, and multi-category extension anchored in fragrance. The brand competes in the elevated lifestyle and fragrance space alongside Diptyque, Le Labo, and Malin + Goetz. Distribution is intentionally selective.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Byredo's surface design expression concentrates in packaging and product object surfaces. Fragrances, leather goods, and makeup products carry a consistently minimal, graphic identity applied across bottle, box, and case surfaces. The IKEA candle collaboration (2020) represents the brand's most direct application of surface/object design at accessible scale. The Virgil Abloh / Off-White collaboration (2018, "Elevator Music") demonstrates cross-brand surface identity work. The Isamaya Ffrench makeup launch (2020) applied a colourful graphic surface treatment to cosmetics packaging.

The 2017 Paris Fashion Week accessories debut and November 2023 fine jewellery launch represent surface-oriented category extensions across leather and metal.

## Design Direction

The brand's aesthetic is minimal and Nordic in sensibility. Packaging is the primary surface design vehicle: clean typography, restrained colour palette, and consistent material treatment across all product categories. Cross-category collaborations introduce surface graphic variation (IKEA, Abloh) without abandoning the core minimal identity.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Byredo distributes through its own boutiques and select premium retail partners. [[wiki/companies/_staging/puig]] holds the majority stake, providing international distribution infrastructure. Established store locations include Stockholm and New York SoHo. Fine jewellery (from 2023) marks the broadest category extension to date.
