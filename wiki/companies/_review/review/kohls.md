# Kohl's

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](609828753_18556393024018576_9080608714606588889_n.jpg)

American mid-market department store chain; founded 1927 (grocery) / 1962 (department store) by Maxwell Kohl, Milwaukee, Wisconsin; HQ Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin; 1,174 stores in 49 US states; largest US department store chain by store count (surpassed JCPenney, May 2012); private label brands generate ~50% of ~$19 billion annual revenue; NYSE: KSS; CEO: Michael Bender (since November 2025).

## Overview

Kohl's was founded by Maxwell Kohl, a Polish immigrant who opened a corner grocery store in Milwaukee, Wisconsin in 1927. In September 1962, after building Kohl's Food Stores into the largest supermarket chain in the Milwaukee area, he opened his first department store in Brookfield, Wisconsin — positioned between higher-end department stores and discounters. British American Tobacco Company's retail division (Batus Inc.) acquired a controlling interest in 1972; the firm then expanded from 10 to 39 stores across Wisconsin, Illinois, and Indiana. A senior management group purchased the company in 1986, added 27 stores in two years, then acquired 26 MainStreet locations (Chicago suburban and Twin Cities) in 1988. Kohl's completed its NYSE IPO (symbol KSS) on May 19, 1992. During the 2000s, the chain expanded from 76 Midwest stores (1992) to a 49-state presence. Kohl's became the largest US department store chain by store count in May 2012, surpassing JCPenney. In 2017, Kohl's partnered with Amazon for in-store returns (expanded nationwide 2019). The company opened Sephora shop-in-shops from 2021. A 2024 deal with Babies R Us added baby supplies across ~200 stores. CEO succession: Michelle Gass → Tom Kingsbury (2022–early 2025) → Ashley Buchanan (fired May 2025 for unethical conduct after less than five months) → Michael Bender (interim then permanent, November 2025). ## Company Profile

Kohl's Corporation, HQ Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin. NYSE: KSS. 1,174 stores across 49 US states (all except Hawaii). ~$19 billion annual revenue (2017 data). Private brands: ~50% of total revenue. Website: kohls.com. About, careers, press, and investor relations accessible via corporate.kohls.com. Loyalty: Kohl's Cash reward programme; Kohl's Pay (mobile payment, from 2016). Credit card: formerly Chase (sold 2006, $1.5B); Capital One from 2011. Store layout: "racetrack" — single aisle circling the entire store perimeter, adapted from discount retail.

## Market Position

Kohl's occupies the mid-market/value-oriented department store segment — below Nordstrom and Macy's, at par with JCPenney and Belk. The chain differentiated through private brand depth (50% of revenue), accessible designer diffusion lines, and celebrity brand collaborations. Its Amazon returns partnership (from 2017) and Sephora shop-in-shops (from 2021) represent tactical diversification to drive store traffic in the face of e-commerce pressure. A 2022 attempted buyout (considered by Hudson's Bay, Sycamore Partners, Simon Property Group/Brookfield, and Franchise Group) did not complete. The chain has faced sustained same-store sales challenges and CEO instability in the 2023–2025 period.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Kohl's operates at a scale where seasonal surface design is a core operational function across hundreds of private label SKUs:

- **Private brand print and pattern volume**: Sonoma Goods for Life, Apt. 9, Croft & Barrow, and Urban Pipeline each release seasonal collections across apparel and home goods — requiring continuous print, pattern, and colour direction for jersey, woven, knit, and home textiles. At ~50% of $19B revenue, this represents approximately $9.5 billion in annual private label product that carries designed surface content.
- **Home goods surface design**: Sonoma Goods for Life covers home goods alongside apparel — bedding, bath, curtains, and tableware require print and pattern design; the 2024 home assortment expansion (40% enlargement) signals increased home surface design demand.
- **Designer diffusion lines**: Vera Wang at Kohl's (from 2006) and Narciso Rodriguez diffusion lines brought fashion-led print and surface aesthetics from the runway to the mass market — a documented model of high-end SPD trickle-down via exclusive mid-market licensing.
- **Celebrity brand print design**: Lauren Conrad's Kohl's collection has included seasonal swimwear prints; Jennifer Lopez, Britney Spears, and other celebrity brands have included seasonal graphic and print fashion.
- **Sephora partnership**: The Sephora shop-in-shop introduces premium beauty brand packaging and graphic design into the Kohl's retail environment — an indirect surface design connection through beauty brand identity.

## Design Direction

No in-house runway or fashion design function. Private brand design is managed through an internal product development and sourcing infrastructure. Designer diffusion and celebrity brand lines bring external creative direction at mass price points. The Sonoma Goods for Life brand relaunch (2016) signalled investment in private brand design coherence.
