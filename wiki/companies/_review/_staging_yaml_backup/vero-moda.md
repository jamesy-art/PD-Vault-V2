## Overview

Vero Moda is a Danish women's fashion label founded in 1987 as the first clothing brand of the [[bestseller]] Group. Bestseller — the Danish fashion group founded by Troels Holch Povlsen — expanded into multi-label territory with Vero Moda as its original vehicle; the group has since grown to include [[Jack & Jones]], ONLY, Selected Femme, and [[wiki/companies/_review/_staging_yaml_backup/topshop]] (75% stake from September 2024). Vero Moda describes its consumer positioning as targeting lifestyle rather than age demographic, positioning attainable fashion as its core offer: "transforming everyday wear from ordinary to modern and elevated essentials." ## Market Position

Vero Moda positions in the mid-market women's high street segment — comparable to H&M and Mango — as an attainable fashion label for contemporary European women across age groups. As Bestseller's first and one of its most widely distributed labels, Vero Moda has significant retail presence across Europe and Scandinavia. Bestseller's group model — multi-label, wholesale-led, mass distribution — gives Vero Moda broad department store, multibrand, and own-store presence across multiple markets. The label's "lifestyle not age" positioning gives it flexibility across a wide consumer demographic.
