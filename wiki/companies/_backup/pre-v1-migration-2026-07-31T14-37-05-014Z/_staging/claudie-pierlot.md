# Claudie Pierlot

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/claudie-pierlot/profile/company-logo.svg)

French women's fashion brand founded by designer Claude Anne Marie Pierlot (1947–2009); established in Paris in the 1980s; known for Parisian feminine aesthetic; [[SMCP Group]] brand since 2009.

## Overview

Claudie Pierlot is a French women's ready-to-wear brand created by designer Claude Anne Marie Pierlot, born 1947 in Troyes — a historically significant hosiery and knitwear manufacturing centre. Pierlot trained in fashion design and pattern making before working in knitwear factories in southern France, then as an assistant at Dorothée Bis. She subsequently worked at the Printemps design studio and the Mafia agency before joining the René Derhy brand in 1974. After ten years as a Derhy designer, she launched her own label with support from Yvette and René Derhy. Pierlot died on 25 February 2009 in Nanterre, having sold the brand to the SMCP group weeks earlier. The brand continues to operate as part of [[SMCP Group]] alongside Sandro and Maje.

## Company Profile

Claudie Pierlot's first collections were produced in the Derhy premises at 6 rue du Faubourg-Poissonnière, Paris. In the mid-1990s the company moved to rue du Sentier. Three Parisian boutiques were established: rue Montmartre, rue du Vingt-Neuf-Juillet, and the historic flagship at 23 rue du Vieux-Colombier in Saint-Germain-des-Prés. The brand became known for "dressing the little women of Paris" (WWD, 1992) and defines its identity as "Parisian, feminine and urban."

In early 2009, knowing she was seriously ill, Pierlot sold the brand to the shareholders of Sandro and Maje, who formed the SMCP group for the acquisition. From September 2010 to 2013, L Capital (LVMH-linked, 41%) and Florac (Louis-Dreyfus group) held majority stakes in SMCP. Model Audrey Marnay became the brand's advertising muse in 2010 and designed a capsule collection, perpetuating the "La Parisienne" identity sought by the founder.

## Market Position

Claudie Pierlot operates in the Parisian accessible luxury / contemporary womenswear segment — above high street, below full luxury price points. Within [[SMCP Group]], it is positioned alongside Sandro (premium contemporary) and Maje (feminine-forward) with a distinctly Parisian-chic feminine identity. The brand operates internationally via its own stores and e-commerce under SMCP's global retail structure.
