# Tendam

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](../../assets/companies/tendam/profile/tendam_logo.jpeg)

Spanish multi-brand fashion retailer and holding company; formerly The Cortefiel Group, renamed Tendam 2018; founded 1880 Madrid as family haberdashery; HQ Madrid; present in 79 countries with 1,462 directly operated stores and 594 franchises (2,056 total points of sale); brands: Women'secret, Springfield, Cortefiel, Pedro del Hierro, Dash and Stars, Hi&Bye, Hoss Intropia, OOTO, SlowLove, High Spirits; Fifty factory outlet chain.

## Overview

Tendam (formerly Grupo Cortefiel) is a Spanish fashion retail holding company headquartered in Madrid, operating a portfolio of multi-tier fashion brands across Europe and Latin America. The company traces its origins to an 1880 family haberdashery on Calle Romanones in Madrid; it transitioned into textile production and distribution, opened the La Palma shirt factory in 1933, and launched its first men's tailoring label — Cortefiel — in 1946. The company became a holding structure in 1954. Subsequent decades saw the creation of the Springfield casual brand (1988), acquisition of Pedro del Hierro (1989), and launch of the Women'secret underwear and swimwear brand (1993). International expansion accelerated from 1993, with the most rapid period of growth between 2000 and 2003, when sales surface area increased by over 70%. In 2005, MEP Retail acquired a stake, bringing a change in ownership and management. The group was renamed Tendam in 2018. As of 2023, its two largest brands — Women'secret (753 stores) and Springfield (685 stores) — operate across 67 and 86 countries respectively. Each brand maintains its own design team while sharing centralised admin, finance, technology, and sourcing functions from Madrid.

## Market Position

Tendam operates across the full span of Spanish mid-market fashion, from accessible youth casualwear (Springfield) and intimate apparel (Women'secret) through to premium adult fashion (Pedro del Hierro) and occasion tailoring (Cortefiel, positioned at ages 35–45). The group's largest physical brand by store count is Women'secret (753 stores, 67 countries), followed by Springfield (685 stores). Spain accounts for approximately 53% of its total store network, with Portugal at 11% and Mexico at 3.5%. International presence spans five continents: Europe, North and South America, Asia, and Africa. The group maintains international buying offices in Spain, Hong Kong, and India, with distribution centralised in Madrid backed by a secondary centre in Hong Kong. The franchise model underpins its international expansion outside Spain; proprietary management is used in markets where the group maintains direct operational control.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Tendam's portfolio spans multiple brands with significant surface pattern design relevance, concentrated most intensively in Women'secret and the casualwear labels.

**Women'secret as seasonal swimwear and lingerie print buyer** — Women'secret is Tendam's largest brand by store count (753 stores, 67 countries) and operates in the swimwear and intimate apparel categories — both of which are heavily print- and surface-design-driven. Seasonal swimwear collections require print design across a broad range of fabrications and silhouettes; lingerie ranges require lace pattern, fabric embellishment, and colourway direction at commercial scale. At 753 stores across 67 countries, Women'secret represents a significant commercial buyer of swimwear and lingerie surface design.

**Springfield casual and print** — as a casual urban fashion brand for young men and women, Springfield's seasonal range will include printed tees, pattern fabric shirts and blouses, graphic knitwear, and printed casualwear — the core commercial product categories of the mass-market casual print buyer. The brand's "young, urban cosmopolitan" target market is exactly the demographic where graphic print and pattern differentiation is most commercially impactful.

**Hoss Intropia** — the acquired Hoss/Intropia brand history (described as a premium women's fashion label with its own "personality and identity") suggests a more design-led print direction than Springfield or Cortefiel. Hoss Intropia has historically been known in the Spanish market for print-forward women's fashion, but this characterisation is based on general market knowledge rather than the Wikipedia source alone.

**Hi&Bye intimate and casual** — the brand's description specifically references lace and cotton underwear sets and vibrant colour palettes (lilac, pink, blue), indicating that colourway and fabric surface decisions (lace pattern, ribbed texture) are central to its product identity for the 14–18 demographic.

**Dash and Stars activewear** — activewear is an increasingly surface-design-active category, particularly for women, where graphic print, colour-blocking, and all-over pattern design are primary commercial differentiators.

**La Palma / shirt heritage** — the 1933 La Palma shirt factory represents a historical textile production base; shirts remain a surface-design-intensive category (stripe, check, print, dobby, jacquard) within Cortefiel and Springfield's menswear ranges.

## Design Direction

Each Tendam brand maintains its own independent design team; these are not named in the source. Centralised functions (admin, finance, technology, expansion, sourcing, HR) are shared from the Madrid head office. The brand architecture spans a wide creative register — from Cortefiel's conservative adult tailoring through Springfield's urban casual to Women'secret's intimate apparel and Hi&Bye's teen-focused vibrant colour palette. No group-level creative director is documented.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Tendam operates through two commercial models: proprietary management (direct store operation with integrated business management) and franchise (managed through specialists with local market knowledge and financial capacity). The franchise model drives international expansion outside Spain and Portugal. As of 2023, the group's physical presence is weighted toward the Iberian Peninsula — Spain (53%) and Portugal (11%) account for nearly two-thirds of directly operated stores. Latin America is the most significant international market: Mexico (67 stores across Women'secret and Springfield), Ecuador (30 stores), Paraguay, and Venezuela. The Fifty outlet format carries multi-brand overstock including third-party brands (Vero Moda, Levi's, Jack and Jones, Dockers), indicating a clearance retail function extending beyond the group's own brands. Online channels are active for Women'secret (since 2000), SPF.com (since 2010), and all major brands including Cortefiel, Pedro del Hierro, and SlowLove.
