# Intersport

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/intersport/profile/company-logo.svg)

International sporting goods retail cooperative; founded 1968 in Bern, Switzerland; one of the largest sporting goods retailers globally; 5,260 specialist stores in 40 countries; HQ Bern; purchasing and brand management entity: INTERSPORT International Corporation (IIC); private label brands include McKinley (outdoor), PRO TOUCH, FIREFLY, NAKAMURA, energetics.

## Overview

INTERSPORT International Corporation (IIC) was founded in 1968 in Bern, Switzerland, through the alliance of ten national sporting goods purchasing centres — Belgium, Denmark, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Austria, Sweden, and Switzerland — forming a cooperative international purchasing organisation. The model gives member retailers collective buying power and access to shared private-label brands while operating independently at a national and store level. During the 1980s, the group expanded beyond collective purchasing into private-label brand development; McKinley was launched in 1984 and became the flagship outdoor private-label brand. Intersport has been the official sports retailer at multiple Olympic Games and sponsors major sporting events globally. In January 2026, INTERSPORT Austria acquired the INTERSPORT Slovenia Group, bringing 500+ stores across twelve countries under INTERSPORT Austria GmbH. Tom Foley was appointed CEO of IIC in 2025. ## Market Position

One of the largest sporting goods retailers globally by store count. The cooperative model differentiates Intersport from vertically integrated sporting goods retailers: member stores retain local identity and management while benefiting from shared purchasing, PLB access, and joint marketing. Competes with Decathlon (vertically integrated, own-brand dominant) and JD Sports (multi-brand, branded product focus) — Intersport's model is closer to a retail franchise network with collective purchasing. Olympic sponsorship history and event partnerships underpin the brand's sports performance credentials.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Intersport's SPD relevance is primarily through its private label brands:

- **Private label sportswear design**: McKinley, PRO TOUCH, FIREFLY, NAKAMURA, and energetics each require branded colour systems, logo design, surface pattern for active apparel (sublimation printing, colour-blocking, performance textile graphics), and packaging design — commissioned annually across product ranges.
- **Technical sportswear surface vocabulary**: Sporting goods apparel uses engineered surface design extensively — moisture management zoning indicated by colour or texture, reflective stripe placement, compression panel mapping, sublimated all-over graphic prints in performance garments.
- **Olympic outfitter heritage**: As official sports retailer at multiple Olympic Games, Intersport has produced uniform-adjacent branded merchandise — a category where pattern, colour, and national graphic identity are primary design deliverables.
- **Brand identity across 5,260 stores**: Consistent visual merchandising and in-store graphics at this store count creates significant demand for repeatable surface and print design systems.

## Design Direction

Private label brands are developed by IIC's product and design teams to deliver accessible performance sportswear across each category. McKinley's 40-year history (1984–2024) makes it the group's most established own-brand identity — outdoor apparel and equipment. The PLB strategy targets everyday sporting needs at accessible price points, not premium performance or fashion-forward aesthetics.
