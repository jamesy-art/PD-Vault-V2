# Weyco Group

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](weyco_group_inc_logo.jpeg)

American footwear holding company; formerly Weyenberg Shoe Manufacturing Company; designs, markets and distributes Florsheim, Nunn Bush, Stacy Adams, BOGS, Rafters, and Umi; focused on North American wholesale and retail; led by Thomas Florsheim Jr. (great-grandson of Florsheim Shoe founder Milton S. Florsheim).

## Overview

Weyco Group (formerly Weyenberg Shoe Manufacturing Company) is an American footwear company that designs, markets, and distributes a portfolio of brands across mid-price and performance categories. The current portfolio includes Florsheim (classic American dress and casual shoes), Nunn Bush (mid-priced men's leather footwear), Stacy Adams (decorative dress and fashion footwear), BOGS (waterproof outdoor boots and sandals), Rafters (water and outdoor footwear), and Umi (children's footwear, acquired 2010).

The company's modern formation began in 1964 when Thomas Florsheim Sr. invested US$750,000 in the Weyenberg Shoe Manufacturing Company after leaving the Florsheim Shoe Company. Weyco subsequently acquired Nunn Bush and Stacy Adams, and in 2002 acquired Florsheim brand assets when the original Florsheim Group filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. BOGS and Rafters were added via the Combs Company acquisition in 2011. The company is led by Thomas Florsheim Jr. (great-grandson of Florsheim Shoe founder Milton S. Florsheim) and operates two business segments in North America: wholesale and retail. 2014 net sales: US$320.5M.

## Market Position

Weyco Group operates in the North American mid-priced footwear market, with wholesale as the dominant revenue channel (US$243.4M vs US$23.3M retail in 2014). Wholesale customers include footwear, department, and specialty stores primarily in the United States and Canada. The portfolio spans mid-priced leather dress shoes (Florsheim, Nunn Bush, Stacy Adams), casual leather and synthetic footwear, and outdoor/performance boots and sandals (BOGS, Rafters). BOGS has established distribution in the US outdoor and waterproof footwear category.

## Products and Categories

**Florsheim** — classic American men's dress shoes; broguing, wingtip, cap toe, and leather tooling traditions; heritage brand with roots from 1892 (original Milton S. Florsheim company); Weyco acquired brand assets from bankruptcy 2002

**Nunn Bush** — mid-priced men's dress and casual leather footwear

**Stacy Adams** — decorative men's dress and fashion footwear; known for highly embellished styles including two-tone colour, brogue patterning, embossed and printed leather, and woven textile uppers

**BOGS** (acquired 2011 via The Combs Company) — waterproof outdoor boots, shoes, and sandals in rubber and neoprene; seasonal print and pattern programmes on outer surfaces

**Rafters** (acquired 2011 via The Combs Company) — water and outdoor sandals and casual footwear

**Umi** (acquired 2010) — children's footwear

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Weyco Group's surface pattern design relevance is concentrated in its decorative footwear brands — particularly Stacy Adams — and in the printed and patterned outer surfaces of BOGS outdoor footwear.

**Stacy Adams** is a brand with strong surface design identity, known for highly embellished dress footwear: two-tone colour blocking, decorative broguing (punched hole patterns in leather), embossed reptile and exotic leather prints, woven textile uppers, and ornamental cap-toe stitching. These decorative surface treatments are core commercial differentiators in the men's fashion and occasion footwear segment. Stacy Adams operates in a segment where surface design — pattern, texture, print, and ornamentation — is the primary product story.

**BOGS** rubber and neoprene outsoles and uppers are a commercial category in which seasonal print and pattern applied to waterproof surfaces is a standard product design tool. Outdoor boot brands routinely deploy floral, abstract, and geometric prints on neoprene boot shafts and rubber uppers.

**Florsheim** maintains a tradition of classic American dress shoe construction in which broguing (decorative perforation), wingtip patterning, and leather tooling are established surface design elements.

## Design Direction

No group-level creative director or named design team is identified in the source. Brand design is managed at the individual brand level within Weyco. Stacy Adams' decorative design tradition predates Weyco's ownership (the brand was founded in 1898 in Brockton, Massachusetts).

## Retail and Licensing Context

Weyco Group operates through two channels: North American wholesale (US$243.4M in 2014) and retail (16 company-owned US stores plus internet, US$23.3M in 2014). Wholesale customers are footwear, department, and specialty stores in the US and Canada. No direct-to-consumer licensing activity beyond owned retail is documented in the source. The Florsheim brand has historical international distribution (Brass Boot line in Europe), though the source focuses on North American wholesale and retail.
