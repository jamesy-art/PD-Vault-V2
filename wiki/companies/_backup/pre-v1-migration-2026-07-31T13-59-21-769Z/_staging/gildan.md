# Gildan

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/gildan/profile/company-logo.png)

Canadian manufacturer of undecorated blank activewear — primarily t-shirts, hoodies, and fleeces — supplied to screen printers and decorators; also a major producer of branded and private label socks for US retail.

## Overview

Gildan Activewear Inc. is a Canadian publicly listed manufacturer of blank activewear and branded hosiery. Founded in 1984 by brothers Glenn and Greg Chamandy with the acquisition of a knitting mill in Montreal, the company built its core business supplying undecorated cotton t-shirts to US screen printers and wholesalers. Gildan owns manufacturing facilities in Honduras and the Caribbean and has approximately 50,000 employees worldwide. It is listed on both the Toronto Stock Exchange and NYSE American. In August 2025, Gildan announced the acquisition of Hanesbrands (HanesBrands Inc.) for US$2.2 billion in cash and stock, a deal approved by Hanesbrands shareholders in November 2025 and expected to close in late 2025 or early 2026.

## Company Profile

Gildan's business model is built around vertical integration and low-cost, high-volume manufacturing. The company operates its own knitting, bleaching, dyeing, finishing, cutting, and sewing facilities — principally in Honduras (Rio Nance complex) and the Caribbean. By 2001, Gildan was the leading distributor of 100% cotton t-shirts in the United States. The company has grown through acquisitions: Anvil Holdings (sustainable/organic apparel, 2012); Doris Hosiery (CA$110M, 2014); Comfort Colors (pigment-dyed garments, $100M, 2015); PEDS Legwear ($55M, 2016); American Apparel (brand and IP, $88M, 2017). Vince Tyra was appointed President and CEO in February 2024 following the departure of co-founder Glenn Chamandy in December 2023. The pending Hanesbrands acquisition would bring HanesBrands, Hanes, Maidenform, and Champion brands into the Gildan portfolio.

## Market Position

Gildan is the dominant supplier in the blank activewear market for the US screen printing and promotional products industry. The company's strategy of manufacturing in low-cost locations (Honduras, Caribbean) allowed it to undercut Chinese manufacturers on price by 2006, cementing its position in the US wholesale blank garment market. In the socks category, Gildan supplies branded product under Gold Toe, PowerSox, SilverToe, Auro, All Pro, American Apparel, and the Gildan brand, and manufactures Under Armour and New Balance brand socks. Comfort Colors, acquired in 2015, addresses the higher-margin garment-dyed blank market, popular with independent apparel decorators and streetwear labels.

## Products and Categories

- **Blank activewear** — undecorated t-shirts (100% cotton and blends), hoodies, fleeces, polos; primary wholesale product for screen printers
- **Comfort Colors** — garment-dyed and pigment-dyed blanks; premium blank segment
- **American Apparel blanks** — fashion-forward cut-and-sew blanks under the acquired brand
- **Socks** — Gold Toe, PowerSox, SilverToe, Auro, All Pro (retail brands); Under Armour and New Balance socks (manufactured under licence)
- **Anvil** — sustainable, recycled, and organic apparel blanks (acquired 2012)
- **HanesBrands** (pending acquisition, 2025) — underwear, t-shirts, hosiery, activewear; includes Champion brand

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Gildan occupies a foundational position in the surface pattern and print supply chain: its blank garments are the physical surface onto which designs, graphics, and patterns are applied by screen printers, embroiderers, digital decorators, and apparel decorating companies globally. Gildan t-shirts are one of the most widely used blank canvases for applied surface pattern in the mass market. The screen printing industry — which decorates Gildan blanks — is one of the primary commercial channels through which surface pattern designers' work reaches product. Comfort Colors' garment-dyed aesthetic (irregular, washed surface with tonal variation) represents a specific surface treatment language that itself functions as a design direction, influencing streetwear and indie apparel aesthetics. The acquisition of American Apparel brought a fashion-adjacent blank product line that attracts more design-conscious decorators.

## Design Direction

Gildan is a manufacturing and supply company rather than a design-led fashion brand. The company's surface design decisions relate primarily to garment construction (fabric weight, dye method, cut) and colourway selection across its blank SKU ranges. Comfort Colors' seasonal colour palettes are among the most referenced colour systems in the US independent apparel decorator market. No named creative director is documented.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Gildan's primary sales channel is wholesale to screen printers, distributors, and promotional products companies. Retail sales of branded socks (Gold Toe, Under Armour, New Balance) go through US mass market and department store channels. American Apparel (brand, not manufacturing) is sold through the gildan.com/americanapparel channel. The pending Hanesbrands acquisition would significantly expand Gildan's direct-to-retail presence and brand portfolio.
