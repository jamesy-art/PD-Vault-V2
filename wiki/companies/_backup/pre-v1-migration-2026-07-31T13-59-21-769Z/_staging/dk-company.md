# DK Company

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](../../assets/companies/dk-company/profile/company-logo.svg)

One of Europe's leading multi-brand fashion suppliers, operating 26 brands across women's, men's, and contemporary markets.

## Overview

DK Company is a Danish fashion group headquartered in Ikast, with additional divisions in Vejle and Copenhagen. Founded in 2001 by Jens Poulsen, the group has grown to approximately 2,400 employees and 450 retail stores. Its portfolio covers 26 distinct fashion brands spanning women's contemporary, menswear, casual, and premium lifestyle markets. The company also maintains its own Jasmin brand and produces private label garments for external fashion labels.

## Company Profile

DK Company built its current scale through a series of strategic acquisitions. In 2012, it acquired three divisions from BTX Group, including the Young division (Blend, ICHI, Gestuz) and the Modern division (Fransa, b.young, Dranella). In 2014, it acquired four brands from [[IC Group]]: InWear, Matinique, Part Two, and Soaked in Luxury. A further acquisition in 2019 added Saint Tropez, Solid, and Tailored & Originals, with each brand integrated into the relevant divisional office based on market positioning.

## Market Position

DK Company operates as a major European fashion wholesale and retail group. Its 450 retail stores and 26-brand portfolio place it among the leading fashion suppliers in the Nordic and broader European market. The group addresses a wide commercial range — from accessible casualwear (Blend, Casual Friday) to premium contemporary womenswear (Gestuz, Soaked in Luxury) — giving it strong shelf presence across multiple retail tiers.

## Products and Categories

The brand portfolio covers:

- **Women's contemporary:** ICHI, Fransa, b.young, By Simonsen, Part Two, Soaked in Luxury, Saint Tropez, Culture, Cream, KAFFE (including KAFFE Curve), My Essential Wardrobe, Sorbet, Lounge Nine, Atelier Rêve
- **Men's:** Blend, Matinique, Casual Friday, FQ 1924, Solid, Tailored Originals, InWear (also womenswear)
- **Denim/casual:** Pulz Jeans, The Jogg Concept
- **Private label:** Jasmin (own brand) + private label production for external clients

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Running 26 distinct brand identities requires seasonal pattern and print decisions across multiple concurrent design teams. Brands such as ICHI, Fransa, KAFFE, and Saint Tropez operate in segments where print and colour are primary seasonal differentiation tools. The women's contemporary category is consistently print-led, and DK Company's scale of production (450 stores, 2,400 employees) means pattern decisions are made at significant commercial volume across the European market.

## Design Direction

Each brand within the group maintains its own creative identity and target customer, managed through divisional offices in Ikast, Vejle, and Copenhagen. The group does not impose a unified design language across its portfolio; individual brands operate their own seasonal direction, with varying trend cycle speeds depending on positioning (accessible fashion vs. premium contemporary).

## Retail and Licensing Context

DK Company distributes through its own retail stores as well as wholesale to third-party fashion retailers across Europe. Its private label manufacturing arm (Jasmin) indicates in-house production capability beyond the branded portfolio. The group does not currently appear to operate a formal external licensing programme.
