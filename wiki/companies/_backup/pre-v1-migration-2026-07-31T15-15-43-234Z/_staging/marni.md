## Overview

Marni Group S.r.l. was founded in Milan in 1994 by Swiss designer Consuelo Castiglioni, originally as an offshoot of her family's fur business. The brand expanded into ready-to-wear and began opening in high-end department stores from 2000. Renzo Rosso's [[OTB Group]] acquired a 60% stake in December 2012; full acquisition followed in November 2015. Consuelo Castiglioni and other family members left the company in 2016. Francesco Risso was appointed creative director in October 2016 and led the brand until 2025; Belgian designer Meryll Rogge was announced as new creative director in July 2025. ## Market Position

Marni operates in Italian luxury fashion across womenswear, menswear, accessories, footwear, kidswear, fragrance, and eyewear. The brand launched a high-profile H&M collaboration in 2012 (promotional film by Sofia Coppola, shot in Marrakesh with Liu Wen and Imogen Poots) — signalling accessibility beyond its luxury retail position. A Coty beauty licensing deal signed February 2024 extends through 2040, with first products launching 2026. During Milan Design Week 2023 Marni launched tableware with Belgian design label Serax and a wallpaper series with Italian wall-covering manufacturer LondonArt.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Bold, eclectic prints and colour combinations are central to Marni's creative identity — the brand is among the most print-focused in Italian luxury fashion:

- **Print as signature**: Marni's womenswear has historically been defined by unconventional print choices — abstract, floral, graphic, and check combinations applied in unexpected colourways; this is one of the most explicit commercial print identities in the contemporary Italian luxury sector
- **Wallpaper series with LondonArt (2023)**: Direct surface pattern design product — wall covering application of Marni's visual language; produced with Italian wall-covering manufacturer LondonArt during Milan Design Week 2023
- **Tableware with Serax (2023)**: Surface design applied to tableware form via collaboration with Belgian design label Serax; Marni's patterns applied to ceramic and glass surfaces
- **H&M collaboration (2012)**: Marni's print and pattern approach distributed at mass market scale — one of the most successful instances of Italian luxury pattern language reaching a broad commercial audience
- **Eyewear surface (Retrosuperfuture, from 2021)**: Frame design translating Marni's surface aesthetic to eyewear; Retrosuperfuture known for distinctive frame colour and surface treatment
- **Coty beauty (from 2026)**: Fragrance and cosmetics packaging — Marni's surface design vocabulary applied to beauty packaging surfaces

## Design Direction

Consuelo Castiglioni established Marni's print-led identity from 1994 through her departure in 2016. Francesco Risso (2016–2025) continued in a more expressive, maximalist direction described in the Financial Times as "remaking Marni in his own image." Meryll Rogge (from July 2025) brings a Belgian avant-garde background. OTB Group's manufacturing arm Staff International manages men's production from 2015. The brand's home design extensions (wallpaper, tableware) signal expansion of Marni's surface language beyond fashion into interior and product categories.
