## Overview

Diesel SpA is an Italian fashion company founded in 1978 by Renzo Rosso and Adriano Goldschmied in Breganze, Veneto, Italy. The brand name was chosen for its cross-language ease of pronunciation. Renzo Rosso acquired sole ownership in 1985. Diesel operates in men's and women's ready-to-wear, denim, accessories, footwear, fragrance, eyewear, watches, and kidswear. The first flagship retail store opened in 1996 on Lexington Avenue, New York. By 2012, the company generated €1.5 billion in annual revenue across 5,000+ points of sale in 80 countries, employing approximately 3,000 people globally. Revenue in France reached €80 million (2006). Top store markets: Japan (75 stores) and Italy (67 stores). In March 2019, Diesel filed for bankruptcy protection in the United States. Glenn Martens became artistic director in 2021.

Diesel's design identity has been characterised by provocation and cultural subversion since 1991. The "For Successful Living" tagline parodied 1950s advertising optimism; later campaigns engaged climate change and geopolitical inversion. Glenn Martens (from 2021) is associated with a technically sophisticated, conceptual approach to denim construction and silhouette. ## History

Renzo Rosso and Adriano Goldschmied founded Diesel in 1978; Rosso took full ownership in 1985 and built the brand into a premium global denim label. Artistic directors: Andreas Melbostad (Diesel Black Gold line, from October 2012); Nicola Formichetti (first brand-wide artistic director, from spring 2013 — former stylist for Lady Gaga and creative director of Thierry Mugler; responsibilities covered products, communications, marketing, and retail interior design); Glenn Martens (from 2021, current). Formichetti's debut initiative was the #Reboot advertising campaign on Tumblr, shot by Inez & Vinoodh, featuring graffiti artists and film students rather than conventional models. Production: formerly Italy; now China, Morocco, Tunisia (textiles); Romania (small leather goods).

## Markets

Diesel operates in the premium accessible fashion segment — positioned above high street, below full luxury, with premium denim as the commercial core. Primary target demographic: 18–35-year-old urban consumers globally. The brand's marketing history — provocative campaigns via Paradiset DDB (from 1991), Kesselskramer, and Terry Richardson ("Highway to Heaven", 2006); "For Successful Living" ironic tagline — historically defined its irreverent, youth-oriented positioning. Formichetti's tenure opened the brand to internet culture and social participation; Martens represents a return to technical rigour in denim construction.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Diesel's SPD relevance centres on denim and garment surface design:

- **Denim surface treatments**: Diesel's core product involves extensive surface design — wash treatments, distressing, dyeing, embellishment, and print applied to denim. Premium positioning has historically been delivered through surface differentiation of the denim itself.
- **Logo as applied surface mark**: The Diesel wordmark sewn to the jeans pocket and printed across garments is one of the most commercially recognised applied brand marks in premium denim — a surface graphic identity applied at mass scale.
- **Adidas Originals Denim by Diesel (2008)**: Co-branded product combining two major brand marks as surface design elements on a single denim product.
- **Retail environment surface design**: Under Formichetti, interior design fell under creative direction — making retail surface environment design a documented brand priority. The Dieselwall (external wall installations in cities including Berlin) treats urban surfaces as brand design environments.
- **Marketing image surface application**: Campaign imagery from Inez & Vinoodh and Terry Richardson is applied to garment hangtags, bags, and in-store print — a recurring form of editorial surface application.
