## Overview

Central Group is a Thai multinational conglomerate founded by Tiang and Samrit Chirathivat in 1947 and still privately owned by the Chirathivat family as of 2025. Tiang Chirathivat emigrated from Hainan Island to Bangkok in 1925, opened a first shop in the Thon Buri district, then established a store near the Mandarin Oriental Hotel with his eldest son Samrit in 1947. In 1956, the family opened Central Department Store in Wang Burapa, Bangkok — the largest department store in Thailand at the time. Central Group's publicly traded subsidiaries include Central Retail, Central Pattana (commercial real estate development), and Central Plaza Hotel (hospitality). Its privately held subsidiaries operate prestigious European department stores plus financial services and ASEAN e-commerce.

## History

Central Group's European luxury department store portfolio (private subsidiaries):
- Illum (Denmark, est. 1891, acquired 2013)
- Alsterhaus, Kaufhaus des Westens (KaDeWe), Oberpollinger (Germany, acquired 2015 from KaDeWe Group)
- La Rinascente (Italy, est. 1865, acquired 2011)
- Arnotts (Ireland, est. 1843, acquired 2022)
- Brown Thomas (Ireland, est. 1848, held since 1983)
- De Bijenkorf (Netherlands, est. 1870, acquired 2022)
- Globus (Switzerland, est. 1907, acquired 2020)
- Selfridges (UK, est. 1908, acquired 2022)

Arnotts, Brown Thomas, De Bijenkorf, and Selfridges are held as a joint venture with the Saudi Arabian Public Investment Fund (PIF): Central Group 60%, PIF 40%.

Central Retail (public subsidiary) operates Big C (1993), Central Department Store (1927), Robinson Department Store (acquired 1995), Tops supermarket (1996), Zen (1989, rebranded to Central 2019), and others across Thailand, Vietnam, and Indonesia. Central Pattana (founded 1980) operates shopping centres including CentralWorld (Bangkok, 2002, first super-regional mall in Bangkok) and Central Embassy (Bangkok, 2014). The portfolio spans Malaysia (Central i-City) and extends to planned openings through 2026–2027. Central Plaza Hotel operates hotels under Centara brands (Centara Reserve, Centara Grand, etc.) plus Park Hyatt Bangkok, Hilton Pattaya, and co-investment in StayFactory Hotel Group (Japan). Restaurant chains include Mister Donut, KFC, Auntie Anne's, Pepper Lunch, Yoshinoya, Ootoya, Tenya, and Katsuya.

## Services

Central Group is the parent of Selfridges Group (Selfridges, Brown Thomas, Arnotts, De Bijenkorf), held as a 60/40 joint venture with Saudi PIF following Signa Holding's insolvency (2023–2024). La Rinascente and the German KaDeWe Group stores are private Central Group subsidiaries. Globus (Switzerland) acquired 2020. Brown Thomas held since 1983.

## Markets

Central Group is one of the largest retail and commercial property conglomerates in Southeast Asia and among the most significant holders of European luxury department store brands. Its portfolio of historic European stores (Selfridges, KaDeWe, La Rinascente, Brown Thomas, De Bijenkorf, Illum) positions it as a major force in premium retail across Western Europe. Within ASEAN, it is the dominant department store, mall development, and hospitality group in Thailand.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Central Group is relevant to SPD primarily as the owner of major European luxury department stores — collectively among the most significant fashion and surface design retail environments in Europe:

- **Selfridges** (London) and **Brown Thomas** (Dublin) — key luxury multi-brand fashion retail environments; major channels for global fashion print, accessories, home textiles, and designer goods
- **De Bijenkorf** (Netherlands) — Netherlands' leading luxury department store
- **KaDeWe** (Berlin) — Germany's most prominent luxury department store; global destination for fashion and design
- **La Rinascente** (Milan/Rome/other Italian cities) — Italy's leading upmarket department store chain; a primary retail destination for Italian and international SPD output across fashion, home, and accessories
- **Illum** (Copenhagen) — Scandinavia's leading luxury department store

Together, these stores constitute a European network of premium SPD retail environments. Central Village Luxury Outlet (Bangkok, 2019) and CentralWorld extend the group's luxury fashion retail infrastructure into Southeast Asia. Central Embassy (Bangkok, 2014) is Thailand's leading ultra-luxury retail destination.
