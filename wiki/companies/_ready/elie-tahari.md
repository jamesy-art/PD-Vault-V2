## Overview

Elie Tahari is the namesake fashion brand of Israeli-American designer and entrepreneur Elie Tahari (born Jerusalem, 1952). The company is headquartered in New York City. Tahari arrived in the US in 1971 and launched his label in 1974 with a boutique on Madison Avenue. The brand is best known for tailored women's suiting and ready-to-wear, and reached $1 billion in total brand sales (including licensed products) in 2019. The brand celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2024. Tahari's son Jeremey currently serves as CEO and creative director.

Elie Tahari built the brand around a tailored aesthetic influenced by office-centric dressing for women, particularly from the 1980s onward. The brand is described as focused on refined ready-to-wear for modern women. The current CEO and creative director is Jeremey Tahari (Elie's son), continuing family control of the brand's direction.

## History

Tahari's entry into fashion was unconventional — he worked as an electrician in New York's Garment District before designing a tube top that gained commercial traction in 1973. His namesake label launched in 1974 and first showed at Studio 54 in 1977. His first boutique opened on Madison Avenue in 1979. In 1989, the brand entered luxury department store distribution, beginning with Bloomingdale's. In 1994, Tahari was admitted to the Council of Fashion Designers of America. In 1997, he co-founded Theory with Andrew Rosen; the label was sold to Link International for $100 million in 2003.

The 2000s saw the brand expand into men's RTW, accessories, jewelry, footwear, and home goods. In 2014, Tahari collaborated with Kohl's on the DesigNation programme, placing the brand into mass-market distribution. The ET Sport sub-line launched in 2015. In 2021, following COVID-19 disruption to the luxury department store channel, Tahari closed the brand's wholesale division ($200 million in annual sales) and transitioned to a direct-to-consumer model centred on branded boutique, outlet, and e-commerce.

## Products

Core product categories include tailored women's suiting, dresses, jackets, coats, tops, and bottoms. Accessory and home goods categories were active in the 2000s–2010s, though the current scope under the direct-to-consumer model is narrower. The Kohl's DesigNation collaboration (2014) placed Tahari-branded product at high street price points. The ET Sport line (2015) introduced an activewear/sportswear category. Elie Tahari is also held as a licensed brand by Bluestar Alliance, which manages its commercial IP alongside names such as Off-White, Scotch & Soda, and Hurley.

## Services

The brand pivoted from a wholesale-dominant model (department stores including Bloomingdale's, Saks, Neiman Marcus) to direct-to-consumer in 2021. The Kohl's DesigNation collaboration (2014) was a documented mass-market licensing exercise. Bluestar Alliance now holds the Elie Tahari brand within its licensing portfolio alongside 600+ licensees and $13B+ in global retail sales across its managed brands.

## Markets

Elie Tahari operates in the accessible luxury and premium contemporary women's fashion segment, with particular strength in tailored suits and work-appropriate RTW. The brand's $1 billion in 2019 total sales (direct + licensed) confirms meaningful commercial scale. The 2021 move away from wholesale was framed by Tahari as a response to the unprofitability of the department store channel following COVID-19 closures. The brand now operates through its own retail, outlet, and online channels.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Tailored women's suiting at the accessible luxury tier relies on fabric quality and textile surface as key commercial signals. Seasonal fabric selection — including woven patterns such as houndstooth, tweed, check, and fine stripe — is central to the brand's proposition. The Kohl's collaboration extended pattern and print decisions to mass-market volumes. The accessories and home goods categories (active 2000s–2010s) would have required surface design for textiles, upholstery, and accessories. Bluestar Alliance's ownership of the Elie Tahari brand IP (for licensed product production) means the brand surface is now applied to goods by multiple licensees.
