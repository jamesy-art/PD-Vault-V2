## Overview

Sycamore Partners is a New York-based private equity firm founded in 2011 by Stefan Kaluzny and Peter Morrow. Kaluzny previously served as managing partner at Golden Gate Capital and also co-founded Delray Farms, Inc. Morrow was a Principal at Golden Gate Capital before co-founding Sycamore. The firm has approximately $10 billion in aggregate committed capital and focuses on retail and consumer sector acquisitions. Since its founding, Sycamore has assembled and divested a large portfolio of fashion, apparel, and retail businesses, with a particular concentration in women's apparel brands and department stores. ## Markets

Sycamore Partners is one of the most active private equity acquirers in US fashion retail, with a particular focus on women's apparel and consumer/retail brands in distress or undergoing ownership transitions. Notable transactions include: Talbots ($391M, 2012); Hot Topic and Torrid ($600M combined, 2013); Belk ($3B, 2015); The Limited ($26.8M bankruptcy auction, 2017); Ann Taylor, Lane Bryant, LOFT, Lou & Grey (from Ascena Retail Group, December 2020, via Premium Apparel LLC affiliate); and Walgreens Boots Alliance (~$10B, completed August 2025). The firm has also operated outside fashion — acquiring Staples ($6.9B, 2017), NBG Home (2017), Azamara Cruises (2021), and Rona Inc. (2023). Labour controversy: in late 2025, US Senator Bernie Sanders publicly criticised Sycamore Partners for eliminating paid holidays for hourly Walgreens workers following the acquisition.
