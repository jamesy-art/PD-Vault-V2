## Overview

Reusch International S.p.A. AG is an Italian sports equipment manufacturer headquartered in Bolzano, South Tyrol. Founded in 1934 by Karl Reusch in the attic of his home in Metzingen, Germany, the company was built into the global market leader in sports gloves under the stewardship of his son Gebhard Reusch. In 1973, working closely with German goalkeeper Sepp Maier, Gebhard Reusch developed the world's first goalkeeper glove — a latex-palmed design that revolutionised the position. During the 1970s, the company produced over 330,000 goalkeeper gloves and one million ski gloves annually; by the 1980s and 1990s it held world market leadership in sports gloves. Gebhard Reusch sold the company to Pentland Group in 1996; it was sold again in 2001 to a consortium including the Weitzmann family from Bolzano, who have been sole owners since 2015. Headquarters were relocated from Reutlingen, Germany to Bolzano, Italy in June 2017. A new logistics and production site in Vignate (Milan area) was opened in 2025, covering 15,900 square metres.

No named creative director or lead designer is documented in the source. The Weitzmann family (Erich Weitzmann as managing director, the Weitzmann family as sole owners since 2015) leads the company. Reusch's product identity is defined by functional performance and world-market-leader athletic brand authority rather than a fashion design direction. The company's established relationships with elite athletes — across FIFA World Cup, Alpine skiing, and UEFA European Championships — provide applied product validation that anchors commercial positioning.

## History

- 1934: Karl Reusch begins developing gloves in Metzingen, Germany
- 1972: Son Gebhard Reusch joins; launches ski gloves reinforced with synthetic material for slalom pole protection
- 1973: Gebhard Reusch and German goalkeeper Sepp Maier develop world's first goalkeeper glove (Soft Grip; latex palm; catches supported by adhesive palm layer)
- 1974 FIFA World Cup (Germany): Sepp Maier wears Reusch gloves in competition — first major international visibility for the goalkeeper glove; first latex palm gloves
- 1970s peak: 330,000+ goalkeeper gloves and 1M+ ski gloves produced annually
- 1980s–1990s: World market leader in sports gloves; 12 of 18 Bundesliga goalkeepers use Reusch
- 1987: FIS Alpine World Ski Championships — Reusch supplies Swiss national team
- 1989: First ski clothing collection launched
- 1992: First team sports collection launched
- 1996: Gebhard Reusch sells company to British Pentland Group (for £12M per The Independent 1994 — source references 1996 as the sale date)
- 2000: Reusch family ends active involvement
- 2001: Reusch sold by Pentland to consortium including Weitzmann family (Bolzano)
- 2009: Product development relocated to Bolzano; Stefan Weitzmann's son Erich Weitzmann active as managing director
- 2015: Entirely owned by the Weitzmann family
- May 2017: Converted to joint stock company (100% family-owned)
- June 1, 2017: HQ relocated from Reutlingen, Germany to Bolzano, Italy (Reutlingen site continues operating)
- Since January 1, 2023: Distribution partner for Speedo in Italy and Austria
- September 2024: Major fire in Reutlingen buildings — approximately 50% of inventory destroyed; 98%+ delivery rate restored by November 2024
- 2025: New site opened in Vignate (Milan area); 15,900 sqm; logistics consolidation

## Products

**Winter sports gloves** — ski gloves across men's, women's, and children's ranges; reinforced slalom construction; Gore-Tex, PrimaLoft, and Polartec materials; annual summer and winter collections

**Goalkeeper gloves** — world-first product (1973); latex palm technology; 250+ goalkeepers in top national leagues globally; as of 2024, Alisson Becker and Gregor Kobel among current users; 2025 Attrakt Fusion Carbon 3D (3D-printed punching zone with Carbon, elastic lattice structure for improved cushioning)

**Ski clothing** — collection launched 1989; ski apparel for winter sports

**Team sports collection** — launched 1992; football and other team sports accessories

**Bogner gloves** — Reusch produces gloves under the German Bogner brand (documented distribution arrangement)

## Services

Reusch distributes through 55 international partners in 50+ countries. Ten European markets are supplied directly from Bolzano headquarters. Logistics operations are based in both Reutlingen (Germany) and Milan (Italy), with the new Vignate site (2025) consolidating logistics in a single 15,900 sqm facility. Distribution partnerships in Italy and Austria include Speedo (from January 2023), Red Bull Spect Eyewear (Italy), and Deuter Sport (Italy). Bogner gloves are produced by Reusch under a separate distribution/manufacturing arrangement. Contract production for Moncler, Prada, and Beretta represents a luxury manufacturing service segment.

## Markets

Reusch operates as the world market leader in sports gloves, with dominant positions in both winter sports (ski gloves) and football goalkeeping (goalkeeper gloves). These are two distinct product categories sharing manufacturing competence in glove construction. Strongest European sales markets are Italy, France, and German-speaking Europe. Two-thirds of revenue derives from winter sports products (2017). The company distributes through 55 international partners across 50+ countries on five continents; ten European countries are supplied directly from headquarters. Official supplier status to seven national ski associations (Austria, Switzerland, France, USA, Norway, Liechtenstein, Slovenia) gives Reusch consistent international competition visibility. Contract manufacturing for Moncler, Prada, and Beretta diversifies the client base beyond direct-to-athlete product.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Reusch's SPD relevance is indirect but present across two distinct dimensions: technical textile surface engineering and luxury contract manufacturing.

**Glove outer shell surface design** — sports gloves are surface design objects: the outer shell of a ski glove or goalkeeper glove involves colour, material texture, panel construction, and logo placement as the primary visual design elements. Reusch's seasonal ski glove collections require colour story, material finish, and graphic identity decisions across men's, women's, and children's ranges. The company's premium positioning and world market leader status means these decisions influence the aesthetics of ski glove design globally.

**Technical textile materials as surface identity** — Reusch's use of Gore-Tex, PrimaLoft, and Polartec in its glove and apparel products brings premium technical textile surfaces into its product construction. These materials — with their distinctive technical finishes, membrane structures, and branding requirements — are surface design elements as well as functional components. The 2025 3D-printed Carbon lattice punching zone on the Attrakt Fusion Carbon 3D goalkeeper glove represents a new approach to sports product surface structure: a designed lattice geometry serving both function and visual identity.

**Contract manufacturing for luxury brands** — Reusch carries out contract production for Moncler, Prada, and Beretta. This places Reusch's manufacturing competence in direct service of luxury fashion brands with distinct surface design identities. Producing gloves for Moncler (quilted luxury outerwear) and Prada (minimalist Italian luxury accessories) requires translation of each brand's visual language into glove construction — a direct applied surface design activity. No further detail on these relationships is documented beyond the source citation.

**Speedo distribution partnership** — since January 2023, Reusch has distributed Speedo products in Italy and Austria. Speedo's swimwear and swim accessories involve substantial print and pattern design; this distribution relationship extends Reusch's commercial presence into the swimwear surface design market.
