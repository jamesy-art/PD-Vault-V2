# The De Rigo Group Vision, Heritage and Innovation

## Profile Image

![Profile](../../assets/companies/the-de-rigo-group/profile/the-de-rigo-group-logo.jpg)

## Overview

De Rigo Group is an Italian eyewear group headquartered in Limana, Belluno — the heartland of Italy's eyewear manufacturing tradition in the Dolomites region of Veneto. Founded in 1978 by the De Rigo family as Charme Lunettes, a 30-person artisan workshop producing sunglasses and prescription frames for third parties, the company grew into one of the most significant integrated eyewear groups globally. Two family companies — Charme Lunettes (maker of Police) and Argosol (maker of Sting) — merged in 1995 to form De Rigo S.p.A., which listed on the NYSE the same year. From 1995, De Rigo developed a licensed brand portfolio, securing rights to produce and distribute prescription and sunglass frames for major fashion houses. The group expanded into optical retail through the acquisition of Dollond & Aitchison (UK, 1998, the world's oldest optical chain), General Optica (Spain, 2000), and in 2009 merged Dollond & Aitchison with Boots Opticians to create the UK's second-largest optical chain. In 2023, De Rigo acquired Rodenstock's Eyewear Division, adding the Rodenstock and Porsche Design Eyewear brands and strengthening presence in premium Central European markets. The group currently operates 23 branches, distributes in 100+ countries through 100 independent distributors, employs 3,400 people, and launches 5,000 new eyewear styles per year. In 2025, De Rigo achieved ISO 14001:2015 environmental certification and installed photovoltaic systems at its central warehouse.

No individual designer or creative director is named in the source for De Rigo Group's house brands or licensed collections. The group's design output is documented by scale — 5,000 new styles per year — rather than by named creative leadership. Police's 40-year history as a fashion eyewear and lifestyle brand implies sustained design direction; its positioning in the 1983 source description as a brand that "embraced the spirit of the times" and contributed to eyewear's transition from utility to fashion accessory suggests a design-forward brand identity. Yalea (launched 2021, dedicated to women) is the group's newest house brand, but no design brief or creative direction is detailed in the source.

## History

- 1978: De Rigo family founds Charme Lunettes in Limana, Belluno; 30 employees; produces sunglasses and prescription frames for third parties
- 1983: Lozza acquired — Italy's longest-standing eyewear brand (established 1878); Police brand launched; eyewear transitions from necessity to fashion accessory
- 1985: Sting Eyewear launched; second house brand; aimed at 15–25 age group
- 1988: First international branch opens in Barcelona, Spain
- 1995: Charme Lunettes and Argosol (maker of Sting) merge to form De Rigo S.p.A.; company enters licensed fashion eyewear for major fashion houses; NYSE listed; direct distribution extended to Germany, Austria, France, UK, Spain
- 1998: Police begins lifestyle brand development — fragrance licence signed (first step); Dollond & Aitchison (UK's oldest and leading optical chain) acquired
- 2000: Branches in Japan and Hong Kong; General Optica acquired (leading optical chain in Iberian Peninsula)
- 2002: Branch in Greece
- 2003: Police becomes a full lifestyle brand comprising fragrances, watches, jewellery, and small leather goods
- 2008: New branches in Croatia, Brazil, Portugal, and China
- 2009: Dollond & Aitchison merged with Boots Opticians — creates UK's second-largest optical retail chain (630+ stores, 5,000+ employees); Turkey branch opened; Opmar Optik (Turkey's second-largest optical chain) acquired
- 2012: US branch inaugurated
- 2016: REM Optical Co. (USA) acquired; branch moves to Los Angeles; all REM Eyewear shares acquired; Australia branch opened
- 2018: De Rigo's 40th anniversary; Fondazione De Rigo H.E.ART (Health, Education & Art for Youth) founded; Ennio De Rigo awarded Cavaliere del Lavoro by Italian President Sergio Mattarella
- 2020: Finland branch opened
- 2021: Yalea launched — De Rigo's fourth house brand; dedicated to women; Lozza designated a 'Historic Brand of National Interest' by Italian Ministry of Economic Development
- 2023: Rodenstock's Eyewear Division acquired — adds Rodenstock Eyewear and Porsche Design Eyewear; strengthens premium segment position in Germany and Central European markets; Police brand celebrates 40th anniversary
- 2025: ISO 14001:2015 environmental certification achieved; photovoltaic system installed at central warehouse

## Services

De Rigo operates across wholesale and retail channels simultaneously. The wholesale distribution network — 23 branches plus 100 independent distributors in 100+ countries — serves optical retailers globally with De Rigo house brands and licensed collections. The retail division — Boots Opticians (UK, 630+ stores), General Optica (Spain, 290 stores), Mais Optica (Portugal, 34 stores), Opmar Optik (Turkey, 75 stores) — provides owned optical retail at significant scale in four European/Turkish markets. The group listed on the NYSE in 1995 during its first phase of international expansion. Police's lifestyle brand licensing programme (fragrances, watches, jewellery, small leather goods) extends the brand's commercial footprint beyond eyewear through third-party licence agreements; no specific licensees are named in the source.

## Markets

De Rigo operates across the full eyewear value chain: design and manufacturing in Italy (Belluno), distribution through 23 direct branches and 100 distributors in 100+ countries, retail through four owned optical chains (Boots Opticians, General Optica, Mais Optica, Opmar Optik), and brand building through four house brands (Police, Sting, Lozza, Yalea) plus a licensed fashion brand portfolio. The house brand Police — 40 years old in 2023 — represents the group's most significant brand asset and has expanded beyond eyewear into a lifestyle brand (fragrances, watches, jewellery, small leather goods). The 2023 Rodenstock acquisition extends De Rigo into the premium optics segment, reinforcing its position against EssilorLuxottica and Kering Eyewear in the European prescription eyewear market. With 5,000 new styles per year, De Rigo is a high-volume eyewear design operation, positioned at a competitive disadvantage in scale versus EssilorLuxottica but ahead of most mid-tier Italian eyewear groups.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

De Rigo's surface pattern design relevance is embedded in the eyewear frame as a surface design object and the role of material surface choices in differentiating 5,000 new styles per year across house brands and licensed fashion collections.

**Eyewear frame as surface design** — an eyewear frame is a three-dimensional product whose visual identity is almost entirely determined by surface design decisions: acetate colour and pattern (solid, tortoiseshell, marble, gradient, multi-layer, jaspe), metal finish (matte, polished, brushed, anodised), temple decoration (printed pattern, engraving, inlay), and lens tint and coating. The choice and development of acetate colourways and patterns is directly analogous to textile surface design — the acetate sheets used in frame production are manufactured with surface patterns embedded in the material, and the cut of the frame reveals a cross-section of the layered acetate, yielding characteristic visual effects (tortoiseshell, horn, Havana, marbled) that constitute the frame's primary surface identity.

**5,000 new styles per year as design output** — De Rigo develops 5,000 new eyewear styles annually across its brand portfolio. At this volume, the group's design teams make continuous surface decisions — material selection, colourway development, pattern/texture specification — representing a high-frequency surface design operation comparable in scale to large fashion accessories manufacturers.

**Police as lifestyle brand surface translation** — Police extended from eyewear into fragrances, watches, jewellery, and small leather goods from 2003. Each product category requires the Police brand surface language (logo, motif, material vocabulary, colour identity) to be translated into the material and surface conventions of that category — applied surface design across fragrance bottle, watch dial, jewellery surface, and leather goods hardware and surface finishing.

**Licensed fashion brand surface translation** — De Rigo's licensed fashion eyewear portfolio requires each fashion house's brand visual identity to be translated accurately into eyewear frame design: logo placement, brand colourway, signature motif as applied surface element, and brand-consistent material selection. This is analogous to the licensed surface design activity documented for Safilo Group — producing eyewear that carries the visual surface language of a fashion house's broader brand design.

**Rodenstock and Porsche Design Eyewear** — the 2023 acquisition adds Porsche Design Eyewear to the portfolio. Porsche Design's brand identity is built on a functional-industrial aesthetic translated across eyewear, watches, luggage, and apparel; its eyewear surface language (titanium, technical finishes, precision engineering) is a distinctive surface design vocabulary in the premium optical segment.

**Lozza heritage** — as Italy's oldest continuously active eyewear brand (established 1878), now designated a Historic Brand of National Interest, Lozza represents a heritage of Italian eyewear surface craft traditions — acetate work, hand-finishing, and material artisanry from the Belluno district.
