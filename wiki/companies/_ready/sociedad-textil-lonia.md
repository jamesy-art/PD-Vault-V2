# Sociedad Textil lonia

## Overview

Sociedad Textil Lonia (STL) is a Spanish fashion group founded in 1997 and dedicated to the design, production, and commercialisation of clothing and accessories for women, men, and children. Headquartered in Pereiro de Aguiar, Ourense (Galicia, Spain), the company operates from a fully integrated campus of 45,300 square metres housing six interconnected buildings in which design, production, and distribution are unified in a single process. STL launched its first designer label — Purificación García — in August 1998, expanding first in Spain and Portugal before achieving wider international presence. In 2000, the company reached an agreement with Carolina Herrera Ltd. to create the lifestyle brand CH Carolina Herrera; this partnership culminated in 2014 when Carolina Herrera Ltd. became an STL shareholder. The group operates stores across four continents with a staff of over 2,500 employees.

No named creative director is documented in the source for either brand. Purificación García, as a designer brand, implies the involvement of its namesake designer (Purificación García) in creative direction; however, no details of her design philosophy, aesthetic, or background are documented in the source. CH Carolina Herrera's design direction is structured within the broader Carolina Herrera brand system, with STL's role as the operator of the lifestyle tier.

## Products

**Purificación García** — Spanish designer brand; women's, men's, and children's clothing and accessories; launched August 1998; the company's founding and primary own brand; built market presence initially in Spain and Portugal before international expansion

**CH Carolina Herrera** — lifestyle brand created under licence from Carolina Herrera Ltd.; apparel and accessories for women, men, and children; created 2000 in agreement with Carolina Herrera Ltd.; distinguished from the main Carolina Herrera luxury line by its "Lifestyle" and "CH" positioning

Both brands span clothing and accessories for women, men, and children — consistent with STL's founding scope of designing, producing, and commercialising across all three demographics.

## Services

STL operates both brands through its own store network, which spans four continents. The dual-brand structure — one wholly owned (Purificación García) and one created through a licence/partnership arrangement (CH Carolina Herrera) — is a common structure in Spanish fashion groups, which have historically used international brand licensing partnerships to establish credibility and scale. The 2014 shareholder development, where Carolina Herrera Ltd. became an STL shareholder (rather than STL remaining merely a licensee), represents a deeper structural integration than a standard licensing agreement and suggests mutual commercial commitment to the CH brand's long-term development. STL's founding description as a "global company from the very beginning" — backed by the Ourense integrated campus with design and production under one roof — reflects an ambition that goes beyond domestic Spanish fashion retail.

## Markets

STL occupies the accessible-to-premium Spanish fashion market with two brand pillars: Purificación García (a Spanish designer brand with a clear aesthetic identity) and CH Carolina Herrera (a lifestyle brand created in partnership with the Venezuelan-American luxury house). The Purificación García brand built its positioning in Spain and Portugal first before expanding internationally — a domestic-first then international model typical of Spanish fashion group strategy. The CH Carolina Herrera licence relationship, which evolved from a 2000 commercial agreement into Carolina Herrera Ltd. becoming an STL shareholder in 2014, gives STL a direct structural connection to one of the most internationally recognised Latin American luxury fashion houses. The group's Galician base (Ourense, northwest Spain) places it geographically alongside the Zara/Inditex ecosystem, though at a much smaller scale and in a different market tier.
