## Overview

United Arrows is a Japanese clothing brand founded in 1989 by Yasuto Kamoshita, Hirofumi Kurino, and Osamu Shigematsu. The company operates in the Japanese select shop and contemporary fashion retail market. The Camoshita United Arrows sub-brand was established in 2007. The brand has documented collaborative product releases with New Balance (30th anniversary M1500, 2019), The North Face Purple Label (mountain outerwear via Beauty & Youth sub-line), Dr. Martens (bit loafer reimagining via United Arrows & Sons), adidas Originals (Continental 80 via Beauty & Youth), and HUF (capsule collection via United Arrows & Sons, January 2020). The Wikipedia source positions United Arrows within a broader Japanese and global streetwear/select shop context.

## History

- 2020 (January): HUF x United Arrows & Sons capsule collection
