## Overview

L Capital was the private equity arm associated with LVMH and Groupe Arnault, investing in consumer and fashion brands globally. In 2016, L Capital (Europe and Asia) merged with Catterton Partners (US consumer-focused private equity) to form L Catterton — a unified global consumer private equity platform backed by LVMH and Groupe Arnault. The YAML website (lcatterton.com) confirms this file represents the L Catterton entity. L Catterton manages multiple funds across buyout, growth, and venture stages, investing in consumer brands across food and beverage, health and wellness, beauty, fashion, retail, and consumer services. SMCP Group — the French accessible luxury group whose brands include Sandro, Maje, and Claudie Pierlot — was a portfolio investment. ## Markets

L Catterton is one of the largest consumer-focused private equity firms globally, with particular depth in fashion, beauty, and lifestyle. Its strategic relationship with LVMH and Groupe Arnault gives it access to luxury market insight and operational support that differentiates it from generalist PE firms investing in consumer brands. Portfolio span covers emerging and established consumer brands across global markets.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

L Catterton's relevance to surface pattern design is as a capital owner and growth partner for fashion and consumer brands:

- **SMCP Group investment**: Sandro, Maje, and Claudie Pierlot are French accessible luxury brands with defined seasonal print and surface design direction — L Capital's investment gave it ownership and governance influence over brands whose product identity depends on strong print and colour design each season.
- **Consumer brand portfolio**: L Catterton's broader portfolio (health/wellness, beauty, lifestyle, fashion) includes brands where surface, packaging, and print design are core commercial differentiators.
- **LVMH strategic alignment**: The Groupe Arnault/LVMH backing connects L Catterton to the primary global luxury ecosystem — giving it a privileged channel to understand how brands use surface design investment as a value-building tool.
