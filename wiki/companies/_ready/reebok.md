## Overview

Reebok traces its origins to J.W. Foster and Sons, founded in 1895 in Bolton, Lancashire, England by Joseph William Foster (maker of the first spiked running shoes). Jeff Foster and Joe Foster, grandsons of the founder, formed Reebok as a companion company in 1958 — the name taken from the Afrikaans word for the grey rhebok (an African antelope). Paul Fireman licensed Reebok for the US market at the Chicago National Sporting Goods Association show in 1979. In 1982 the Reebok Freestyle became the first athletic shoe designed for women. In 1984 Fireman bought the English parent company. Reebok International Limited was listed on the NYSE in 1985 (ticker RBK) and briefly overtook Nike as the largest US athletic shoe company in 1986. Adidas acquired Reebok in 2005 for $3.8 billion. Authentic Brands Group acquired Reebok from Adidas on 12 August 2021 ($2.5 billion deal; finalised 1 March 2022). HQ: Boston MA Seaport District (relocated from Canton MA in 2016). YAML parent_company "authentic-brands-group" is correct. YAML location "United States" is correct.

Reebok's design identity has oscillated between sport-performance, fashion/urban, and fitness lifestyle across its history. The 1982 Freestyle established the brand in women's fitness aesthetic. The Rbk era (2000s) pushed toward urban celebrity culture. The 2019 restoration of the vector logo and Motter Tektura script signals a return to heritage positioning. Under ABG, Reebok operates through licensees whose design teams manage brand consistency within ABG-set guidelines. The CrossFit and Les Mills partnerships sustained a training-focused product direction through the 2010s. Celebrity and fashion collaborations (Jay-Z, 50 Cent, Victoria Beckham, Ariana Grande, Gal Gadot, Gigi Hadid, Cardi B) have been consistent design communication vehicles.

## Markets

Reebok is a global athletic footwear and apparel brand operating primarily in the fitness, cross-training, and lifestyle categories. Key market positions: CrossFit official partner (from 2010); Les Mills official partner (from 2013); UFC exclusive apparel partner (six-year deal from 2015, ended 2021); NFL official uniform and apparel licence (2002–2012); NBA official uniform and apparel licence (2004–2017); NHL official uniform and apparel licence (2007–2017); CFL licence (2004–2015). Under ABG ownership, Reebok operates through a licensing model with Galaxy Universal as the primary US operator (following SPARC Group → Catalyst Brands transition). Reebok Freestyle positioned the brand as the leading women's athletic shoe in the early 1980s aerobics boom. The Rbk fashion-oriented subbrand (early-to-mid 2000s) was targeted at urban street and celebrity culture.
