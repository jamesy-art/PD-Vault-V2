## Overview

Ermenegildo Zegna N.V. (Zegna Group) is an Italian luxury fashion house headquartered in Milan, with legal headquarters in the Netherlands since 2022. Founded in 1910 by Ermenegildo Zegna (1892–1966) in Trivero, Biella, as the wool mill Zegna & Giardino (later Lanificio Ermenegildo Zegna), the company began by sourcing fine wool directly from Mongolia, Australia, and South Africa and processing it into luxury fabric. From this single Piedmont operation, the group has grown into the world's largest men's luxury fashion brand by revenue, listed on the NYSE since 20 December 2021 (via SPAC deal, US$3.1B valuation).

The Zegna family retains 60.3% control; Gianluca Tagliabue became Group CEO on 1 January 2026 (succeeding Gildo Zegna, CEO since 1997). The group operates three consumer brands: ZEGNA (men's luxury ready-to-wear and made-to-measure; 66% of 2021 revenues); Thom Browne (85% acquired 2018, US$500M valuation; 20% of 2021 revenues); and TOM FORD FASHION (license from Estée Lauder effective April 2023; 20+10 year term; men's and women's clothing, accessories, lingerie, fine jewellery, children's, home textiles, and design products). The Filiera — a vertically integrated chain of nine company-controlled Italian textile producers — underpins the group's supply chain from raw material to finished fabric. 2023 revenues: €1.9B; net profit €135.7M; ~500 retail stores worldwide.

Alessandro Sartori is the artistic director of ZEGNA (since June 2016), responsible for creative direction across all ZEGNA brands. Thom Browne remains Chief Creative Officer of the Thom Browne brand. No named creative director for TOM FORD FASHION is identified in the source under the Zegna license.

## History

- 1892–1910: Angelo Zegna (watchmaker, Trivero) opens textile factory in Flecchia
- 1910: Zegna & Giardino wool mill founded in Trivero, Biella (Piedmont Alps) by Ermenegildo Zegna and partners; sourcing wool from Mongolia, Australia, South Africa
- Late 1920s: Lanificio employs 700+ workers; 1,000+ by late 1930s
- 1930s: Ermenegildo Zegna promotes construction of Panoramic Road 232 and plants 500,000+ conifers in Biellese Alps
- 1938: Company begins US exports via Zegna Woollens Corporation, NYC
- 1942: Sons Aldo and Angelo join; renamed Ermenegildo Zegna and Sons; 1,400+ employees by mid-1950s
- 1963: Wool Awards established in Australia
- 1968: First ready-made suit factory in Novara; plants in Spain, Greece, Switzerland; sales offices in France, Germany, UK, US
- 1970: Mohair Trophy established in South Africa
- 1972: Su Misura made-to-measure service launched
- 1980: First owned boutique opens in Paris
- 1985: Milan boutique opens
- 1991: First luxury brand to open a store in China (Beijing flagship)
- 1997: Gildo Zegna (Angelo's son; grandson of founder) becomes CEO
- 2003: Fragrance market entered
- 2008: New HQ Milan (architects Antonio Citterio and Gianmaria Beretta)
- 2009: Tessitura di Novara acquired (silk weaving, cashmere, pure silk, high-end natural fabrics; founded 1932)
- 2012: Minority stake in Pettinatura di Verrone (combing superfine wools, cashmere, vicuña; with Loro Piana and Marzotto)
- 2014: Achill wool farm, Australia (~12,500 sheep) acquired
- 2016: Bonotto acquired (experimental fabrics; "slow factory" model; Bonotto Foundation: 24,000+ artworks)
- 2018: Cappellificio Cervo acquired (historic hattery, Sagliano Micca, Biella; founded 1897); Thom Browne 85% acquired (US$500M valuation)
- 2019: Dondi acquired 65% stake (high-end knitwear and jersey; Carpi/Modena)
- 2020: Agnona (women's fashion brand, held since 1990) sold to Aimone family; Zegna retains 30%
- 2021 (June): Tessitura Ubertino acquired 60% (womenswear fabrics: tweed and jacquard; near Biella)
- 2021 (June): Filati Biagioli Modesto majority acquired jointly with Prada (cashmere and noble yarns; founded 1919/1967; Montale, Pistoia)
- 2021 (December 20): NYSE IPO via SPAC; US$3.1B valuation; US$2.4B market cap
- 2022: Legal headquarters moved to Netherlands; operational headquarters remains Milan
- 2023 (April 29): TOM FORD FASHION license acquired from Estée Lauder; 20+10 year term
- 2023: Luigi Fedeli e Figlio minority acquired jointly with Prada (high-quality knitwear; Monza; founded 1934)
- 2026 (January 1): Gianluca Tagliabue becomes Group CEO

## Products

**ZEGNA brand:** luxury men's ready-to-wear tailoring, suits, knitwear, sportswear, footwear, accessories, fragrance (since 2003), eyewear (Marcolin license since 2014); Su Misura made-to-measure service (since 1972)

**Thom Browne** (85% acquired 2018): luxury menswear and womenswear; signature grey flannel tailoring; grosgrain ribbon trim; madras check and tartan pattern references; sold in ~300 stores in 40 countries; founder Thom Browne retained as Chief Creative Officer

**TOM FORD FASHION** (license from Estée Lauder, effective April 2023): men's and women's fashion; accessories; lingerie; fine jewellery; children's clothing; home textiles; design products; 20-year term with automatic 10-year renewal

**Filiera (nine controlled Italian textile producers):**
- Lanificio Ermenegildo Zegna (Trivero) — wool mill from raw fleece to finished fabric; founding operation
- Tessitura di Novara (acquired 2009) — silk weaving; cashmere; pure silk; high-end natural fabrics
- Pettinatura di Verrone (minority stake 2012) — combing superfine wools, cashmere, vicuña
- Achill wool farm, Australia (acquired 2014) — ~12,500 sheep
- Bonotto (acquired 2016) — experimental fabrics; "slow factory"; 24,000+ artwork archive
- Cappellificio Cervo (acquired 2018) — historic hattery, Biella (founded 1897)
- Dondi (65% stake 2019) — high-end knitwear and jersey fabrics
- Tessitura Ubertino (60% stake 2021) — womenswear fabrics: tweed and jacquard
- Filati Biagioli Modesto (majority stake with Prada 2021) — cashmere and noble yarns

## Services

Zegna Group operates 500+ retail stores worldwide. Zegna was the first luxury brand to enter China (Beijing flagship, 1991). TOM FORD FASHION license from Estée Lauder (effective April 29, 2023): 20-year term with automatic 10-year renewal; covers men's and women's fashion, accessories, lingerie, fine jewellery, children's clothing, home textiles, and design products — the full commercial fashion and licensing scope of the Tom Ford brand. The Marcolin eyewear license (since 2014) and documented partnerships with Maserati and Real Madrid illustrate Zegna's use of licensing for brand extension. Zegna entered the fragrance market in 2003.

## Markets

Zegna Group is the largest men's luxury fashion brand in the world by revenue. 2023 revenues: €1.9B; net profit €135.7M. Revenue breakdown: APAC 41%, EMEA 35%, North America 22%, Latin America 2%. Over 500 retail stores worldwide. Thom Browne is present in approximately 300 stores across 40 countries and contributes a distinct American luxury aesthetic. TOM FORD FASHION (licensed from 2023) adds a second globally recognised brand. The Filiera positions Zegna as the most vertically integrated luxury group in Italian menswear — controlling supply chain from raw wool sourcing through textile production and retail.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Zegna is fundamentally a textile company: the Lanificio Ermenegildo Zegna wool mill in Trivero has produced luxury fabric since 1910. The Filiera gives Zegna Group direct ownership of or participation in Italian textile producers specialising across wool, cashmere, silk, knit jersey, tweed, jacquard, and hat manufacture — one of the deepest supply-chain integrations in luxury textile production.

**Lanificio Ermenegildo Zegna** — the Trivero wool mill processes shorn fleeces through artisanal and modern technologies from raw material to finished woven fabric. Weave structure, yarn quality, colourway, texture, and surface finish are the primary design outputs. Zegna fabric is supplied to the Zegna brand and historically to other luxury fashion houses.

**Bonotto** (acquired 2016) — characterised as a "slow factory" textile manufacturer producing "experimental fabrics" that reject standardisation. The Bonotto Foundation houses 24,000+ artworks. This experimental fabric orientation implies surface design innovation beyond standard production patterns.

**Tessitura di Novara** (acquired 2009) — specialises in silk weaving, cashmere, pure silk, and high-end natural fabrics. Silk weave structure and surface pattern are core outputs.

**Tessitura Ubertino** (acquired 60% stake 2021) — specialises in high-end womenswear fabrics including tweed and jacquard. Tweed is a woven fabric with complex colour-and-texture surface pattern; jacquard is a woven surface pattern format capable of elaborate decorative motif. These are significant surface pattern design capabilities within the Filiera.

**Dondi** (acquired 65% stake 2019) — high-end knitwear and jersey manufacturer; knit surface structure and knitwear design as luxury fashion fabric.

**Thom Browne** (85% acquired 2018) — distinctive surface pattern vocabulary: signature grosgrain ribbon trim (striped woven ribbon as surface decoration on finished garments), madras check, tartan and plaid references, and grey flannel texture. Thom Browne's pattern use is commercially recognisable and consistent across collections.

**TOM FORD FASHION** (licensed 2023) — includes "home textiles and design products," which directly involves surface pattern design for interior applications.
