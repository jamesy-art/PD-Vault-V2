## Overview

Kurt Geiger is a luxury British footwear and accessories brand, named after its Austrian founder and fashion designer. The company operates approximately 70 standalone stores and concession departments within major UK luxury department stores including Harrods and Selfridges. It owns three brands developed by its in-house team: Kurt Geiger London, KG Kurt Geiger, and Carvela. Headquartered at Britton Street, Clerkenwell, London, in a building remodelled in 2009 by Archer Architects, finished in a distinctive Pompeian Red exterior.

Three in-house brands, each developed by the Kurt Geiger design team. No external design direction or named creative director is documented in the source material. The Clerkenwell headquarters, rebuilt in a distinctive Pompeian Red finish (Archer Architects, 2009), suggests an awareness of design identity extending to the brand's physical environment. The company's luxury department store concession strategy implies seasonal design renewal as a commercial requirement.

## History

Kurt Geiger's recent ownership history: management buyout in 2008 (backed by Graphite Capital) → sold to The Jones Group (US-based brand management company) in 2011 → acquired by Sycamore Partners with The Jones Group in 2014 (spun Kurt Geiger into its own company) → acquired by London-based private equity firm Cinven in December 2015 for £245 million → acquired by US footwear company Steve Madden in February 2025 for £289 million.

Neil Clifford has been CEO since 2003, having joined the company in 1995. Clifford is credited with expanding the business beyond the UK, establishing international partnerships, and launching online retail. In October 2023, Kurt Geiger opened its first international stores in Mexico City: one in the Santa Fe business district and one in the Perisur shopping mall, marking the beginning of a Latin America expansion.
