# Pacific Brands

## Overview

Pacific Brands was formed as a consumer goods division of Pacific Dunlop in 1985. Pacific Dunlop's origins trace to the Dunlop Pneumatic Tyre Company, which opened a Melbourne branch factory in 1893. In 2001, Pacific Dunlop sold the division to CVC Asia Pacific and Catalyst Investment Managers; the company was floated on the Australian Stock Exchange (ASX) in 2004. Trading was suspended 28 June 2016 when Hanesbrands completed the acquisition of Pacific Brands for $800 million. Following acquisition, Pacific Brands was rebranded as Hanes Australasia and now operates as a Hanesbrands business unit; the company's website operates as hanesaustralasia.com. ## Products

**Underwear group**: underwear, bras, socks, hosiery, babywear, activewear, outerwear. Key brands: Bonds (core brand; founded 1915 by George Alan Bond; Men's Athletic Singlet/Chesty Bond from 1920; acquired by Pacific Brands 1987), Berlei (acquired 1969; Berlei Sport — Serena Williams global ambassador 2015), Jockey, Explorer, Rio, Bras N Things, Holeproof (acquired 1969).

**Sheridan group**: premium bedding, accessories, towels, and home lifestyle under the Sheridan brand (acquired 2005; includes Actil and Arthur Ellis). First Sheridan Boutique stores opened 1994 (Double Bay and St Ives, Sydney). Sheridan concept areas within Farmers department stores (New Zealand).

**Tontine and Dunlop Flooring**: pillows and quilts under Tontine, Dunlopillo, Fairydown, Crestell, Biozone, Dream-a-way, and Natures Dreams; carpet underlay and hard flooring under Dunlop Flooring and Heartridge brands.

**Former brands (divested)**: workwear portfolio sold to Wesfarmers $180M in 2014 (Stubbies, King Gee, Hard Yakka); footwear/sport "Brand Collective" sold 2014 (Clarks, Hush Puppies, Julius Marlow, Volley, Mossimo, Superdry, Dunlop Sport, Slazenger, Everlast, Grosby, Harrison, Freshjive); Sleepmaker and Dunlop Foams sold 2011; Sleepmaker and Leisure/Fitness (Malvern Star Bicycles) divested separately. Streetwear division of Globe International acquired 2007 (Mooks, Mossimo, Paul Frank, Superdry, Stüssy) — held until divestiture as part of Brand Collective 2014.

## Markets

Hanes Australasia / Pacific Brands holds market leadership positions across Australasian underwear, hosiery, and home textiles. Bonds is the core Australian underwear brand (singlets, underwear, socks, babywear); Berlei is the leading Australian women's bra brand; Sheridan is Australia's leading premium home textiles brand (bedding, towels, home lifestyle). Pacific Brands was one of the largest suppliers to the 2000 Sydney Olympic Games. In 2009 the company exited the majority of local Australian manufacturing (1,850 redundancies) and moved to offshore sourcing as a GFC response strategy.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Pacific Brands / Hanes Australasia's primary SPD relevance runs through Sheridan (home textiles) and Bonds (printed underwear and babywear):

- **Sheridan (premium home textiles — direct SPD application)**: Sheridan is Australia's leading premium bedding and towel brand; its seasonal product line carries full print and pattern direction across bedding sets, duvet covers, pillowcases, and towels. Floral, geometric, abstract, and stripe surface pattern in home textile format is Sheridan's primary creative design output; this is a direct and commercially significant SPD application at scale
- **Bonds (printed underwear and babywear surface)**: Bonds' product range includes printed underwear, printed socks, and babywear with surface print — singlet graphics, babywear animal and character prints, and underwear surface patterns. The Chesty Bond singlet (Men's Athletic Singlet, from 1920) is an iconic Australian brand garment whose ribbed knit surface and branding constitute a recognised surface identity
- **Berlei (bra and lingerie surface)**: Bra and underwear surface design — lace, mesh, printed, and constructed surface materials — forms part of Berlei's product development. Berlei Sport (Serena Williams partnership, from 2015) carries activewear surface graphics direction
- **Bras N Things (lingerie surface)**: Lingerie surface print, lace, and embellishment design
- **Dunlop Volley (footwear surface, pre-divestiture)**: Volley was the official "dress" shoe of the Australian Olympic Team at the 2012 London Olympics (specially designed shoe for team uniform Opening and Closing ceremonies; limited public sale via Big W); footwear surface and graphic design was an active SPD output prior to the 2014 Brand Collective divestiture
- **Streetwear brands (2007–2014)**: Paul Frank (character graphic surface), Stüssy (graphic streetwear surface), and Mossimo (graphic coastal surface) were briefly held under Pacific Brands 2007–2014 before divestiture; during that period, Pacific Brands was a distributor of some of the most recognisable graphic surface brands in Australian streetwear
