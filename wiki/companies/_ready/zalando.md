# Zalando

## Profile Image

![Profile](../../assets/companies/zalando/profile/zalando-logo.jpg)

## Overview

Zalando SE is a publicly traded international online retailer headquartered in Berlin, specialising in fashion, shoes, and beauty across 25 European markets. Founded in 2008 by Robert Gentz and David Schneider — who met at WHU business school alongside the Samwer brothers, whose capital backed the launch — Zalando began as a shoe retailer inspired by US e-commerce company Zappos. The name is derived from the Spanish word for shoes (*zapatos*). The company added apparel in 2010, was listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in 2014, joined the MDAX in 2015, and entered the DAX (as one of 40 expanded components) in September 2021. Revenue grew from €0.15 billion in 2010 to €10.5 billion in 2024. Active users across 25 markets exceed 51 million. In 2025, Zalando completed the acquisition of competitor About You for approximately €1.13 billion.

Zalando has no proprietary aesthetic design direction. The platform's curation is driven by brand partner inventory and algorithmic commercial logic. The company's own-brand design function is not specifically documented in source material. The Bread & Butter fashion festival (2016–2018) represented a brief period of direct engagement with street and youth fashion cultural positioning.

## History

Zalando launched in Germany and expanded progressively across Europe: Netherlands and France (2010), UK, Italy, Switzerland (2011), and further markets through 2022, ultimately reaching 25 countries. From 2013, the company transformed from a pure e-commerce retailer into a digital platform, opening sales via a Partner Program that allows fashion brands and retailers to sell through Zalando with limited platform interference — a model inspired by Chinese digital commerce platforms. The company acquired the Bread & Butter fashion trade fair in June 2015, running it as a consumer-facing "fashion festival" from 2016 before discontinuing it in 2018. Kickz (15 German basketball footwear shops) was acquired in March 2017. Beauty launched as a category in Germany, Poland, and Austria in 2018, with a dedicated concept store in Berlin. The pre-owned resale app Zircle was discontinued in November 2022; a pre-owned peer-to-peer category continues on the main platform. In December 2024, Zalando announced the acquisition of About You (including its Scayle subsidiary) for €1.13 billion; the transaction closed July 2025 and About You became a wholly-owned subsidiary after a merger squeeze-out in November 2025. A partnership with Vestiaire Collective for authenticated luxury pre-owned was announced in May 2026.

## Products

Footwear, women's and men's clothing, activewear, bags, accessories, jewellery, lingerie, kidswear, and beauty. Own-brand labels supplement multi-brand curation. The Lounge by Zalando shopping club offers flash sales events. Physical outlets operate in 12 German cities. The beauty concept store in Berlin offers a regularly updated product selection. Pre-owned fashion is available peer-to-peer on the main platform following the discontinuation of the standalone Zircle app.

## Services

Zalando operates multi-format: online multi-brand retail, Partner Program (third-party brand sales), Lounge by Zalando (flash sales), physical outlets (12 German cities), and a beauty concept store in Berlin. No fashion brand licensing is documented. The Anatwine software company (20% stake acquired 2015, Cheltenham) provides technology enabling fashion brands to integrate their stock and processes with Zalando's platform. Controversial logistics labour practices were publicly documented in ZDF (2012) and RTL (2014) investigations, prompting stated improvements in service provider oversight.

## Markets

Zalando is the dominant multi-brand fashion e-commerce platform in Europe, targeting gross merchandise volume of €30 billion+ by 2025 and long-term capture of more than 10% of the €450 billion European fashion market. The platform competes with national fashion e-commerce operators (e.g., ASOS in the UK) and physical multi-brand retailers across all 25 markets. The combination of direct retail, Partner Program (third-party brand sales), Lounge by Zalando (flash sales club), and physical outlets in 12 German cities gives the company a multi-format market position. The About You acquisition substantially consolidates Zalando's position in European online fashion. ESG commitments include 80% Scope 1+2 emissions reduction by 2025 (78% achieved in 2023) and 40% private-label product emissions reduction (43% achieved).

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Zalando's relevance to surface pattern design operates at platform scale across European fashion distribution:

- **Europe's primary digital fashion marketplace**: As the dominant multi-brand online fashion platform in 25 European markets with 51 million active users, Zalando is the largest commercial distribution channel for pattern-forward fashion in Europe. Every seasonal print collection, graphic-led brand, and patterned apparel range from the brands on the platform reaches European consumers through Zalando. The volume of patterned fashion flowing through the platform annually represents the full spectrum of European print and graphic design in commercial fashion.
- **Own-brand product design**: Zalando's own labels (referenced in source as "multi-brand online shopping including their own brands") require internal or close-partner product design — including print and pattern design for own-label apparel.
- **Partner Program**: The Partner Program allows fashion brands — including print-led and pattern-focused brands — to sell via Zalando with their own inventory and fulfilment, effectively integrating Zalando into the commercial infrastructure of the entire European fashion market. This gives surface pattern design brands a major digital retail channel.
- **Lounge by Zalando**: The flash sale format mirrors the Veepee model — distributing seasonal fashion (including printed/patterned product) at discounted prices to members, benefiting both consumer and brand partner.
- **About You acquisition (2025)**: About You was known for a strong fashion content and influencer-driven positioning, bringing additional fashion brand distribution to the combined group.
- **Vestiaire Collective partnership (2026)**: The luxury pre-owned channel extends Zalando's distribution reach into authenticated luxury fashion — including high-value pattern-designed items (Hermès scarves, printed luxury goods, etc.).
- **Sustainability and private-label materials**: Zalando's stated goal of 25% sustainable (recycled, organic, natural fabric) products — at 10.5% in 2023 — signals ongoing engagement with material sourcing at industrial scale, affecting the textile base on which printed and patterned fashion is produced.
