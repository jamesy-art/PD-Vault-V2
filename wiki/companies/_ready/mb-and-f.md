## Overview

MB&F (Maximilian Büsser and Friends) was founded in July 2005 in Geneva, Switzerland by Maximilian Büsser. The company specialises in small-series, concept-type watches — referred to as Horological Machines (HM series) and Legacy Machines (LM series). MB&F launched its first watch, HM1, in 2007. The company positioned its timepieces explicitly as kinetic art and established the M.A.D. Gallery (Mechanical Art Devices) in Geneva in 2011 as an art gallery context for displaying the watches alongside mechanical art by other artists. In 2024, Chanel acquired a 25% stake for an undisclosed sum; Büsser retained majority ownership at 60%, with Serge Kriknoff holding 15%. ## Products

Two primary collections:
- **Horological Machines (HM series)**: Futuristic concept watches — biomorphic cases, dual hemisphere displays, flying tourbillons, unusual power reserve indications. Notable: HM1 (2007), HM4 Thunderbolt (2010), HM6 (2016, rounded biomorphic case, flying tourbillon, twin turbine winding), HM10 Bulldog (2020), HM12
- **Legacy Machines (LM series)**: More traditionally-proportioned round-cased watches with visible, architecturally composed movements. Notable: LM1 (2011), LM Perpetual (2015, perpetual calendar developed by Stephen McDonnell), LM FlyingT (2019, first ladies' watch, vertical movement architecture, 50° tilted dial), LM Sequential EVO (2022, dual chronograph, GPHG Aiguille d'Or)

## Markets

MB&F operates in the ultra-niche segment of independent Swiss luxury watchmaking, producing small series of concept watches. The M.A.D. Gallery (Geneva 2011, Taipei 2015) positions the brand within an art/design context rather than conventional retail. Awards: GPHG Best Concept and Design Watch (HM4 Thunderbolt, 2010); GPHG Best Men's Watch and Public Prize (LM1, 2012); Red Dot Best of the Best (HM6, 2015); GPHG Best Calendar Watch (LM Perpetual, 2016); GPHG Best Ladies Complication (LM FlyingT, 2019); GPHG Aiguille d'Or — top prize (LM Sequential EVO, 2022). Founder Maximilian Büsser received the Gaïa Prix Entrepreneurship award in 2018.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

MB&F is peripheral to surface pattern design in the conventional sense (textile, print, repeat pattern). Its relevance is as a luxury object design entity whose aesthetic language operates in three-dimensional form rather than two-dimensional surface:

- **Watch case as sculptural surface**: Each Horological Machine is conceived as a sculptural object; the case surfaces, domes, shields, and turbines constitute three-dimensional surface design at a high-precision, high-concept level
- **Futuristic/sci-fi aesthetic**: MB&F's visual language — biomorphic cases, retractable shields, dual-dome displays — has established a distinct luxury product surface aesthetic associated with mechanical art
- **M.A.D. Gallery positioning**: By framing watches as kinetic art within a gallery context, MB&F explicitly connects luxury horology to art and design disciplines — including surface and object design
- **Chanel investment (2024)**: The 25% stake by Chanel brings MB&F into direct relationship with one of the most significant fashion surface design ecosystems; convergence point between watchmaking and fashion brand surface identity
