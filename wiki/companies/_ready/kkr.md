## Overview

KKR (Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co.) is a major global investment firm headquartered in New York City, founded in 1976 by Jerome Kohlberg Jr., Henry Kravis, and George Roberts. The firm manages multiple alternative asset strategies including private equity, credit, real assets, infrastructure, and capital markets. KKR is publicly listed on the New York Stock Exchange (KKR). It is known particularly for pioneering the modern leveraged buyout structure and has historically invested across consumer, retail, and luxury goods sectors. SMCP Group — the French affordable luxury group whose brands include Sandro, Maje, and Claudie Pierlot — was a KKR portfolio holding.
