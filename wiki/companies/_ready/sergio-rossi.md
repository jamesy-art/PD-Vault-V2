## Overview

Sergio Rossi is an Italian luxury footwear brand tracing its origins to 1951, when Sergio Rossi began producing sandals in San Mauro Pascoli (Forlì-Cesena Province, Emilia-Romagna) — the same region as Pollini. In his early career, Rossi crafted sandals during winter and sold them on the beaches of Rimini in summer, as well as to boutiques in Bologna. The first shoes bearing the Sergio Rossi name appeared in the late 1960s, marking the transition from artisanal to branded production. *Vogue* described the brand in the 1960s as having "rapidly become synonymous with Italian quality and classic feminine designs." In the 1970s, Sergio Rossi collaborated with Gianni Versace, producing footwear for his early runway collections. In 1999, Gucci Group (PPR Luxury Division) acquired 70% of shares for approximately $96 million; by 2005 Gucci Group held full control. In 2015 the brand was sold to Investindustrial. In June 2021, Fosun Fashion Group (Fosun International) acquired 100% of Sergio Rossi S.p.A. Paul Andrew was named creative director in July 2024. The Versace collaboration established the brand's capacity to translate high-fashion design direction into footwear surface. ## Markets

Sergio Rossi occupies the luxury end of Italian footwear — positioned alongside brands such as Manolo Blahnik, Jimmy Choo, and other artisan Italian luxury shoemakers. The brand's heritage of collaboration with Gianni Versace in the 1970s and its period of ownership under Gucci Group (1999–2015) established its position in the international luxury fashion ecosystem. Under Investindustrial (2015–2021), boutiques were renovated and menswear expanded. Under Fosun Fashion Group (from 2021), the brand sits within a portfolio that also includes Lanvin, Wolford, and other European luxury brands. Paul Andrew (appointed July 2024), himself a luxury shoemaker, brings direct footwear design expertise to the creative directorship.
