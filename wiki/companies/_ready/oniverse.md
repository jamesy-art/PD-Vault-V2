# Oniverse

## Profile Image

![Profile](../../assets/companies/oniverse/profile/oniverse-logo.jpeg)

## Overview

Oniverse Holding S.p.A. (formerly Calzedonia Group) is an Italian fashion and retail company headquartered in Dossobuono near Verona, Italy. Founded in 1986 by Sandro Veronesi — who previously worked for Golden Lady, an Italian hosiery company — the group initially adopted a franchise-based expansion model that enabled rapid international growth. By 2009 it surpassed €1 billion in revenue; by 2016, €2 billion. In 2017, revenues were €2.314 billion with EBITDA of €489 million. The group officially changed its name from Calzedonia Group to Oniverse on December 4, 2023. As of 2025, the group operates over 5,700 mono-brand stores across 55 countries. Sandro Veronesi holds a degree in economics from the University of Verona and is listed by Forbes among the world's billionaires with an estimated net worth of $1.8 billion (2025).

No group-level creative director is documented. Individual brand creative directions are led by their respective brand design teams; Antonio Marras heads the high-end fashion brand of the same name. Calzedonia's advertising strategy has been built around internationally recognised celebrity campaigns emphasising swimwear and hosiery product aesthetics, creating strong visual brand associations with aspirational outdoor/resort lifestyle imagery. The group's commercial strategy has historically focused on the combination of accessible pricing, broad sizing, and trend-responsive seasonal collections across all brands.

## History

- Established 1986, Vallese di Oppeano (province of Verona); officially launched 1987
- Founded by Sandro Veronesi (previously at Golden Lady, Italian hosiery)
- Franchise-based model from founding; rapid expansion across Italy and internationally
- 2009: Exceeded €1B revenue (Mediobanca ranking)
- 2016: Revenue exceeded €2B
- 2017: Revenue €2.314B; EBITDA €489M
- 2018: Revenue ~€2.3B; EBITDA €478M
- December 4, 2023: Group officially renamed from Calzedonia Group to Oniverse
- 2025: 5,700+ mono-brand stores in 55 countries
- Production facilities: Italy, Sri Lanka, Croatia, Bulgaria, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Ethiopia
- Distribution centres: Oppeano and Castagnaro (Italy), Varaždin (Croatia), São Paulo (Brazil)
- Sandro Veronesi: chairman and majority shareholder
- Sons Marcello, Matteo, and Federico Veronesi involved in business; Marcello = CEO of US operations

## Products

**Calzedonia** — hosiery, swimwear, and leggings; the founding and largest brand by store count; global celebrity campaign advertising (Julia Roberts, Adriana Lima, Gisele Bündchen, Sara Sampaio, among others)

**Intimissimi** — lingerie and intimate apparel; women's underwear, nightwear, and bras; celebrity campaigns (Sarah Jessica Parker among others)

**Intimissimi Uomo** — men's underwear, knitwear, nightwear, and swimwear

**Tezenis** — casualwear, underwear, and loungewear; positioned as a younger, more affordable sub-brand; celebrity campaigns (Rita Ora among others)

**Falconeri** — fine knitwear and cashmere clothing; luxury positioning within the group

**Atelier Emé** — bridal and formal wear

**Antonio Marras** — high-end fashion; the group's luxury fashion brand

**Cantiere del Pardo** — luxury superyachts

## Services

Oniverse operates primarily through direct mono-brand stores (5,700+ stores, 55 countries), with the franchise model central to international expansion from the brand's founding. Production is vertically integrated across seven countries (Italy, Sri Lanka, Croatia, Bulgaria, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Ethiopia), with logistics infrastructure in Italy, Croatia, and Brazil. The celebrity ambassador strategy (Julia Roberts, Sarah Jessica Parker, Adriana Lima, Gisele Bündchen, Rita Ora, Chiara Ferragni, among others) is a consistent marketing tool across Calzedonia, Intimissimi, and Tezenis. The group supports the San Zeno Foundation (nonprofit, educational/vocational training in Europe, Asia, Africa, Latin America).

## Markets

Oniverse occupies a distinctive position in the European mass-market fashion landscape: a specialist multi-brand retailer focused on intimate apparel, hosiery, swimwear, and knitwear, operating an exceptionally large direct mono-brand store network (5,700+ stores, 55 countries) rather than wholesale or department store distribution. The franchise origin model and vertical integration through in-house production (seven countries) gives the group tight control over cost, quality, and speed-to-market in high-turnover product categories. Calzedonia (the founding and largest brand) competes as one of the world's largest hosiery and swimwear specialists by retail footprint. The group's diversification into luxury knitwear (Falconeri), bridal (Atelier Emé), high fashion (Antonio Marras), wine retail (Signorvino), and luxury yachts (Cantiere del Pardo) reflects Veronesi's willingness to extend the group well beyond its core intimate apparel origin.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Oniverse's commercial relevance to surface pattern design is substantial across several of its core brands.

**Calzedonia hosiery and tights** — patterned tights, fishnet constructions, lace-effect hosiery, and printed leggings are commercially central to Calzedonia's product range. The brand operates as one of the world's largest specialist retailers of hosiery and tights, where surface texture, knit structure, and pattern are the primary design and commercial differentiators between product lines. Seasonal pattern direction in hosiery — seasonal lace structures, patterned sheers, printed opaque tights, decorative fishnet constructions — is Calzedonia's primary design activity at scale.

**Calzedonia swimwear** — swimwear is a heavily print-dependent product category. Calzedonia's swimwear line relies on seasonal print direction (floral, geometric, graphic, colour-block, and pattern-led designs) as the core product differentiation mechanism. The brand runs aspirational international swimwear campaigns (Adriana Lima, Gisele Bündchen, Sara Sampaio) that foreground the swimwear surface design as the hero product.

**Tezenis** — casual underwear and loungewear, a category in which print and graphic motifs (character prints, geometric repeat, placement graphics) are primary product signals, particularly at the accessible price point where Tezenis operates.

**Intimissimi lingerie** — lace pattern construction and printed intimate apparel are standard product categories in lingerie retail.

**Falconeri knitwear** — fine knitwear and cashmere involves knit structure design, colour, and texture as the primary surface design vocabulary. Falconeri's luxury positioning implies investment in materials and construction quality.

**Antonio Marras** — Antonio Marras is one of the most artistically ambitious fashion designers in the Italian market, known for rich textile work, cultural references, and pattern-intensive garments. The brand's presence within Oniverse brings a luxury creative fashion design function into the group.
