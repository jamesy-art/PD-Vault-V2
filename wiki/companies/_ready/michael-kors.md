American luxury fashion brand founded 1981 in New York by Michael David Kors; labels include Michael Kors Collection, MICHAEL Michael Kors, and Michael Kors Mens; owned by [[Capri Holdings]] (NYSE: CPRI) since January 2019.

## Overview

Michael David Kors (born Karl Anderson Jr., August 9, 1959, Long Island, New York) launched his women's label at Bergdorf Goodman in 1981. He enrolled at the Fashion Institute of Technology in 1977 and left in 1978 to work at boutique Lothar's on 57th Street, New York, where he became designer and visual display head. In 1990 he launched KORS Michael Kors as a licensee, which went on hold following a 1993 Chapter 11 filing caused by closure of the licensing partner. In 1997 Kors was named the first women's ready-to-wear designer for French house Celine, a role he held until October 2003. First menswear line launched 2002; MICHAEL Michael Kors and KORS Michael Kors lines (footwear, bags, RTW) launched 2004. Michael Kors Holdings Ltd renamed [[Capri Holdings]] Limited (NYSE: CPRI) on January 2, 2019. As of December 2025, Michael Kors and [[Jimmy Choo]] are the two founder-led brands under Capri Holdings. ## Market Position

Michael Kors operates across luxury RTW (Michael Kors Collection) and a broader accessible fashion segment (MICHAEL Michael Kors). Forbes estimated Michael Kors' personal fortune at over $1 billion in January 2014; by that time Michael Kors Holdings had "minted two billionaires" — Silas Chou and Lawrence Stroll. The brand held a strong celebrity client base including Michelle Obama (first term official portrait; State of the Union 2015), Alicia Keys (Barack Obama's inaugural ball 2013), and an extended list of red carpet clients. Kors was a judge on Project Runway (Bravo, five seasons; later Lifetime) from launch, leaving December 2012; returned as guest judge 2016. 40th anniversary show staged on streets of Theater District, New York (2021); 45th anniversary show at The Metropolitan Opera House, Lincoln Center (2026).

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Michael Kors' SPD relevance runs across multiple product categories:

- **Seasonal print direction in RTW**: The MICHAEL Michael Kors diffusion line is a significant commercial vector for seasonal print and pattern in accessible womenswear — dresses, tops, and activewear incorporating floral, graphic, and abstract print direction each season
- **Accessories surface design**: Bags, footwear, and small leather goods carry the Michael Kors surface identity — logo-print jacquard (MK monogram), hardware treatment, material surface (leather, canvas, exotic embossments)
- **Watch face and strap design**: [[Fossil Group]] produces licensed Michael Kors watches; watch face layout, colour, and strap surface treatment carry the brand's surface aesthetic
- **Red carpet sequin and embellishment**: High-profile custom pieces (Emily Blunt 510,000-sequin gown, Met Gala 2019) demonstrate the brand's capacity for couture-level surface embellishment
- **Resortwear print**: The 2009 Tony Duquette infringement suit (Kors allegedly used Duquette's name and images in promoting 2009 resortwear) indicates print and image sourcing for surface design as a documented brand practice
- **Celine creative direction (1997–2003)**: Kors designed successful accessories and critically acclaimed RTW for French luxury house Celine — an earlier chapter in which his design sensibility influenced Celine's surface and accessory language

## Design Direction

Michael Kors' aesthetic is rooted in American sportswear luxe — clean, wearable, sun-drenched resort references filtered through New York pragmatism. The Collection line reaches toward couture embellishment; the MICHAEL line addresses commercial print-driven fashion. Kors has led all creative direction since 1981. The brand operates under [[Capri Holdings]] alongside [[wiki/companies/_ready/versace]] and [[Jimmy Choo]].
