## Overview

MF Brands (formerly Maus Frères, operating as "the Group" in corporate communications) is an independent, family-owned lifestyle company with origins dating to 1902. The group rebranded from Maus Frère to MF Brands in 2020. It describes itself as a "major player" in the lifestyle sector and emphasises its entrepreneurial, independent character as a competitive differentiator. The company is managed for four generations by the Maus family. CEO is Thierry Guibert; Deputy CEO is Alexandre Nodale; Chief Human Resources Officer is Gaelle Petit-Perrin.
