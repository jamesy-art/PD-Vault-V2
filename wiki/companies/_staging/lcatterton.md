## Overview

L Capital was the private equity arm associated with LVMH and Groupe Arnault, investing in consumer and fashion brands globally. In 2016, L Capital (Europe and Asia) merged with Catterton Partners (US consumer-focused private equity) to form L Catterton — a unified global consumer private equity platform backed by LVMH and Groupe Arnault.
