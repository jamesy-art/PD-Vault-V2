## Overview

The Cato Corporation is an American retailer of women's fashions and accessories headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. The company was founded in 1946 by Wayland Cato, who left United Merchants to establish his own business with his sons Wayland Henry Cato Jr. and Edgar Thomas. Five main-street stores were opened in the first year; two more followed in 1947, with earnings of $404,000. The company continued expanding through the 1950s, entering shopping centres. It went public in 1968, took itself private in 1980, then returned to public markets in 1987. Facing bankruptcy risk in the early 1990s, the business avoided insolvency by implementing a discount pricing strategy and updating its inventory. As of January 2016, the company operated 1,372 stores under five names: Cato, Cato Plus, It's Fashion, It's Fashion Metro, and Versona.

## Company Profile

Wayland Cato's background at United Merchants — a major US fabric and apparel conglomerate — provided the founder with supply chain and fashion trade knowledge from the outset. The company's core retail format has consistently targeted the value end of the women's fashion market, with strip mall locations co-anchored by national discounters such as Walmart. The multi-format structure covers different demographic segments: Cato and Cato Plus serve junior, misses, and plus sizes; It's Fashion targets juniors and plus sizes; It's Fashion Metro is the larger format covering men's, big and tall, infants, and toddlers in addition to women's; and Versona represents the "exclusive" apparel and accessories positioning above the core Cato discount tier. The company's NYSE listing (ticker: CATO) reflects its sustained commercial scale across the value fashion market.

## Market Position

Cato occupies the value end of US specialty fashion retail, competing on price accessibility and convenience in strip mall locations. The company targets customers who shop in proximity to Walmart and similar value-oriented national discounters — a demographic prioritising affordable pricing over premium brand positioning. The Versona sub-brand signals an intent to serve a slightly more design-aware consumer than the core Cato customer, within the same strip mall retail ecosystem.

## Products and Categories

Women's apparel (junior, misses, plus sizes), men's apparel and accessories (via It's Fashion Metro), children's clothing (infants and toddlers via It's Fashion Metro), shoes, bags, jewellery, watches, eyewear, matching sets, coats and jackets, dresses and jumpsuits, and home goods. The Versona format focuses on "exclusive" apparel and accessories at a step above the core Cato price tier.
