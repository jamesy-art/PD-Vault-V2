# Ratti

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/ratti/profile/company-logo.png)

Italian luxury textile manufacturer founded 1945 by Antonio Ratti in Como; vertically integrated production of printed, plain, jacquard, and yarn-dyed fabrics for global fashion, design, and upholstery; also produces luxury accessories (ties, foulards, scarves); part of the [[Marzotto Group]]; Borsa Italiana listed; Ratti Archive holds every design produced from 1945 to present.

## Overview

Ratti SpA was founded in 1945 by Antonio Ratti in Como, Italy — the historic centre of Italian silk and luxury textile production. The company operates from a vertically integrated production site in Guanzate, Como Province (50,000+ square metres), covering design, weaving, printing, and finishing in-house. Ratti supplies printed, plain, jacquard, and yarn-dyed fabrics to global fashion houses, design studios, and upholstery clients, and also produces finished luxury accessories (ties, foulards, scarves) under licensed brand arrangements. Ratti is part of the [[Marzotto Group]] and is publicly traded on Borsa Italiana. CEO: Rossella Ceruti. CFO: Lorenzo Zampetti. ## Market Position

Ratti is one of Italy's leading luxury textile manufacturers, supplying printed and woven fabrics to global fashion houses, interior design, and upholstery clients. Market split: Europe approximately 45%, Italy approximately 40%, with the remainder exported to the USA and Japan. As a B2B manufacturer the company does not operate retail, but its output appears in finished luxury fashion products sold globally. The Ratti Archive — a physical and digital catalogue of every design produced by the company from 1945 to present — functions as a primary reference and inspiration resource for high-fashion clients. Partners with the Accademia Costume & Moda (Rome). The Second Life Srl textile waste processing plant and Società Benefit status reflect a stated sustainability positioning within the luxury textile supply chain.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Ratti is among the most directly relevant companies in this wiki to surface pattern design. The company's core business is the design, production, and supply of printed and woven surface patterns on luxury textiles:

- **Printed fabrics (primary SPD output)**: Ratti's printed fabric division produces the repeating surface patterns applied to luxury fashion and upholstery textiles used by global fashion houses. Print design — floral, geometric, abstract, conversational, stripe, and figurative — in silk and silk-blend substrates is the most direct expression of surface pattern design practice at industrial luxury scale
- **Jacquard fabrics**: Jacquard weave embeds pattern into the textile structure rather than the surface — woven repeat structures including damask, brocade, and dobby weaves are distinct SPD categories applied through loom construction rather than printing
- **Ratti Archive (design archive from 1945)**: Physical and digital catalogue of every Ratti design from the company's founding; used by fashion houses as an inspiration and licensing resource — a primary source archive for the history of Italian printed textile surface design. The archive's accessibility to clients positions Ratti as a living design reference institution as well as a production facility
- **Luxury accessories (ties, foulards, scarves)**: Foulard and scarf design is a distinct SPD category — the design of printed silk squares and scarves involves all-over repeat, placement print, border design, and colourway management at a specialist level. Ratti's tie and foulard output applies surface pattern directly to premium accessories
- **Upholstery and interior design markets**: Ratti supplies fabric to interior design and upholstery — extending surface pattern design application beyond fashion into home and contract interior contexts

## Design Direction

As a B2B textile manufacturer, Ratti's design output is primarily driven by client briefs from fashion houses, licensed brand partners, and upholstery design studios rather than by independent seasonal collections. The Ratti Archive serves as a design resource for both internal reference and client licensing. The company's own accessory production (ties, foulards, scarves) requires in-house print design capability for licensed brand execution. Sustainability commitments — GOTS organic certification, FSC-certified viscose, Second Life Srl textile waste processing — form part of the company's manufacturing proposition to luxury fashion clients with sustainability requirements.
