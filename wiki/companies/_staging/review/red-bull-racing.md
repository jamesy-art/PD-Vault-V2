## Overview

AlphaTauri is a premium fashion brand launched in 2016 by Red Bull GmbH as a fashion brand extension of the Red Bull energy drink parent company. The brand takes its name from Alpha Tauri, the brightest star in the Taurus constellation (also known as Aldebaran). HQ: Salzburg, Austria. The first AlphaTauri campaign was released for Autumn/Winter 2018. The brand participated in Berlin Fashion Week and has collaborated with Schoeller Textil AG on proprietary textile technology (Taurex, a patented performance fabric technology). In 2022, three Austrian flagship stores opened (Graz, Vienna, Salzburg) and the first international flagship opened in London's Knightsbridge. AlphaTauri entered the US market at New York Fashion Week in February 2023, timed to coincide with the launch of the 2023 Scuderia AlphaTauri Formula 1 car. The brand became Official Premium Fashion Apparel Supplier of Formula 1 for two years from 2022, and the FIA's first clothing partner for three years from 2024.

## Market Position

AlphaTauri positions itself as a premium fashion brand with a technology-forward proposition, leveraging the Red Bull brand's motorsport, extreme sports, and youth culture associations. The brand occupies a niche between technical sportswear and premium streetwear/lifestyle — neither mainstream athletic nor traditional luxury. Key differentiators: Taurex proprietary textile technology (patented, co-developed with Schoeller Textil AG); the Formula 1 naming rights partnership (Scuderia AlphaTauri 2020–2024); and the FIA official clothing partner status (from 2024). Retail presence concentrated in Austria (three flagships) and London Knightsbridge (one international flagship). US market entry via NYFW February 2023. The brand's F1 and motorsport association provides global visibility through race broadcasting.

## Design Direction

AlphaTauri's design direction combines premium fashion aesthetics with technical material innovation — a positioning consistent with Red Bull's brand values (performance, technology, youth culture). The brand has not made fashion-season print direction central to its documented output; technical textile, 3D construction, and functional innovation are the primary creative differentiators. The F1 association shapes the brand's visual language around motorsport, precision, and speed-derived aesthetics.
