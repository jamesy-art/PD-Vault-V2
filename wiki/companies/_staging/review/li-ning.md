# Li-Ning

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/li-ning/profile/company-logo.png)

Chinese sportswear and sports equipment company; founded 1989 by former Olympic gymnast Li Ning; one of China's largest sportswear brands; revenue 14.46 billion yuan ($2.22 billion) in 2020; withdrew from US market in 2022 after North Korean labor allegations.

## Overview

Li-Ning Company Limited is a Chinese sportswear and sports equipment company founded in 1989 by Li Ning, a former Chinese Olympic gymnast who lit the torch at the 2008 Beijing Olympics opening ceremony. The company is headquartered in China and is one of the country's largest sportswear brands. Revenue reached 14.46 billion yuan ($2.22 billion) in 2020 with net profit of 1.7 billion yuan ($261 million), following a recovery from loss-making years (2012–2014). Li-Ning operates domestically through both company-owned and franchised stores (4,297 retail locations as of March 2007; significant growth since). In January 2010, Li-Ning opened US headquarters and a flagship store in Portland, Oregon, but withdrew from the US market in March 2022 following US Customs & Border Protection product detentions related to North Korean labor in its supply chain. In June 2026, NBA star Stephen Curry signed a reported 10-year, $400 million endorsement deal with Li-Ning.

## Company Profile

Li-Ning Company Limited was founded in 1989 by Li Ning, a Chinese gymnast who won six medals at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics. The company grew rapidly through the 1990s and 2000s, becoming the second-largest sportswear brand in China after Nike and Adidas. Key milestones:

- 1989: Company founded by Li Ning
- 2005: Joint venture with AIGLE (French sports apparel); Li-Ning becomes exclusive distributor of AIGLE products in China for 50 years
- 2006: Revenues US$418 million; profits ~$39 million; 4,297 retail stores; NBA and ATP strategic partnerships
- January 2010: US headquarters and flagship store opened in Portland, Oregon; new logo and "Let the Change Occur" slogan launched (Revitalization programme)
- 2012: Dwyane Wade signed (left Jordan Brand; $10 million deal); as of 2015, Li Ning remained board chairman
- 2013: Revenue RMB 2,906 million (down 24.6% YoY); brand entered a multi-year loss period
- 2014: Projected net loss up to 820 million yuan — third consecutive unprofitable year
- 2019: Revenue 13.87 billion yuan (up 32%); net profit ¥1.5 billion (up 110%)
- 2020: Revenue 14.46 billion yuan ($2.22B), net profit 1.7 billion yuan ($261M, up 34.2%)
- January 2020: Paris menswear fashion show (30th anniversary); capsule collection with Jackie Chan; Dwyane Wade sneaker collaboration
- March 2022: Norway's Government Pension Fund excluded and divested from Li-Ning (human rights violations); US CBP began detaining Li-Ning products (North Korean labor); withdrawal from US market
- June 1, 2026: Stephen Curry signs 10-year/$400M deal with Li-Ning (leaving Under Armour, which he departed November 2025)

## Market Position

Li-Ning operates primarily as a mass-market to mid-market Chinese sportswear brand with growing aspirations toward international positioning. The company competes domestically with Nike, Adidas, and other Chinese brands (Anta, Peak). Its China brand awareness is reinforced by athletic endorsements, Olympic committee sponsorships, and the founder's personal celebrity. The attempted US market expansion (Portland headquarters, 2010; Acquity Group partnership, 2011) did not gain significant traction, and the CBP detentions (March 2022) effectively ended US market activity. The June 2026 Stephen Curry signing ($400M/10 years) is the most significant international endorsement deal in the brand's history and represents a renewed attempt to establish international premium positioning — particularly relevant to basketball footwear and athleisure. The Curry deal also allows Curry to sign other athletes under the Curry Brand banner within the Li-Ning structure.

- **Sports equipment** — badminton, table tennis, and other equipment

- **Golf line** — included in the Curry Brand/Stephen Curry endorsement agreement (from June 2026)

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Li-Ning operates in the sportswear and activewear category, where surface design is applied through branded graphic identity, performance textile finishing, colourblock construction, and signature shoe design.

**Athletic graphic identity** is the primary surface design activity at Li-Ning. The brand's logo mark (stylised "LN" derived from Li Ning's initials) is applied across apparel, footwear, and equipment as the primary surface identifier. The 2010 Revitalization rebrand introduced a new logo and visual identity system, representing a deliberate shift in how the brand's surface mark was presented on product.

**Basketball footwear design** is commercially central: the Dwyane Wade signature shoe line (from 2012) and the Curry Brand footwear (from June 2026) involve graphic design, colourway development, and surface material treatment specific to basketball performance and lifestyle footwear. The Wade line featured branded colourways and limited-edition graphic treatments over its lifespan.

**Paris menswear show (January 2020)** — Li-Ning's 30th anniversary presentation at Paris Men's Fashion Week applied runway-level fashion direction to sportswear product. The Jackie Chan capsule collection represented a cultural collaboration using Li-Ning's surface language in a fashion-forward presentation format. This signalled the brand's engagement with the sportswear-as-fashion (Guochao/China chic) trend, in which Chinese brands explicitly present national identity through graphic and surface design.

**Knitwear quality** — Li-Ning received the "Outstanding Contribution to Quality Standardization Award" in the knitwear division at the Third National Textile Standardization Technical Committee (April 2012), indicating engagement with knitwear textile standards.

**AIGLE products** distributed by Li-Ning in China include technical outdoor apparel where surface treatments (DWR coatings, technical textile constructions) are applied surface design categories.

## Design Direction

No named chief creative officer or head of design is documented in the available source. Li Ning remained board chairman as of 2015. The brand's creative direction in the 2010s shifted from a domestic mass-sportswear positioning toward a fashion-influenced "Guochao" (Chinese national pride) aesthetic, which became commercially significant from 2018 onward. The Paris menswear show (January 2020) was the most prominent expression of this direction. The Stephen Curry deal (June 2026) introduces the Curry Brand as a distinct design identity within the Li-Ning structure.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Li-Ning distributes through a mix of company-owned stores and franchises. As of March 2007, there were 4,297 retail stores. By 2006 revenues were US$418 million. The US market entry (Portland headquarters, January 2010) and Acquity Group US distribution partnership (January 2011) were withdrawn following the March 2022 CBP detentions of Li-Ning products. The AIGLE joint venture (2005) gives Li-Ning exclusive China distribution of a French outdoor brand for 50 years. The company's exclusion from the Norwegian Government Pension Fund of Norway (March 7, 2022) and the CBP detention order (March 2022) over North Korean labor allegations created significant reputational and commercial restrictions in Western markets. Uyghur forced labor allegations have also been documented in reports (Bureau of Investigative Journalism, 2025).
