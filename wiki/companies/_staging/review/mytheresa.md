# Mytheresa

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](329989732_3052084775093630_1153728732888969582_n.jpg)

Listed luxury fashion e-commerce platform. Based Munich, Germany. Founded 1987 as Theresa boutique (Susanne and Christoph Botschen); online store launched 2006. IPO on NYSE January 2021 ($2.2–2.3B valuation). Flagship brand of LuxExperience group (from May 2025) alongside Net-A-Porter, Mr Porter, and Yoox — formed following the April 2025 acquisition of Yoox Net-A-Porter from Richemont. Carries 250+ designer brands; delivers to 130+ countries.

## Overview

Mytheresa.com is a listed luxury fashion e-commerce company headquartered in Munich, Germany. The business originates from the Theresa boutique opened in Munich city centre in 1987 by Susanne and Christoph Botschen, selling designer womenswear. An online store, Mytheresa, was added in 2006, offering ready-to-wear, shoes, accessories, and fine jewellery from more than 250 designers. The business expanded into kidswear (2019), menswear (2020), and lifestyle/home décor (the "Life" category, 2022). In January 2021, the parent company MYT Netherlands was listed on the NYSE with a $2.2–2.3 billion valuation, raising $407 million in issue proceeds. In April 2025, Mytheresa acquired Yoox Net-A-Porter (YNAP) from Richemont; the parent company was renamed LuxExperience B.V. in May 2025. Mytheresa now operates as the flagship brand of LuxExperience alongside Net-A-Porter, Mr Porter, and Yoox. CEO from January 1, 2026: Francis Belin (previously Christie's Asia-Pacific; Swarovski; Richemont), succeeding Michael Kliger, who moved to CEO of LuxExperience.

## Company Profile

Founded as a physical boutique in Munich in 1987; online store launched 2006 with a multi-brand luxury selection. Growth trajectory: ~250 luxury designer brands from launch; kidswear added October 2018/2019; menswear December 2019/2020; "Life" category (home décor and lifestyle) May 2022; Certified Pre-Owned watches (in collaboration with Bucherer) 2023; Fine Jewelry expanded 2023; new 55,000 sqm distribution centre at Halle/Leipzig Airport opened autumn 2023.

Ownership history: Originally owned by the Botschen family. Neiman Marcus Group acquired Mytheresa before filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in May 2020; Mytheresa was carved out of the bankruptcy estate by financial investors and financed via $200 million in shareholder loans before the January 2021 NYSE IPO. Richemont acquired a 33% equity stake in Mytheresa as consideration for the YNAP acquisition (April 2025).

## Market Position

Upper-end luxury fashion e-commerce; positioned as a curated multi-brand platform focused on consistent luxury experience, editorial curation, and global logistics. Competes with Net-A-Porter (now a sister brand within LuxExperience) and Farfetch in the global luxury e-commerce market. The YNAP acquisition — combining Mytheresa, Net-A-Porter, Mr Porter, and Yoox — creates the largest luxury fashion e-commerce group globally. Mytheresa operates in 8 languages (English, German, French, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Korean, Spanish) and delivers to 130+ countries.

## Products and Categories

Ready-to-wear (women's, men's, children's); shoes; bags; accessories; fine jewellery; certified pre-owned watches (via Bucherer, from 2023); lifestyle and home décor (Life category, from 2022). All products sourced from 250+ luxury designer brands — Mytheresa does not manufacture or design its own products. Product selection is editorially curated.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Mytheresa is a distribution channel rather than a design originator. Its SPD relevance is through the luxury brands it carries and the categories it distributes:

**Luxury brand distribution**: The 250+ designers carried by Mytheresa include brands whose print and surface pattern identities are documented elsewhere in this wiki — brands such as Valentino, Prada, Burberry, Dolce & Gabbana, Missoni, and others with distinctive pattern vocabularies. As the principal digital retail channel for these brands in the global luxury market, Mytheresa is a key endpoint in the supply chain of printed fashion goods to the consumer.

**Life category (home décor, from 2022)**: The documented expansion into home décor and lifestyle products in 2022 extends Mytheresa's distribution into the home textiles and surface design product category — including tabletop objects, home furnishings, and lifestyle accessories where surface pattern is a primary design driver. This is a direct extension of SPD-relevant product into the platform.

**Fine Jewellery and accessories**: These categories include items where surface treatment, material finish, and applied decoration (enamel, engraving, texture) are the primary design variables.

**Certified Pre-Owned watches (Bucherer, 2023)**: The watch category introduces products where surface design (dial, case, bracelet) and applied finishing are among the primary product design attributes.

## Design Direction

Mytheresa does not have an internal design direction — it is a curated retail platform. Editorial curation of its product selection, campaign imagery, and website experience constitutes its equivalent of brand design. The Munich boutique origin (Theresa, 1987) gives the brand a specialist fashion retail provenance that distinguishes it from platform-first competitors.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Pure e-commerce platform; no physical retail stores beyond the original Theresa boutique in Munich. Operates through a 55,000 sqm distribution centre at Halle/Leipzig Airport (opened autumn 2023). Delivers to 130+ countries in 8 languages. NYSE-listed (MYT Netherlands, January 2021). No wholesale, licensing, or manufacturing documented. Richemont holds 33% equity stake (acquired as consideration for YNAP) as of April 2025.
