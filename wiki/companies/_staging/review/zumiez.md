# Zumiez

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](zumiez_logo.jpeg)

American specialty retailer of action sports apparel, footwear, accessories, and hardgoods; founded 1978 in Seattle; 726 stores in the US, Canada, Europe, and Australia (as of 2018); target market: young men and women interested in skateboarding, snowboarding, BMX, and motocross.

## Overview

Zumiez Inc. is an American multinational specialty clothing and action sports retailer founded by Thomas Campion and Gary Haakenson in 1978, and publicly traded since 2005. The company is based in Lynnwood, Washington and is led by president and CEO Richard Brooks. Zumiez markets apparel, footwear, accessories, and hardgoods for action sports — skateboarding, snowboarding, BMX, and motocross — to a young adult and youth demographic. As of February 2018, the company operated 726 stores: 608 in the United States, 52 in Canada, 54 in Europe, and 12 in Australia.

## Company Profile

The first store opened in 1978 at Northgate Mall in Seattle under the name "Above the Belt." The company grew through the early 1980s with additional stores at Everett Mall, Tacoma Mall, Alderwood Mall, and Bellevue Square. Expansion beyond the Puget Sound area began in the mid-to-late 1980s, and the corporate name changed to Zumiez at that time. The corporate office relocated from Everett to Lynnwood, Washington in 2012. The company acquired Fast Forward in 2006, Blue Tomato (an Austrian European action sports retailer) in 2012, and Fast Times in 2015. Zumiez was publicly listed in 2005. For 13 years the company ran an annual Couch Tour — travelling to 12 "full stops" at malls with skate demonstrations, live music, and amateur competitions — with 2013 sponsors including Bones Bearings, Adidas, Deathwish, DGK, Neff, and Oakley. A charitable foundation, established in 2002, donated over 180,000 items to 180+ organisations in 2012.

## Market Position

Zumiez operates as a leading specialty retailer for action sports lifestyle brands in the US and international markets through Blue Tomato (Europe) and Fast Times (Australia). The brand's positioning — physically located in shopping malls while cultivating authentic action sports culture through events, demos, and brand curation — distinguishes it from mainstream sporting goods chains. The retail model centres on curating brands with credibility in the skateboarding, snowboarding, and BMX communities rather than carrying mass-market activewear.

## Products and Categories

Apparel, footwear, accessories, and hardgoods for action sports. Clothing includes graphic tees, hoodies, outerwear, and bottoms; footwear covers skate shoes and lifestyle sneakers; accessories span headwear, bags, and small goods; hardgoods cover skateboards, snowboard equipment, and related gear. The Couch Tour sponsors (Deathwish, DGK, Neff, Oakley) indicate the brand profile of the apparel lines stocked.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Zumiez operates in a retail sector — action sports culture — that has one of the most graphically intensive product landscapes in commercial fashion:

- **Action sports apparel graphic culture**: Skateboarding, snowboarding, and BMX apparel is defined by bold, brand-specific graphic design applied to garment surfaces — screen-printed logos, graphic tees with illustrative imagery, sublimated snowboard outerwear, and heavily branded lifestyle apparel. The brands carried by Zumiez (Deathwish, DGK, Neff, Oakley, Adidas skateboarding) all operate with strong graphic surface design identities applied to clothing and accessories.
- **Skateboarding graphic tradition**: Skateboarding has an especially rich history of surface graphic design on decks and apparel — a tradition of illustrative, bold, and often subversive graphics that crosses fine art, illustration, and surface pattern design. Apparel sold at Zumiez carries this graphic vocabulary onto garment surfaces.
- **Snowboard outerwear and sublimated print**: Snowboard outerwear frequently uses full-coverage sublimated print techniques — all-over surface printing that covers jacket and pant surfaces with graphic patterns, colour-blocked designs, and brand-specific visual systems. This is a direct surface pattern design context at commercial retail scale.
- **Headwear and accessories surface design**: Snapback caps, beanies, and accessory items stocked by Zumiez carry brand graphic embroidery and woven patch applications — a surface embellishment context consistent across the action sports accessories market.
- **Blue Tomato (European)**: The acquisition of Blue Tomato in 2012 brings the same action sports graphic apparel model to the European market, extending the distribution reach for graphic-heavy action sports brands internationally.

## Design Direction

Zumiez has no proprietary design direction. The store's aesthetic is defined by the collective graphic identity of the brands it stocks — a curated selection of action sports brands whose combined visual language constitutes the Zumiez shopping environment. The Couch Tour and brand partner events reinforce a community-embedded cultural positioning rather than a store-driven design philosophy.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Zumiez operates through mall-based specialty stores in the US, Canada, Australia, and Europe (via Blue Tomato). No proprietary brand licensing or design operation is documented in source material. The wholesale buying function — selecting which action sports brands to carry — is the primary creative decision-making context at Zumiez, analogous to a gallery's curatorial role in the context of graphic design culture.
