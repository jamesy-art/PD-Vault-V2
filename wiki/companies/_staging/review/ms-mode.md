# MS Mode

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](473133648_1038044981683023_8709987920426607548_n.jpg)

Dutch women's fashion retailer; founded 1964 Rotterdam by Max Abram; 200+ stores in the Netherlands, France, Belgium, Spain, and Luxembourg; focuses on women's clothing in sizes 38–54 at uniform pricing; part of Roland Kahn's Excellent Retail Brands group since 2010.

## Overview

MS Mode is a Dutch women's fashion retailer founded in 1964 in Rotterdam by Max Abram, whose retail business originated from a market stall at Amsterdam's Albert Cuypmarkt. The first permanent store opened in Rotterdam, initially as Mantel Specialist, selling ladies' coats only before expanding to clothing for women of all ages. The brand formerly traded as M&S, a name abandoned to avoid confusion with British department store Marks & Spencer, which uses the same abbreviation. As of the Wikipedia source date, MS Mode operates over 200 stores across the Netherlands, France, Belgium, Spain, and Luxembourg. Since 2010, the company has been part of Excellent Retail Brands, a portfolio vehicle run by Roland Kahn, alongside Coolcat, America Today, and Sapph. Previously, it was owned by Maxeda, which also held the Vroom & Dreesmann and De Bijenkorf department store chains.

## Market Position

MS Mode operates in the mass-market women's fashion segment with a clear size-inclusive positioning: all products are offered in sizes 38–54 at the same price, removing the common retail practice of charging more for larger sizes. This positioning focuses on the plus-size and extended-size women's fashion market in the Benelux and wider Western European retail context. The brand's combination of accessible pricing, inclusive sizing, and pan-European store network distinguishes it within the value-to-mid-market women's fashion category.
