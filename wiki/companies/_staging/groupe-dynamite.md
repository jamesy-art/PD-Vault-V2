# Groupe Dynamite

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/groupe-dynamite/profile/company-logo.svg)

Canadian women's fast-fashion group operating the GARAGE and DYNAMITE retail banners; headquartered in Montreal; listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange since November 2024.

## Overview

Groupe Dynamite Inc. is a Canadian women's fashion company operating two retail banners: GARAGE and DYNAMITE. The group was founded in 1975 with the launch of the first Garage store and formally established as Groupe Dynamite in 1984. Headquartered in Montreal, Quebec, the company employs approximately 6,000 people and operates more than 300 stores across Canada and the United States. In November 2024, Groupe Dynamite completed an IPO on the Toronto Stock Exchange (ticker: GRGD), raising approximately CA$300 million and achieving a market valuation of CA$2.3 billion. The group's target customer is women in the Gen Z and Millennial age brackets.

## Company Profile

The Garage banner launched in 1975; the Dynamite banner was added as the group expanded its retail positioning. The company remained privately held for nearly five decades before its November 2024 TSX IPO — a significant public market event for Canadian fashion retail. In September 2020, Groupe Dynamite sought creditor protection under Canada's Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act (CCAA) as the COVID-19 pandemic forced retail store closures; the company emerged from CCAA protection and subsequently grew its store network and prepared for public listing. The IPO and CA$2.3B valuation reflect the company's recovery and sustained competitive position in the Canadian and US fast-fashion women's retail market.

## Market Position

Groupe Dynamite competes in the fast-fashion and contemporary women's apparel segment. The GARAGE banner targets a younger demographic (Gen Z); DYNAMITE addresses a slightly broader Millennial/Gen Z women's market. With 300+ stores across Canada and the USA, the group has meaningful physical retail scale. The Canadian market is its primary base, with US store expansion a growth vector. The TSX IPO (November 2024) positions Groupe Dynamite alongside Canadian retail peers and increases financial transparency and brand profile in North American retail.
