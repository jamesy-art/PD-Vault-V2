## Overview

Fosun International (復星國際有限公司, SEHK: 656) is China's largest private conglomerate, headquartered in Shanghai. It was founded in 1992 by four Fudan University students — Guo Guangchang, Liang Xinjun, Wang Qunbin, and Fan Wei — with initial capital of 38,000 yuan (approximately 6,000 Swiss francs), initially operating as Shanghai Fosun High Technology Co. to advise foreign investors in China. By 2015, annual turnover reached 78.796 billion RMB (approximately €10.738 billion). ## Company Profile

Fosun is structured across three primary business areas — health, finance, and entertainment/fashion — plus real estate, steel, and mining operations:

- **Health**: Fosun Pharma (39.78%), listed Shanghai Stock Exchange 1998; Luz Saúde (83.63%); Gland Pharma India (86%, acquired 2016, $1.3B); Star Healthcare
- **Finance**: Fosun Insurance Portugal (84.97%), Peak Reinsurance (85.1%), Pramerica Fosun Life Insurance (50%); Hauck Aufhäuser Lampe (sold to ABN Amro 2024, €672M); Ageas stake 9% (sold to BNP Paribas 2024, ~€730M); Banco Comercial Português 17% stake (2016)
- **Entertainment and lifestyle**: Club Med (78.1%); Thomas Cook brand (£11M, acquired November 2019 from liquidation); Cirque du Soleil (20%); Atlantis; Studio 8; Bona film (6.2%)
- **Fashion**: Lanvin (acquired March 2018 — the sole documented fashion holding)
- **Real estate**: Forte (99.15%), The Bund Finance Center; Dalian Donggang (64%)

The group has pursued an aggressive international acquisition strategy. Chairman Guo Guangchang disappeared for four days in December 2015 amid anti-corruption investigations; he reappeared and stated he was questioned about personal matters. Carlyle Group partnership for a China investment fund was established in February 2010. Tsingtao Brewery stake (17.90%) acquired in December 2017 for $844M from Asahi.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Fosun's SPD relevance is entirely indirect — as the owner of fashion brands that produce SPD-relevant products:

- **Lanvin (owned from March 2018)**: Lanvin is one of the oldest active French couture houses. As Lanvin's parent, Fosun's investment decisions directly affect the creative and commercial direction of a significant SPD-relevant fashion brand.
- **Club Med (78.1%)**: Resort hospitality is a consumer environment where textile surface design (bedding, towels, uniforms, poolside textiles) plays a commercial role. Club Med's large global resort network represents a commercial buyer of hospitality textile product.
- **Conglomerate acquisition context**: Fosun's pattern of international heritage brand acquisition is relevant context for the SPD industry — changes of ownership at fashion houses affect creative direction, designer relationships, and licensing strategy.

## Design Direction
