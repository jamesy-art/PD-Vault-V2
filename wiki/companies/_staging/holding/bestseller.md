# Bestseller

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/bestseller/profile/company-logo.png)

Danish privately held family-owned fashion company; founded 1975 by Troels Holch Povlsen in Brande, Denmark; ~9,000 shops worldwide (6,000+ in China); ~17,000 employees; operates ONLY, Vero Moda, Jack & Jones, Selected Homme, SELECTED Femme, VILA, Name It, and several other brands across 70+ markets; parent holding company Heartland owns 29.5% of ASOS and 10% of Zalando.

## Overview

Bestseller A/S is a privately held family-owned clothing and accessories company founded in Brande, Denmark in 1975 by Troels Holch Povlsen. The company provides affordable fashion for men, women, and children, operating across approximately 70 markets in Europe, the Middle East, Canada, and India, with extensive presence in China (~6,000 of its ~9,000 stores). Bestseller employs approximately 17,000 people. The company began with a focus on women's fashion, adding children's clothing in 1986 and menswear in 1988. It is currently led by Anders Holch Povlsen (son of the founder). Through its parent holding company Heartland, Bestseller is the largest shareholder in British e-commerce firm ASOS at 29.5%, and owns 10% of German fashion platform Zalando. Heartland also invested $300 million in German fashion startup About You in 2018.

## Company Profile

Bestseller's multi-brand architecture gives the company distinctive market coverage: separate brand identities address different demographics and price points across the same retail infrastructure. The flagship women's brand ONLY targets young female fashion; Vero Moda is the core women's basic brand (800+ stores globally; Gisele Bündchen was brand ambassador 2007–2008); VILA targets younger women; Mamalicious is maternity wear; Junarose focuses on plus size; Y.A.S, Noisy May, PIECES, Object, Jacqueline de Yong, produkt, and the newest brand JJXX complete the women's portfolio. Joint ventures with ASOS operate Topshop (women's) and Topman (men's). On the menswear side, Jack & Jones is the flagship brand — originally a denim specialty label launched in 1990; it has since expanded to cover urban sportswear, shoes, and accessories, growing to approximately 1,000 stores by 2025. The first Jack & Jones store opened in Trondheim, Norway in 1991; 36 Scandinavian stores existed by 1994. Jack & Jones sponsors Danish boxer Mikkel Kessler, Formula One driver Kevin Magnussen, Danish esports team Astralis, and the Haas F1 Team. Selected Homme targets the contemporary tailoring market with suits and shirts; Only & Sons and LMTD (formerly Outfitters Nation) address younger male consumers. The children's line Name It covers babies and children. Bestseller also holds a 71.29% stake in the variety store chain Normal. A planned 320-metre headquarters tower in Brande (designed by Dorte Mandrup, announced 2017, which would have been the tallest building in western Europe) was shelved in 2020.

## Market Position

Bestseller is one of the largest privately held fashion groups in Europe, with particular strength in the affordable segment across the Nordic markets, Germany, the Netherlands, and the UK, and extraordinary scale in China ("one of the only foreign clothing companies successful at penetrating the middle price range Chinese consumer market"). The Jack & Jones brand alone operates approximately 2,000 retail shops worldwide (270 franchised). Vero Moda runs 800+ stores globally. The Heartland investment portfolio (29.5% ASOS, 10% Zalando, $300M About You) gives Bestseller financial exposure to three of the leading European online fashion platforms — effectively a significant indirect stake in the digital fashion market.

## Products and Categories

Women's casual and contemporary fashion (ONLY, Vero Moda, VILA, Y.A.S, PIECES, and others); maternity wear (Mamalicious); plus-size women's (Junarose); menswear including denim, urban sportswear, tailoring (Jack & Jones, Selected Homme, Only & Sons); children's wear (Name It); joint venture brands Topshop and Topman (via ASOS partnership). Accessories are included across brands. Jack & Jones also carries footwear.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Bestseller's relevance to surface pattern design operates through its multi-brand design infrastructure and the specific aesthetic identity of its core brands:

- **Multi-brand parallel design teams**: Bestseller operates multiple fashion brands simultaneously, each with a distinct design identity and target demographic. This means the company runs numerous concurrent seasonal design processes across ONLY, Vero Moda, Jack & Jones, Selected Homme, VILA, and others — all making independent decisions about print, colour, fabric treatment, and surface design per season at commercial fast-fashion scale. The sheer volume of seasonal apparel collections designed and produced across the portfolio represents one of the largest commercial surface design outputs among European privately held fashion groups.
- **Jack & Jones — denim origins and surface design**: Jack & Jones launched in 1990 specifically as a denim brand. Denim design involves a significant body of surface design technique: washing (stone-wash, acid-wash, enzyme wash), sandblasting, laser printing, distressing, embroidery, embellishment, and paint application are all surface design operations applied to denim fabric. Although Jack & Jones has expanded well beyond denim, its founding product category places it within a tradition of textile surface treatment as brand identity.
- **ONLY and Vero Moda — fast fashion print production**: ONLY (Bestseller's largest brand, targeting young female fashion) and Vero Moda are high-volume women's fast fashion brands producing seasonal collections with print and pattern decisions at commercial scale across European and global markets. These are the brands in the Bestseller portfolio most directly active in conventional fashion print design on womenswear.
- **Topshop joint venture (with ASOS)**: Topshop, operated as a joint venture between Bestseller and ASOS, carries the heritage of one of the UK's most pattern-forward and print-aware high street brands. At its peak under Topshop's original Arcadia Group management, the brand was closely associated with bold print choices, distinctive colour palettes, and fashion-forward surface design in women's clothing. The Bestseller/ASOS joint venture preserves and continues the Topshop brand identity in e-commerce.
- **Selected Homme / SELECTED Femme — contemporary tailoring**: The Selected brands address the contemporary tailoring market — a product category requiring careful decisions about woven fabric pattern (stripe, check, houndstooth, plain) at the level of suiting fabric selection from mills and design of shirting.
- **Vero Moda brand ambassador model**: The use of Gisele Bündchen as Vero Moda ambassador in 2007–2008 represented a deliberate elevation of the brand's image through association with a high-fashion model context — signalling design ambition above the typical fast fashion positioning.

## Design Direction

No group-level design direction documented in source material. Each brand maintains independent design teams with distinct aesthetic and market briefs. Jack & Jones has the most documented external brand identity through its sports and esports sponsorship strategy (Haas F1, Astralis), which implies a young male urban/sports aesthetic as the brand design register. Selected Homme and SELECTED Femme position toward the contemporary tailoring market — cleaner, more minimal design sensibility. ONLY and Vero Moda are fast-fashion responsive to seasonal trend cycles.

## Retail and Licensing Context

Bestseller operates through a combination of brand-owned retail stores, franchise stores (approximately 270 Jack & Jones franchises documented), and wholesale to independent retailers. Total stores: approximately 9,000 worldwide (6,000+ in China; remainder mainly in Europe). The Jack & Jones brand is sold in approximately 2,000 shops. Vero Moda operates 800+ stores. Product is sold through brand-specific stores and through Bestseller-branded multi-brand shops. Supply chain controversies: Cambodian garment factory conditions documented by DanWatch and The Guardian (2017, mass fainting events at Vero Moda supplier factories); Uyghur forced labour connections at China-based supplier (December 2023 European academic report); union-busting allegations at supplier factories (2022, Business & Human Rights Resource Centre).
