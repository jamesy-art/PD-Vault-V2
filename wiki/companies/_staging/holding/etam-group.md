# Etam Group

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/etam-group/profile/company-logo.png)

French family-owned lingerie and fashion group operating five brands across 1,300+ stores in 57 countries, with a stated focus on innovative and creative lingerie.

## Overview

The Etam Group is a French family-owned fashion company, currently led by Pierre and Laurent Milchior. The group describes itself as "one hundred years old, still family-owned and independent," positioning its longevity as a commercial differentiator. It operates in the lingerie, fashion, and swimwear segments through five brands: Etam, Maison 123, Undiz, Livy, and Ysé. The group has an omnichannel presence across more than 1,300 stores in 57 countries.

## Company Profile

The Etam Group is privately held and family-managed. Its five brands address distinct market segments within womenswear, lingerie, and swimwear. Etam is the group's primary namesake brand, with the broadest market reach. Undiz operates as a younger, more accessible lingerie sub-brand. Maison 123 targets contemporary womenswear. Livy and Ysé are positioned as more elevated or specialist lingerie offerings. The group frames its identity around "strong identity and historical know-how" and a "unique relationship with customers" cultivated across its brand portfolio.

The group also operates WeDareLab, described as a "tailor-made acceleration programme dedicated to young lingerie and swimwear brands." This programme positions the Etam Group as an incubator for emerging brands within its core category.

## Market Position

With 1,300+ stores across 57 countries, the Etam Group has significant international retail scale for a family-owned fashion company. Its core strength is in the lingerie and intimate apparel category, where its brands have a documented multi-decade presence in France and pan-European markets. The multi-brand architecture allows the group to address a range of customer profiles within lingerie and womenswear without fragmenting its operational infrastructure.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Lingerie and intimate apparel are print-intensive product categories at every market tier, from commodity basics to luxury. The Etam Group's multi-brand lingerie portfolio — spanning accessible (Undiz) to elevated (Livy, Ysé) — requires seasonal print, lace pattern, and textile surface design decisions across all brands. Swimwear, also within the group's scope, is one of the most print-driven commercial fashion categories. WeDareLab, the group's acceleration programme for young lingerie and swimwear brands, creates a documented pipeline through which new surface pattern approaches in these categories can enter the group's commercial orbit.
