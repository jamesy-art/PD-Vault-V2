# Groupe Artémis

## Profile Image

![pd-profile|160](wiki/assets/companies/groupe-artemis/profile/company-logo.svg)

French private investment holding company; Pinault family vehicle; founded 1992 by François Pinault; HQ Paris; owns [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]] (luxury fashion group), Christie's (auction house), Pinault Collection (Palazzo Grassi, Punta della Dogana, Bourse de Commerce), Artémis Domaines wine (Château Latour), Courrèges (100%, 2018), Giambattista Valli (2017), CAA talent agency (majority, 2023); formerly held Puma 29% (sold Jan 2026, €1.5B to Anta Sports); 100% owned by Financière Pinault.

## Overview

Groupe Artémis is a French private investment holding company, headquartered in Paris, and the Pinault family wealth vehicle. It was founded in 1992 when François Pinault transferred his majority stake in Pinault-Printemps-Redoute (PPR, renamed [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]] in 2013) into the new holding structure. In May 2003, François-Henri Pinault (son of François) assumed the presidency. The group is 100% owned by Financière Pinault. ## Company Profile

Groupe Artémis manages a diversified portfolio across luxury, art, wine, entertainment, and technology:

- **Luxury & Fashion**: [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]] (luxury group: Gucci, Saint Laurent, Balenciaga, Bottega Veneta, etc.); Courrèges (40.9% 2014, 100% 2018); Giambattista Valli (2017)
- **Former Luxury & Fashion**: Puma (29% acquired 2018 from Kering; sold to Anta Sports January 2026 for €1.5B)
- **Art**: Christie's (auction house, 1998); Pinault Collection (Palazzo Grassi Venice 2005/2006; Punta della Dogana Venice 2009; Bourse de Commerce Paris 2021)
- **Wine**: Artémis Domaines — Château Latour Premier Grand Cru Classé (Pauillac, 1993); Domaine d'Eugénie (Vosne-Romanée); Château Grillet (Condrieu); Grand Cru Le Montrachet parcel; Eisele Vineyard (Calistoga); Clos du Tart; Bouchard Père et Fils (restructured 2022)
- **Entertainment**: Creative Artists Agency/CAA (majority stake, 2023, ~$7B)
- **Sports**: Stade Rennais FC (Ligue 1, 1998)
- **Tourism**: Ponant luxury cruises (2015)
- **Media**: Le Point news magazine (1997); Point de vue (2018 consortium); Éditions Tallandier (1999)
- **Technology**: Red River West fund (2017); portfolio companies include 1stDibs, GOAT platform, Bowery Farming, Deezer, Delage
- **Food**: Breizh Café (Brittany-Japan crêperie)

Charitable: €100M pledge for Notre-Dame de Paris reconstruction (2019).

## Market Position

Groupe Artémis does not operate commercially in the fashion market — it is a private holding structure. Its market relevance is as the ultimate controlling shareholder of [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]], the second-largest global luxury group. Through Kering, Artémis influences the creative, commercial, and operational direction of Gucci, Saint Laurent, Balenciaga, Bottega Veneta, and other major luxury fashion brands. Direct fashion investments (Courrèges, Giambattista Valli) and luxury marketplace investments (1stDibs, GOAT) extend this influence.

## Products and Categories

Groupe Artémis does not produce or sell fashion products. Fashion-sector relevance is through holding and strategic direction of portfolio brands — primarily [[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]] and directly held fashion houses (Courrèges, Giambattista Valli).

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Groupe Artémis's SPD relevance is structural and strategic:

- **[[wiki/companies/_staging/trk/kering]] ownership**: As the controlling shareholder of Kering, Artémis is the ultimate owner of Gucci (Flora scarf, GG jacquard, maximalist allover print under Alessandro Michele), Balenciaga, Bottega Veneta (intrecciato weave surface identity), and other luxury houses with significant SPD vocabularies.
- **Courrèges (100%, 2018)**: A historically significant couture house for geometric surface design — André Courrèges's 1960s space-age aesthetic was defined by structured graphic surface pattern and modular white-on-colour composition. Direct ownership gives Artémis stewardship over one of fashion's most iconic geometric print legacies.
- **Giambattista Valli (2017)**: Haute couture house known for exuberant floral and botanical surface design in evening wear — romantic allover floral surface construction is the house signature.
- **Christie's**: Historically significant textiles, costumes, and decorative arts appear regularly at Christie's — creating a secondary commercial market for historical SPD works and archival fashion.
- **1stDibs and GOAT**: Luxury resale platforms distributing pattern-design-rich archival fashion to new buyers.

## Design Direction

Groupe Artémis does not operate a creative direction function. Creative direction is delegated to portfolio brand management teams. The Pinault Collection's engagement with contemporary art signals long-term investment in visual culture and material aesthetics, though no direct SPD application is documented.
