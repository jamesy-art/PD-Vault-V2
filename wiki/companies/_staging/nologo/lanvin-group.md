# Lanvin Group

Shanghai-based luxury fashion holding company; subsidiary of [[Fosun International]]; formerly Fosun Fashion Group, renamed Lanvin Group in October 2021; NYSE listed (2023, US$1.5B valuation); portfolio brands: [[raw/companies processed/Lanvin]], Sergio Rossi, Wolford, St. John Knits, Caruso.

## Overview

Lanvin Group is a multi-brand luxury fashion holding company controlled by [[Fosun International]], a Shanghai-based conglomerate. Fosun International acquired [[raw/companies processed/Lanvin]] — the oldest French fashion house, founded 1889 — in February 2018. Fosun Fashion Group, the Fosun subsidiary managing its fashion investments, was renamed Lanvin Group in October 2021, taking the name of its flagship brand. The group listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 2023, valued at US$1.5 billion. Its portfolio spans European heritage fashion houses and luxury specialist brands. ## Market Position

Lanvin Group positions itself as a multi-brand luxury platform competing with Western-backed holding companies (LVMH, Kering, Richemont, OTB Group, Tapestry, Capri Holdings) in European and global luxury fashion. Its differentiating factor is Chinese capital ownership pursuing European heritage brand acquisition — targeting luxury houses underserved by existing conglomerates. The NYSE listing (2023) represents a rare instance of a Chinese-controlled luxury fashion group trading on a US exchange. The group's brand portfolio spans haute couture heritage (Lanvin), Italian luxury footwear (Sergio Rossi), precision legwear and shapewear (Wolford), American luxury womenswear (St. John Knits), and Italian luxury tailoring (Caruso).

## Products and Categories

Lanvin Group is a holding company and does not directly produce products. Portfolio brand categories (per source):

- **Sergio Rossi**: Italian luxury footwear
- **Wolford**: Austrian legwear, shapewear, and bodywear
- **St. John Knits**: American luxury womenswear (knits, suiting)
- **Caruso**: Italian luxury tailored menswear

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

- **Lanvin heritage surface design**: [[raw/companies processed/Lanvin]] (fashion house) has one of the longest-running surface design traditions in French couture — Jeanne Lanvin's Art Deco embroidery work, the signature forget-me-not blue (used for packaging and brand identity since the early twentieth century), and successive creative directors' print and pattern directions; Alber Elbaz (CD 2002–2015) developed significant seasonal print directions
- **Wolford body surface design**: Wolford's core products (legwear, shapewear, bodysuits) require sophisticated textile construction and surface pattern — printed tights, woven stitch patterns, and body-mapping surface design are category-defining product decisions
- **St. John Knits textile surface**: American luxury knit fashion places knit structure at the centre of surface design — pattern, colour, and stitch construction are primary product design decisions each season
- **Caruso tailoring cloth**: Italian luxury tailoring sources high-quality suiting cloths where weave structure, pattern (herringbone, houndstooth, check), and colour are fundamental surface design specifications
- **Lanvin Lab collaborations**: The 2023 Lanvin Lab initiative — with rapper Future as first collaborator — shows the group supporting brand-licensed cross-market surface design and graphic product extensions under the Lanvin name

## Design Direction

Creative direction is managed independently by each portfolio brand. At [[raw/companies processed/Lanvin]], Peter Copping was appointed Artistic Director from September 2024. The Lanvin Lab initiative (from 2023) signals a group-level strategy of licensing the Lanvin heritage brand for limited-edition cultural collaborations — generating new surface design and graphic product extensions beyond the core fashion calendar.
