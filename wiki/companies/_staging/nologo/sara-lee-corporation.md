# Sara Lee Corporation

American consumer goods conglomerate; HQ Downers Grove, Illinois; originally traded as Consolidated Foods Corporation (renamed Sara Lee Corporation 1985); best known as the parent company that spun off [[Hanesbrands]] Inc. in 2006; prior to that spin-off, held the world's largest branded apparel businesses in underwear, hosiery, and intimate apparel; split into Hillshire Brands (food) and D.E. Master Blenders 1753 (beverages) in 2012; current Sara Lee is a frozen bakery brand (Kohlberg & Company, 2018).

## Overview

Sara Lee Corporation was an American consumer goods company headquartered in Downers Grove, Illinois, tracing its corporate lineage to a Chicago wholesale grocery business (Sprague, Warner & Company, founded during the Civil War). The Sara Lee name derives from a cream cheesecake named by founder Charles Lubin after his daughter Sara Lee Lubin; the business became Kitchens of Sara Lee, acquired by Consolidated Foods Corporation in 1956, which renamed itself Sara Lee Corporation in 1985. By 2005, Sara Lee had operations in 40+ countries and 137,000 employees, selling food, beverages, household and body care, and apparel in 180+ countries. In 2006 Sara Lee spun off its branded apparel (Americas/Asia) business as a separate NYSE-listed company, [[Hanesbrands]] Inc. Simultaneously it sold its European branded apparel business to an affiliate of Sun Capital Partners. In 2012 the remaining food/beverage company split into Hillshire Brands (North American food; acquired by Tyson Foods 2014) and D.E. Master Blenders 1753 (international beverages; now part of Jacobs Douwe Egberts). The Sara Lee brand itself was sold by Tyson to Kohlberg & Company in 2018, forming Sara Lee Frozen Bakery. ## Market Position

During its apparel-holding period (pre-2006), Sara Lee Corporation was one of the world's largest innerwear and hosiery businesses. Adams-Millis (acquired February 1988) was the largest private-label sock and stocking manufacturer in the US (3,000 employees; 1987 sales ~$200M). The Hanesbrands business (underwear, activewear, socks) operated at mass-market scale across the US and internationally. The European branded apparel portfolio (Dim, Playtex, Wonderbra) covered hosiery, bras, and intimate apparel at large scale in the European market. Sara Lee's 2005 strategic decision to divest apparel entirely and "refocus on core food, beverage, and household/body care businesses" culminated in the 2006 spin-off and European sale. After 2006, Sara Lee Corporation had no apparel or fashion operations.

## Pattern and Surface Design Relevance

Sara Lee Corporation's SPD relevance is entirely historical and contained within the apparel holdings divested in 2006:

- **Hosiery and underwear surface design (Adams-Millis; Dim; L'eggs)**: Hosiery surface design — stitch pattern, sheerness, colour, and texture of knitted leg and foot coverage — is a direct SPD category applied to socks, stockings, and tights. Adams-Millis was the largest US private-label producer in this category. Dim (European hosiery, sold 2006) was a leading European hosiery brand; hosiery surface constructions (opaque, sheer, patterned, micro-fibre) represent surface pattern design on the body
- **Intimate apparel surface (Playtex, Wonderbra, Lovable, Berlei)**: Bra, underwear, and intimate apparel surface — lace pattern, embroidery, print, and fabric construction — is a surface design category. These brands held significant European intimate apparel market positions prior to their 2006 divestiture
- **Underwear and activewear surface (Hanesbrands, pre-spin-off)**: The Hanesbrands portfolio (underwear, socks, activewear) carried garment surface design — printed underwear, graphics on activewear, and decorative sock patterns — at mass market scale

Sara Lee Corporation as a corporate entity has held no apparel or SPD-relevant operations since 2006.

## Design Direction

As a conglomerate, Sara Lee Corporation did not operate a central design function; design direction was brand-specific within each business unit. The apparel businesses (Hanesbrands pre-spin-off; Dim; Playtex; Wonderbra) each maintained their own design teams producing seasonal underwear, hosiery, and intimate apparel collections. After 2006, Sara Lee had no design function relevant to surface pattern design.
