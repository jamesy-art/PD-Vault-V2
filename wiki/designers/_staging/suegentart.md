## Overview

Sue Gent is a Somerset-based illustrator, designer, and lecturer whose work spans editorial illustration, surface pattern, and publishing. She has an established client base including the National Trust, English Heritage, BBC History, BBC Countryfile, BBC Wildlife, Waitrose, and SAGA magazine. She also lectures in Illustration at the University of the West of England, Bristol.

## Style and Aesthetic

Gent's work draws from nature, history, folklore, and mythology. Her visual references span medieval woodcuts and illuminated manuscripts, 1960s poster art, Indian matchbox design, Art Nouveau, and Japanese woodblocks — a synthesis that produces illustration with a graphic-historical character. Selected Instagram patterns suggest bold figurative and nature compositions with decorative edge and a controlled palette suited to both editorial and surface pattern applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Gent works in a combined traditional and digital process: hand-drawing with pencil, ink, and brush, then adding colour digitally. This hybrid method supports detailed editorial illustration alongside surface pattern work. She holds a first-class honours degree in Art and Visual Culture from UWE Bristol.

## Markets and Clients

Documented client credits include: National Trust, English Heritage, Quarto Publishing, Orion Publishing, SAGA magazine, Joanne Harris, BBC History, BBC Countryfile, BBC Wildlife, Homes & Antiques, Waitrose, Popshot Magazine, Burn the Curtain, Llewellyn Books, Old Dungate Press, Boneshaker Magazine, History Revealed Magazine, and Wildscreen Festival. Her surface pattern output includes a documented Leopard wallpaper design. Selected placement images suggest editorial book and magazine application contexts.

## Career Path

Gent holds a first-class honours degree in Art and Visual Culture from UWE Bristol. Prior to her illustration career she worked in film — producing Radiohead's multi-award-winning 'There, There' music video and working with Bolexbrothers and Collision Films on animated short films. She now combines freelance illustration with lecturing at UWE Bristol. Awards: 2025 World Illustration Awards (longlisted); 2021 3x3 International Illustration Awards (Merit Winner); 2019 3x3 International Illustration Awards (Honourable Mention).
