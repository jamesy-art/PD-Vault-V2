## Overview

Sian Downey is a surface pattern designer and illustrator based in Toowoomba, Queensland, Australia, creating joyful, whimsical designs suited to fabrics, clothing, children's products, and stationery. Designs and illustrations are available for licensing. She acknowledges the Jarowair and Giabal people as traditional custodians of the land on which she works.

## Style and Aesthetic

Self-described as "sweet and quite whimsical," the work aims to bring colour and sunshine to everyday products. Selected Instagram pattern images — 8 chosen from 50 reviewed — suggest bright, illustration-led nature motifs developed into repeating surface designs, with upbeat colour palettes and accessible, warm visual language suited to lifestyle and children's markets.

## Techniques and Tools

Illustration-led surface pattern design with repeat construction. Background in Fashion Design and practical industry experience across fabric retail and wholesale (Spotlight, fabric agents). Comes from a multi-generational creative family, with skills learned from mother, grandmother, and aunt.

## Markets and Clients

Fabrics, clothing, children's products, stationery, art prints, greeting cards, phone and tech cases, and digital downloads listed on the website. Open for licensing collaboration via direct email. Designs are self-described as suitable for fabrics, clothing, children's products, and stationery.

## Career Path

Formally trained in Fashion Design. Worked in the fabric industry across retail (Spotlight) and wholesale (fabric agents) before focusing on surface pattern design. Now works from home in Toowoomba, Queensland. Background in sewing informs understanding of how fabric designs function in use.

## Pattern Focus

Selected pattern images suggest bright, illustrative nature-inspired motifs in upbeat colour palettes. Work spans all-over repeat structures suited to fabric printing. No placement images selected; the website shop covers clothing, fabric, wallpaper, stationery, art prints, and phone cases as documented application contexts.
