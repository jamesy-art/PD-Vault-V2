## Overview

Anelia Madjarova is a surface pattern designer and watercolour illustrator based in Sofia, Bulgaria, working under the handle @annelyblooms. Her Spoonflower catalog spans 638 fabric designs and 607 wallpaper designs — output that places her among the more prolific independent designers on the platform. She also sells through Etsy and maintains a secondary Instagram account at @redclovermeadow. Custom resizing and colorway changes are offered directly to buyers via email.

## Style and Aesthetic

Floral-led across a broad stylistic and tonal range. The Spoonflower bestseller list is anchored by Art Deco–styled florals — "Art Deco stylised flower" leads with 221 favourites — alongside wild flower collages (117 favourites) and large elegant decorative designs (62 favourites). The catalog also includes cottage garden florals, botanical wilds, ditsy lawns, abstract flowers, and block print motifs. Colorway variants are offered across most core motif families.
