## Overview

Latifa Righi is an Algerian surface pattern designer and illustrator working under the Jasmine Blooms Designs studio name. She produces colourful, commercially oriented repeat patterns for licensing across textiles, home décor, stationery, apparel, and lifestyle products. The studio offers three service modes: non-exclusive licensing, full-rights buyouts, and custom design development.

## Style and Aesthetic

Jasmine Blooms Designs is positioned around vibrant, joy-forward patterns described as "modern designs crafted with love, drenched in joyful color." Selected Instagram images suggest a portfolio of colourful botanical and floral patterns with strong colour saturation. The work is designed with "versatility, color, and commercial appeal in mind," targeting brands seeking distinctive, commercially readable artwork.

Primary digital tool is Procreate.
