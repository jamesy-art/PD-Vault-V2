## Overview

Joana Mattos is a Brazilian surface pattern designer operating under the name Studio Joana Mattos. She has professional experience at Brazilian fashion and textile companies — M.Officer, Midas Textil, and EXCLUSIVA TÊXTIL — and has run her own studio continuously since 2019. Her Instagram positions her work within the swimwear and womenswear markets, drawing on the visual energy of Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram patterns suggest tropical and botanical compositions, warm coastal colour palettes, and illustrative print work suited to womenswear and swimwear. Her stated aesthetic references Rio's energy alongside São Paulo's elegance — a Brazilian design sensibility that positions her work at the intersection of coastal vitality and commercial refinement.

## Techniques and Tools

Mattos works across watercolour, Copic Markers, Procreate, Adobe Illustrator, and Photoshop, with additional skills in drawing, Estamparia (print production), and surface pattern design. Her toolkit spans traditional media and digital production.

## Markets and Clients

Mattos's documented employer experience spans Brazilian fashion and textile manufacturing. M.Officer is a Brazilian contemporary fashion brand; Midas Textil and EXCLUSIVA TÊXTIL are Brazilian textile producers. Her current independent studio practice is positioned toward swimwear and womenswear, based on her Instagram bio. No placement images were selected.

## Career Path

Mattos has worked in surface pattern design since 2019. Employment history includes roles at M.Officer (Jan–Jul 2019), EXCLUSIVA TÊXTIL (Jul–Oct 2021), and Midas Textil (Jan–Aug 2022). She has run Studio Joana Mattos as an independent studio from January 2019 to present. She is fluent in English.

Selected patterns suggest illustrative print work suited to womenswear and swimwear — tropical motifs, botanical arrangements, and coastal-inspired compositions. The body of selected work indicates a warm-palette decorative print practice oriented toward the Brazilian fashion market.
