## Overview

Andreea Robescu is a multidisciplinary visual artist based in Spain, working in bold, experimental illustration and mixed-media. Her practice has been developed in close collaboration with brands and creative teams, adapted across image, product, motion, and spatial contexts. She describes her Instagram presence as "art & illustration, with a twist of chaos" and has built an organic following of over 12,000. Open to commissions and collaborations.

## Style and Aesthetic

Robescu's visual language is bold, experimental, and deliberately chaotic — combining drawing, collage, typography, and found photographic material. The result is a mixed-media practice that fuses analog and digital techniques to produce distinctive contemporary visual identities. Selected Instagram patterns suggest graphic, mark-driven compositions with expressive line quality and strong surface tension. The work sits closer to experimental illustration applied to surfaces than to conventional repeat pattern design.

## Techniques and Tools

Works across drawing, collage, typography, and found photographic material; combines analog and digital methods throughout. Develops self-initiated projects exploring scale, motion, and materiality alongside commissioned client work. Portfolio hosted on Cargo.

## Markets and Clients

Work has been applied across brand campaigns, product collaborations, and physical/spatial environments. Robescu describes her practice as flexible across "image, motion, object, and spatial applications." Selected placement images from Instagram suggest context-specific applied visual work. Open to commissions and collaborations (contact: hi@andreearobescu.com).
