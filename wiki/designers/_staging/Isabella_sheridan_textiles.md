  About Me


   As a textiles designer specializing in interior design, my expertise lies in screen printing, digital printing, laser cutting, and dyeing. I'm passionate about creating bold and exciting designs that incorporate large scales and a maximalist aesthetic. My focus is on crafting visually striking and engaging textiles that bring a sense of fun and vibrancy to interior spaces. I aim to push the boundaries of traditional textile design with innovative techniques and a keen eye for impactful, eye-catching patterns and colours. Botswana Safari Project


  This project explores Botswana Safaris, aiming to raise awareness about
  endangered animals by creating statement pieces. The focus is on high-end
  luxury safari lodges in England, bringing the safari experience closer to
  people. The project uses digital and print interior design, with a maximalist
  style using bold colours and large scales. Gilded Dreams Project Tropical
  Gardens Project


   This project is based of my local area Alum Chine  I want to share the beauty of it with others. Alum Chine captures the calming beach essence as well as the green natural element in the tropical garden. I wanted to incorporate these elements through the techniques screen print and digital print. These designs are based for a high end spas to create calmness and relaxation.


  This project has explored the 1920 era and 'The Great Gatsby'.  My project
  'Gilded Dreams' conveys the rise of wealth and high income in the 1920s
  creating the illusion of the American Dream. The word 'gilded' means something
  covered in gold - ‘gilded age’. The gilded age was in the 1900s however, the
  emphasis on glamour and luxury increased in the 1920s. The roaring 20s
  significantly changed the world, especially in America. I have taken this
  period and created a luxury interior collection focusing on maximalism within
  the Art Deco style. I have used the processes of screen printing, digital
  print, collage, laser and UV Print to create a collection of work. As
  sustainability is an important factor within the textiles industry,  my work
  has been focused on this using recycled , second hand and deadstock fabrics.

## Overview

Isabella Sheridan is a UK-based textile designer specialising in bold, maximalist interior textiles. Holding a Textile BA (Hons) degree, she applies screen printing, digital printing, laser cutting, UV print, collage, and dyeing to create large-scale statement pieces for interior contexts. Her practice incorporates a sustainability focus through the use of recycled, second-hand, and deadstock fabrics.

## Style and Aesthetic

Sheridan's aesthetic is maximalist and high-impact — large scales, bold colours, and visually striking pattern compositions. Key projects span wildlife-inspired exotica (Botswana Safari), 1920s Art Deco glamour (Gilded Dreams), and tropical botanical calm (Tropical Gardens, based on her local area of Alum Chine). Selected Instagram patterns suggest richly detailed, bold-scale designs built for interior application rather than apparel.

## Techniques and Tools

Screen printing, digital print, collage, laser cutting, UV print, and dyeing — with each project deploying a combination of processes. Demonstrated capability across both traditional print craft and digital production methods. Sustainability-conscious material selection throughout.

## Markets and Clients

High-end interior textiles. Projects position the work across luxury safari lodge contexts (Botswana Safari — statement pieces for high-end English lodges), luxury spa environments (Tropical Gardens), and upscale residential interiors (Gilded Dreams, Art Deco framing). No commercial client history documented; work appears to be student/graduate project-based (unverified).
