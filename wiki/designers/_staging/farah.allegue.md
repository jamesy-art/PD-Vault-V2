## Overview

Farah Allegue is a Barcelona-based illustrator, pattern maker, and creative director working across Barcelona, Montreal, and Tunisia. Her practice spans surface pattern design, illustration, brand communication, and stage design, with a client roster across Europe and North America. She also serves as art director at @jigo.atelier.

## Style and Aesthetic

Allegue's work is characterised by bold outlines and a vivid colour palette with strong floral tendencies. Selected Instagram images suggest all-over repeat compositions combining dense floral motifs with lively linework — energetic but structured. The overall aesthetic is colourful and celebratory, described by the designer as "colorful and joyful."

## Techniques and Tools

Illustration and colour theory are her declared primary skills. Her role as art director at @jigo.atelier indicates experience in creative direction alongside pattern-making. She lists communication as a further professional skill, consistent with her multi-sector client base.

## Markets and Clients

Her client base covers advertising and communication (Cartier, Mural Festival, Goethe Institut Tunis, Piknik Elektronik MTL, Hivos), fashion and lifestyle brands (Doc Cotton, Inkbox, Bosky, Daniele Henkel, Le Cartel clothing, Oshlag, Outline MTL), book publishing (Hachette jeunesse, Éditions XYZ, KO Éditions, Les Éditions La Souris Qui Raconte), and editorial (Urbania). She has also worked in stage design. Available for commissions worldwide.

## Pattern Focus

Floral-tendency repeat patterns with bold outlines and vivid, joyful colour. Selected patterns suggest dense all-over compositions rather than tossed or geometric arrangements. Work spans pattern design and illustrative commissions across print, branding, editorial, and stage contexts.
