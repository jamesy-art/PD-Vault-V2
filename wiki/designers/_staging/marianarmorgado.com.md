## Overview

Mariana Morgado is a Portugal-based fashion and textile designer with close to a decade of experience across creative industries. Her practice combines a background in fashion and craft with a personal artistic path exploring watercolour paintings translated into fine art prints, digital clipart, and seamless patterns.

## Style and Aesthetic

Morgado draws on contrasts — softness and strength, movement and structure, simplicity and detail — and is drawn to imagery conveying identity, emotion, elegance, and power. Her aesthetic reflects deep influences from fashion and nature, translated into painterly surface work.

## Techniques and Tools

Watercolour painting as primary medium, digitised into seamless patterns, fine art prints, and clipart for multi-format use.

## Markets and Clients

Home décor, stationery (including wedding invitations), and crafting products are documented application areas. Open to commissions and special collaborations.

## Career Path

Close to a decade of experience working across various design fields with a strong emphasis on craftsmanship. Transitioned toward a personal artistic direction developing watercolour paintings for surface design and fine art output.

## Pattern Focus

Seamless patterns derived from watercolour paintings. Design philosophy emphasises originality and authenticity, with patterns intended for home decoration, event stationery, and handmade product design.
