## Overview

Chloë Turner is a UK-based freelance illustrator and greeting card designer creating colourful, trend-led artwork across florals, typography, characters, and surface pattern. Previously designing full-time at [[John Lewis]], [[Paperchase]], Moonpig, Urban Outfitters Home, Paper & Cloth, and Caroline Gardner, she now works independently with brands and retailers. A wholesale launch at [[Top Drawer]] Spring 2027 is in active preparation.

## Style and Aesthetic

Turner's work is characterised by playful colour, layered illustration, and strong trend awareness. Her practice spans floral repeats, typographic motifs, and character-led compositions with a warm, commercially polished finish consistent with the greeting card and gifting markets she has served. Selected Instagram images include both flat pattern work and product placement shots, suggesting a portfolio built for commercial deployment across print and licensing.

## Techniques and Tools

Illustration-based practice producing print-ready artwork for greeting cards, stationery, and licensed product. The aesthetic balances a hand-informed sensibility with clean digital execution suited to commercial production workflows.

## Markets and Clients

Deep track record across UK retail and gifting: [[John Lewis]], [[Paperchase]], Moonpig, Urban Outfitters Home, Paper & Cloth, and Caroline Gardner (most recent in-house role). Now operating independently with a focus on wholesale and brand collaboration. Active Top Drawer positioning signals trade-facing ambition.

## Career Path

Career began with sustained in-house design roles at major UK greeting card and lifestyle brands. Following her most recent full-time position at Caroline Gardner, Turner transitioned to independent practice, building a wholesale body of work ahead of her Top Drawer debut.

Florals, typography, characters, and trend-led motifs. Placement images indicate product-ready output across card and print formats. Palette is bright and commercially accessible.
