## Overview

Heather Zhou is a graphic artist and designer based in Shenzhen, China, working across illustration, surface design, and multi-disciplinary creative projects. Her practice centres on translating emotional and sensory experience into visual form, with work described as blending "poetry, playfulness, and order." She is open to worldwide collaborations including art licensing and artist partnerships.

## Style and Aesthetic

Zhou's visual language navigates geometry, organic form, and emotional resonance. Selected Instagram images suggest a range from structured stripe patterns and geometric compositions to expressive floral studies and character-led illustration. Work is characterised by precise execution and a thoughtful, layered quality. Collections organised around themes of nature, lifestyle, and emotion.

## Techniques and Tools

Graphic art and multi-disciplinary creative projects. Develops ideas in phases—recording concepts at various stages of completion and selecting those to develop when timing is right. Background rooted in books, music, and fictional narrative from childhood; technique refined over years of practice.

## Markets and Clients

Works across brand, cultural, and corporate sectors. Documented clients include Luckin Coffee, Alipay, ByteDance, CGTN, China Minsheng Bank, China Post, China Publishing, iFeng News, NetEase, Sohu, Tencent, Visual China Group, Nanshan Museum, and Geometry. Available for custom illustration, visual design, concept development, art licensing, and creative consultancy.
