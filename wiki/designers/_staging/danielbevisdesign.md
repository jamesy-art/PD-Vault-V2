## Overview

Daniel Bevis is a UK-based surface pattern designer and illustrator working under the brand Daniel Bevis Design. Self-taught through the Adobe Creative Suite and supplemented by Skillshare and YouTube, his practice has evolved from graphic art apparel into surface pattern design. He sells work through Contrado UK, [[Spoonflower]], and Redbubble, and offers designs for freelance licensing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Daniel's design sensibility has shifted from early influences in gaming, pop culture, and Japanese animation toward a broader love of patterns drawn from nature, colour, and everyday observation. Selected Instagram patterns suggest a diverse output — organic natural motifs alongside bold graphic compositions — unified by confident colour choices. His Instagram bio explicitly states no AI use.

## Techniques and Tools

Adobe Creative Suite, self-taught. Vector and digital illustration. Supplementary learning via Skillshare and YouTube.

## Markets and Clients

Print-on-demand via Contrado UK, [[Spoonflower]], and Redbubble. Available for freelance project collaboration and licensing. Womenswear listed as a design category.
