## Overview

Marina Ellen Simillide is a surface pattern designer based in Cyprus operating as Studio Sunshine. She holds a BA (Hons) in Fashion & Textile Design from UWE Bristol, specialising in printed textiles. She has been working as a freelance designer for several years, collaborating with fashion and lifestyle brands. The studio positions itself as a licensing source for vibrant, trend-aware print designs across fashion, home decor, and accessories.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest bold, high-colour dopamine-led compositions. The studio's stated positioning is "playful, fun, and bursting with colour." Designs are described as distinctly on-trend and designed to make brand products stand out. The aesthetic sits within the dopamine dressing and maximalist print movements — bright florals, graphic all-overs, and colour-saturated surface patterns.

## Techniques and Tools

Surface pattern design with illustration. Trained in printed textiles at UWE Bristol. Offers custom design alongside ready-to-license collections and direct design purchases with exclusive rights. Licensing terms include unlimited production runs on physical products.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion, home decor, and accessories. Designs sold with exclusive rights for physical product production including clothing, homeware, stationery, accessories, and commercial merchandise. Targets fashion and lifestyle brands.
