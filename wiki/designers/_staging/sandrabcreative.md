## Overview

Sandra Botero is a US-based surface pattern designer working under the brand Sandra B Creative. After 25 years teaching Spanish and art, she transitioned to surface pattern design in 2016, building a portfolio of bold, expressive patterns suited to textiles, home decor, and product licensing. She also runs the fashion label @heroicouture and offers designs through Spoonflower and La Galerie.

## Style and Aesthetic

Bold, high-colour patterns drawing on Hispanic heritage and a wide pop culture vocabulary — comics, sci-fi, fantasy, anime, and gaming. Selected Instagram images suggest vivid florals and graphic repeat compositions with strong, expressive colour. Described by collaborators as producing "pretty and funky florals," the work carries a playful, character-rich sensibility.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital pattern design with print-ready output. A background in hand-sewn garment construction — having made all samples herself during her fashion line — informs a practical understanding of how pattern applies across product types.

## Markets and Clients

Home decor and textile markets. Designs available for licensing through Spoonflower and La Galerie (@legaleriste). Fashion commissions taken on a one-on-one basis rather than by seasonal collection. Open to commissions per her website.

## Career Path

Taught Spanish and art for 25 years. In 2016, launched a fashion design side project — creating original garments with self-designed fabrics and hand-sewn samples. Surface pattern design became her primary creative focus, leading to the establishment of sandrabcreative.com and the ongoing fashion label @heroicouture.

## Pattern Focus

Selected Instagram images suggest bold botanical florals and graphic repeat patterns with vivid, expressive colour. Hispanic heritage is a cited influence. Pop culture references — comics, sci-fi, anime, gaming — feed the distinctive, playful edge of the work.
