## Overview

Jun Shu is a surface pattern designer operating under the studio name Jun Shu Create, based in Abu Dhabi after nearly a decade in Spain and Switzerland. Born and raised in China, she studied traditional Chinese painting and calligraphy from childhood. She holds a university degree in journalism and spent over a decade working as a translator in the non-profit sector before returning to painting and establishing her design studio. Her work targets fashion textile, wallpaper, and home decor markets, with both non-exclusive and exclusive print portfolios available for licensing or purchase.

## Style and Aesthetic

Jun Shu's practice is rooted in florals and nature, blending hand-painted sensibility with a considered modern aesthetic. The work is frequently described as calming, elegant, and light-filled — reflecting influence from traditional Chinese painting, global travel, and close attention to natural beauty.

## Techniques and Tools

Jun Shu's process combines hand-painted origins with digital refinement. Her background in traditional Chinese painting and calligraphy informs the delicate quality of her mark-making. She offers non-exclusive and exclusive print portfolios, and accepts custom artwork commissions tailored to brand style.

## Career Path

Jun Shu grew up in China, where her father introduced her to illustrated fairy tale books and encouraged study of traditional Chinese painting and calligraphy. She pursued journalism at university and spent over a decade as a translator in the non-profit sector, living extensively across multiple countries. After becoming a mother of two, she returned to painting and founded Jun Shu Create. She has since relocated to Abu Dhabi following years in Spain and Switzerland.

## Pattern Focus

Floral and botanical patterns in a calming, elegant register. Hand-painted mark quality combined with modern digital sensibility. Motifs drawn from nature — flowers, foliage, botanical forms — in light-filled palettes. Suited to womenswear, wallpaper, and home decor.
