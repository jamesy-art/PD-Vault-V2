manual_about_raw: >-
  Hello, I am a surface designer, maker and illustrator. My creative process
  begins by hand: I cut the basic shapes of each design from paper with
  scissors, then add color and texture digitally. This unique combination of
  craftsmanship and digital design results in a powerful visual language that
  reflects my signature style. From tapestries and artprints for my Etsyshop to
  patterns and designs available for licensing across a wide range of surfaces,
  every creation is made to bring character, color, and joy into everyday life.
  Hello,
   I’m Sabeth Elberse — an illustrator, surface designer, and maker from Utrecht, the Netherlands. Started as a Graphic designer, now creating bold, colorful designs inspired by imperfect shapes, playful compositions, and a love for handmade craftsmanship. Featured o.a. in Uppercase magazine and the Surface pattern guide of 2025 and as a finalist of the Etsy design Awards in '23.   From my home studio, I design artwork for a variety of surfaces and applications, ranging from textiles and stationery to editorial illustration and home décor. My creative process combines traditional techniques with digital design, always beginning by hand. Using scissors and black or white paper, I intuitively cut shapes and compositions without overthinking the outcome. These handmade paper cutouts — and sometimes even the leftover scraps — become the foundation for my final designs.

  Afterward, I refine the shapes digitally into vibrant vector artwork, adding
  color, texture, scale, and detail while preserving the movement and energy of
  the original cut paper. The result is a distinctive visual language that feels
  bold, organic, playful, and full of character. Birds often find their way into
  my work as well — quietly slipping into patterns and compositions along the
  way.


  Growing up on a farm in a highly creative family shaped the artist I am today.
  I have always been drawn to the simplicity and imperfection of working with
  paper and scissors. There is something freeing about this process: the
  unexpected edges, spontaneous forms, and tactile quality often lead to the
  most interesting results.


  My work is strongly inspired by mid-century modern graphic design, combining
  abstract forms, vibrant colors, retro influences, and graphic patterns. These
  elements translate beautifully into surface pattern design, fabrics,
  stationery, editorial projects, and art prints.


  In my Etsy shop, you’ll find products featuring my designs, including large
  printed tapestries and art prints. I love seeing my work translated onto
  fabric — it brings the scale, texture, and color fully to life.


  My designs from Sabeth Elberse Studio are also available for licensing and
  creative collaborations within the home décor, textile, stationery, and
  lifestyle industries. Looking for expressive patterns, bold artwork, fresh
  imperfect lines, or colorful retro-inspired designs with texture and
  personality? You are in the right place.

   (Sabeth Elberse Studio)

## Overview

Sabeth Elberse is a surface designer, maker, and illustrator based in Utrecht, the Netherlands, working as Sabeth Elberse Studio. Her practice is built around hand-cut paper techniques: she intuitively cuts shapes from paper without a predetermined plan, then refines the results digitally into vibrant vector artwork. She began as a graphic designer and developed this handmade-to-digital methodology over time. She is featured in Uppercase magazine and the Surface Pattern Guide 2025, and was a finalist in the Etsy Design Awards 2023.

## Style and Aesthetic

Bold, graphical, and colourful with a mid-century modern vibe. Compositions are characterised by abstract forms, vibrant colour, retro influences, and organic shapes derived from the paper-cutting process. Birds appear frequently in her work, often entering patterns organically during the cutting process. Selected images — including cutout garden compositions, folk art/lace patterns, and botanical flower and bird arrangements — reinforce a distinctive visual language that is bold yet humanly imperfect.

## Techniques and Tools

Process begins entirely by hand: shapes cut intuitively from black or white paper using scissors, with offcuts and scraps often incorporated. These paper originals are then digitised and refined into vector artwork with added colour, texture, scale, and detail. The final output retains the movement and organic energy of the hand-cut source material.

## Markets and Clients

Sells tapestries and art prints via an Etsy shop (SabethElberseStudio). Designs available for licensing across home décor, textile, stationery, and lifestyle surfaces. Selected pattern images include application to mugs, teapots, and textile formats, indicating range across kitchenware and decorative textile.

## Career Path

Started as a graphic designer. Developed a distinctive hand-cut paper practice from a home studio in Utrecht, growing up in a highly creative family on a farm. Press recognition via Uppercase magazine, Surface Pattern Guide 2025, and Etsy Design Awards finalist 2023.

## Pattern Focus

Paper cut-out based surface patterns with bold colour and mid-century modern graphic character. Selected patterns include folk art and lace-style compositions, botanical flower and bird arrangements, and kitchenware-applied patterns (coffee and teapot series). Designs are structurally graphic while retaining the tactile irregularity of handmade origins. No placements selected — pattern work is the primary output.
