## Overview

Milly Vine is a UK-based textile designer and recent graduate, holding a BA (Hons) Textile Design. Her practice combines print and pattern design with a strong interest in sustainable materials and bio-fabrication. She completed a student placement with NEXT's kidswear design team during her studies, gaining exposure to print design and fashion sourcing in a retail context.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest nature-inspired pattern work with an organic, earthy palette rooted in natural dyes and bio-material colour ranges. The visual language is quiet and textural rather than bold or graphic, reflecting a research-led approach to design. Patterns are informed by natural structures and botanical observation.

## Techniques and Tools

Pattern design, colour theory, and bio-materials research and fabrication. Her website documents extensive experimental work with natural dyes, recycled plastics, and gelatine-based bio-fabric construction. She describes blending techniques and disciplines across her practice.

## Markets and Clients

No commercial clients documented. Student placement at NEXT (kidswear design team). Her stated career goal is print and pattern design, potentially in the sustainable textiles sector.
