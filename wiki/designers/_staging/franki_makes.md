## Overview

Franki Barber is a UK-based illustrator and surface pattern designer working under the FrankiMakes name. She creates colourful conversational prints and illustrated narratives through a mixed hand-and-digital practice. Her work targets kidswear, homeware, and stationery markets. Her Instagram notes a current role as design assistant at JoJo Maman Bébé, indicating active industry placement alongside her independent studio and ThePatternCloud presence.

## Style and Aesthetic

Colourful, storytelling-driven conversational prints with a playful, whimsical character. Selected pattern images suggest character-forward illustration — hand-crafted marks developed into repeat structures, with a warmth and narrative quality suited to children's and family markets. The work is expressive and distinctly illustration-led rather than purely decorative.

## Techniques and Tools

Hand sketching forms the foundation using watercolour, gouache, and coloured pencil. Motifs are developed digitally through CAD design software. The mixed-media origin gives the work warmth and a handmade quality that carries through to the finished repeat.

## Markets and Clients

Kidswear, homeware, and stationery are the primary target markets. ThePatternCloud listing makes designs available to commercial buyers. Currently employed as a design assistant at JoJo Maman Bébé (UK childrenswear and maternity brand), demonstrating direct industry placement within the kidswear sector. Custom collection development available to brands.
