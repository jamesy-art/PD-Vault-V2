## Overview

Beatrice Campbell is a New York–based textile designer and surface pattern designer completing her degree at FIT (Fashion Institute of Technology, graduating 2025), with prior study at SCAD (Savannah College of Art and Design, 2023). Her practice centres on repeat pattern construction, screen printing, and fabric dyeing — combining an illustration foundation with hands-on textile techniques.

Selected pattern images suggest illustration-led work — organic, drawn motifs with a hand-made quality bridging fine art sensibility and textile application. The portfolio reflects a developing practice grounded in drawing and craft-based techniques.
