## Overview

Gae Helton is a US-based surface and pattern designer working under the brand Ditsy Fabric. She specialises in perfectly repeating patterns across florals, fauna, textures, geometrics, and Art Deco designs, with a portfolio targeting wallpaper, clothing, home décor, and quilting markets. Ready-made designs are available for licensing alongside custom commissions.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images reveal named patterns that establish the portfolio clearly: Morning Glory (floral), Deco Checks (indigo), Nouveau Rondell (fuchsia), Passion Flower (pewter), Art Deco Lattice Fretwork (copper), block weave (pale violet), sunset abstract, and Deco diamonds (green). The range spans botanical florals, geometric Art Deco structures, and abstract textures with a sophisticated, muted-to-refined palette — cool violets, pewter, copper, and warm florals.

## Techniques and Tools

Patterns derived from digital art, sketches, and fine art. Produces perfectly tiling repeat structures across diverse motif categories including Art Deco and Art Nouveau historical styles, botanicals, and geometrics.

## Markets and Clients

Primarily clothing manufacturers, furniture designers, and interior designers. Open to licensing ready-made designs and custom commissions. Dedicated quilters section of the portfolio — coordinating prints, cheater quilts, and blenders — extends reach to the quilt fabric market.

## Career Path

Working independently as Ditsy Fabric, building a portfolio across multiple pattern categories from digital art, fine art, and sketch origins. Background in surface design spanning historical decorative styles and contemporary botanical work.

## Pattern Focus

Strong Art Deco thread across selected patterns: lattice fretwork, deco diamonds, and Nouveau rondells alongside botanical florals (Morning Glory, Passion Flower). The block weave and sunset abstract entries demonstrate range beyond strictly historical revival territory. Portfolio is clearly organised and commercially named, suitable for licensing catalogues.
