## Website Images

## Overview

Lucrecia Caporale is a licensing artist and surface pattern designer based in Ireland, working primarily in botanical and nature-inspired design. Her work is placed with major US retailers including Target, Minted, Pottery Barn Kids, Golden Coil, and Barnes & Noble. She also produces fine art prints sold directly through her website under collections including "El Jardín."

## Style and Aesthetic

Soft, botanical, and nature-detailed with a refined, delicate palette. Self-described as "an expression of soft colour palette and textures through botanical compositions and organic lines with a delicate style." Selected pattern images suggest hand-feeling botanical repeat compositions — floral tapestries, organic linework, and nature studies in muted, natural tones. Selected placement images suggest retail stationery and home art contexts.

## Techniques and Tools

Gouache painting is the documented primary medium ("delicate gouache strokes" referenced in product listings). Work printed archivally on fine art quality, acid-free paper for direct sales. Both original art prints and surface pattern licensing are active arms of the practice.

## Markets and Clients

Licensing — retail stationery, home décor, and children's markets. Documented retail placements: Target, Minted, Pottery Barn Kids, Golden Coil (planners/notebooks), Barnes & Noble. Fine art prints sold in multiple sizes via direct website sales.

## Career Path

Based in Ireland. Built a licensing portfolio covering major US mass market and specialty retail channels — Target, Barnes & Noble, Pottery Barn Kids — alongside boutique stationery platforms Minted and Golden Coil. Operates both a licensing practice and a direct fine art print business in parallel.

## Pattern Focus

Botanical and landscape compositions. Documented works include "Nasturtium Tapestry," "Tulips in a Vase," "Coast Trees Landscape," "Autumn Light," "Forest Path Landscape," "Dancing Lines," "Above the Mountain," "Interlacing." Selected patterns suggest richly detailed botanical repeat work. Selected placements suggest stationery, home art, and interior décor applications.
