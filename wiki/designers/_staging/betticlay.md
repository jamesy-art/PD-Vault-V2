## Overview

Betty Clay is a US-based surface pattern designer working under her personal brand betticlay. Rooted in a Minnesota farm upbringing and Scandinavian heritage, her collections are inspired by prairie landscapes, wildflowers, and folk-influenced design sensibility. She designs exclusively for licensing, targeting fabrics, home textiles, wallpaper, and paper goods.

## Style and Aesthetic

Prairie-led with a Scandinavian undercurrent. Named collections — Prairie Bouquet, My Nordic Heart, Woodland Spring, Grandma's Kitchen, Just Squares, and Flower Party — indicate a range that spans botanical florals, folk geometry, and seasonal woodland motifs. Selected Instagram images suggest warm naturalistic palettes consistent with the heartland aesthetic she describes: wildflower hues, soft pinks, sage greens, and muted blues. The overall tone is cheerful and domestic rather than trend-driven.

## Career Path

Betty Clay grew up on a Minnesota farm and built several careers before turning to surface pattern design — music teaching, graduate study, and a 20-year role as an instructional designer in banking. She came to SPD after retirement, describing it as a return to lifelong creative making. Her Scandinavian ancestry and prairie upbringing are the explicit source of her creative identity.
