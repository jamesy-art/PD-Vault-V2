## Overview
Axelle Valentin is a Nantes-based freelance textile designer creating repeat patterns and collections for fashion and homeware brands worldwide. Services span concept to delivery: pattern development, complete collection building, trend and competitive analysis, colour research, and mood board creation. She holds an exclusive agency arrangement for the Japanese market through Taru Hironari of Talk International Japan.

## Style and Aesthetic
Work is framed as designed to reflect brand identity and showcase the product — each project conceived across rhythm, colour, material, and functionality. Instagram bio flags fashion, nature, home, and DIY as recurring themes. Selected patterns suggest nature-influenced, handcrafted-quality work with careful attention to repeat rhythm.

## Techniques and Tools
Handmade prints ("exclusive handmade prints" per Instagram bio). Services include colour research and mood board development. No specific software documented.

## Markets and Clients
Fashion, interior design, and DIY sectors. Works with brands internationally; exclusive Japan agent in place. No specific client names documented.
