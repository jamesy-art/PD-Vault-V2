## Overview

Suchi Deb is a self-taught watercolour artist and surface pattern designer based in the Netherlands. Her practice produces hand-painted designs for fabric, wallpaper, and home decor. Work is available through Spoonflower (keyword: suchideb) and directly via licensing, commissions, and collaboration enquiries.

## Style and Aesthetic

Deb's work is defined by soft watercolour painting with naturalistic botanical and floral subjects. Named collections include Radiant Blooms, Heirloom Botanica, The Ribboned Garden, Wildflower Collage, Cottage Garden, Summer Muse Poppies, Whimsical Woodland Animals, Trailing Whispers, Icelandic Poppies & Butterflies, Cottagecore Floral, and Winter Woodland. Selected Instagram patterns suggest lush floral compositions, warm botanical palettes, and organic surface arrangements. She also produces geometric and organic pattern work alongside her watercolour florals.

## Techniques and Tools

Deb is self-taught in watercolour, which she describes as philosophically central to her practice — valuing the medium's acceptance of imperfection. Designs are hand-painted and adapted for surface pattern production across fabric, wallpaper, and home decor categories.

## Markets and Clients

Deb targets home decor, fabric, and wallpaper markets and is actively available for licensing, commissions, and collaborations. Her work is available through Spoonflower and via direct licensing. The selected placement image suggests a home product application context.

## Career Path

Deb is self-taught and based in the Netherlands. She frames her creative motivation around the transformation of everyday objects through art. Her practice is currently oriented toward building a licensing client base alongside Spoonflower print-on-demand distribution.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns indicate botanical watercolour work across multiple named collections — florals, woodland animals, poppies, and cottage garden arrangements — produced as repeatable surface designs for fabric and wallpaper. Work spans densely decorative floral repeats and more open geometric compositions.
