## Overview

Mae Thompson is a UK-based textile designer and graduate of Textile Design from Arts University Bournemouth (AUB). Her practice centres on machine embroidery, hand embellishment, printing, and dyeing, with a mixed-media approach that contrasts hard and soft materials. She also creates jewellery under the name Mae Joan Jewellery, drawing on similar techniques — laser cutting, digital design, and hand beading.

## Style and Aesthetic

Thompson's textile work is technique-led and texture-focused. She explores contrast between hard and soft materials, and between machine and hand processes. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest surface textile work with varied embellishment and printing. Colour and finish are central to the practice. Her approach is tactile and material-first rather than digitally flat.

## Techniques and Tools

Machine embroidery, hand embellishment, screen and digital printing, dyeing processes. For jewellery: laser cutting, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, hand beading. Mixed-media across all practice areas.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion textiles, womenswear. Interest in styling for film and TV (wardrobe, set design). During her degree she collaborated on external projects — decorative stitching, distressing, and accessory styling for photoshoots. Mae Joan Jewellery stocked in an AUB-run shop.

## Career Path

Graduate of Textile Design, Arts University Bournemouth. Developed Mae Joan Jewellery during her studies as a parallel practice combining textile skills with jewellery-making. Has collaborated on styling and accessory projects within and beyond university.

## Pattern Focus

Surface textile patterns produced through embroidery, printing, and dyeing. Selected Instagram images suggest embellished and printed textile samples with tactile character. Her pattern work is embedded in a broader material and finishing practice. Selected placement images indicate garment application.
