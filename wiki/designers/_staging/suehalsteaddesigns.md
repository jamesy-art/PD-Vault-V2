## Overview

Sue Halstead is a UK-based botanical linocut artist and surface pattern designer working under the name Sue Halstead Designs. Her handcrafted lino prints form the source material for surface pattern collections targeting luxury stationery, greetings, and giftware. She is actively open for licensing and maintains a buyer mailing list for art directors and trade buyers.

## Style and Aesthetic

Halstead's work carries the distinctive quality of handcut lino: clean-edged botanical forms, strong graphic contrast, and a slow craft sensibility. Her source imagery draws from seasonal British gardens, wildflowers, and nature observed indoors. Selected Instagram patterns indicate detailed flower heads, leaves, and plant structures rendered with characteristic linocut clarity. The resulting surface patterns have a premium artisanal quality suited to markets that value craft heritage and nature connection.

## Techniques and Tools

Halstead's primary medium is lino printing — a physical process in which designs are cut by hand and pressed, then translated into repeat surface pattern for commercial application. Seasonal cycles inform her print development. She also teaches in-person lino printing workshops, suggesting an established presence in the UK craft and print community.

## Markets and Clients

Halstead targets the luxury stationery, greetings, and giftware markets. Both her website and Instagram bio reference licensing collections, and she maintains a quarterly buyer update list. Selected placement images suggest application to cards and printed gift goods contexts.
