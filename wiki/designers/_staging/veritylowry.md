## Overview

Verity Lowry is a Dublin-based surface pattern designer and illustrator with over a decade of professional experience in graphic design for the children's clothing industry. She now focuses on original prints for kidswear, home decor, stationery, and fashion, and is open to licensing and commission opportunities globally. Her client roster includes established international children's and lifestyle brands.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram patterns suggest bold, hand-drawn, graphic prints with a playful energy well-suited to childrenswear and related lifestyle products. Work plays with shape and colour to produce visually active, imaginative compositions. Instagram bio describes her approach as "creating playful patterns for cool clients" — a tone consistent with a commercially proven print practice. No placements selected; the focus is the pattern library.

## Techniques and Tools

Hand-drawn illustration is the documented foundation; designs are digitally finished and technically prepared for licensing and production. Background in graphic design for the children's clothing industry informs layout, scale, and commercial application.

## Markets and Clients

Primarily kidswear, with extension into home decor, stationery, and fashion. Documented clients include Hanna Andersson, Quince, Next, Muddy Puddles, Lalo, Alice & Ames, Loocsy, and Flow Magazine. Open to global licensing, commissions, and freelance work. Full portfolio available on private request via hello@veritylowry.com.

## Career Path

Over ten years in graphic design within the children's clothing industry before transitioning to independent surface pattern practice. Built a client roster spanning US, UK, and Irish brands. Based in Dublin; works internationally. Maintains a private portfolio for licensing and collaboration enquiries.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns suggest bold, graphic all-over prints — hand-drawn motifs, geometric shapes, and playful compositions calibrated for childrenswear scale and colour requirements. Consistent with commercial briefs from clients such as Muddy Puddles and Hanna Andersson. Portfolio also covers stationery and home decor applications. Experience of 10+ years positions this as a commercially mature practice.
