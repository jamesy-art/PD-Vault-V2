## Overview

Charlotte Victoria is a surface pattern designer and illustrator based between Cornwall and London, working as Salty Siren Studios. She holds a BA in Graphic Design from Falmouth University. Her practice centres on coastal-themed surface pattern and illustration, directly informed by her personal participation in free diving, swimming, and surfing. She produces and sells GOTS-certified organic cotton tea towels and illustrated greetings cards direct-to-consumer, and offers her surface designs for fabric licensing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Coastal and ocean-inspired, celebrating colour, abundance, and the emotional quality of life by the sea. Selected Instagram images suggest a naturalistic, illustration-led approach with coastal motifs and vibrant colour. The brand aesthetic is joyful and experiential — rooted in the designer's own relationship with the ocean rather than generic coastal clichés.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital illustration is the primary method, consistent with her BA in Graphic Design. Work described as "digitally illustrated" for greetings card production. Her website also references graphic design, illustration, surface pattern, and mural work as distinct practice areas.

## Markets and Clients

Direct-to-consumer products: GOTS-certified organic cotton tea towels (309gsm, UK-made, 49×72cm), A6 greetings cards on recyclable uncoated paper with compostable packaging. Fabric and surface designs available for licensing. Markets: home decor, stationery, gifting. Selected placement images indicate work on textile and card formats.

## Career Path

Studied Graphic Design at Falmouth University. Founded Salty Siren Studios from her personal connection to coastal activities and ocean culture. Based between Cornwall and London.

## Pattern Focus

Coastal motifs — sea, marine life, and the emotions of outdoor water-based activities — applied to illustrated surface patterns. Selected patterns suggest structured, colourful compositions. Placements confirm work applied to tea towel and greetings card formats, with licensing potential for broader textile applications.
