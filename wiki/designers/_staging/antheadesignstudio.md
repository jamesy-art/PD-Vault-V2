## Overview

Priyadarshini Das Sharma — known as Priya — is an illustrator and surface pattern designer based in a small town in South India, working under the studio name Anthea Designs. Represented by licensing agency Brenda Manley Designs, her practice centres on licensing-oriented surface pattern design with a whimsical, cottagecore aesthetic and a strong botanical orientation.

## Style and Aesthetic

Priya describes her work as "bold, vibrant art with a modern-vintage, cottagecore vibe." Selected pattern images suggest vibrant, illustrative botanical compositions with a warm, hand-rendered quality. Inspiration is drawn from her garden and her collection of Indian sarees — visible in the richness and layering of her botanical motifs. The aesthetic bridges cottagecore warmth with a retro decorative sensibility.

## Techniques and Tools

Works digitally and with coloured pencils, supporting a hand-drawn aesthetic within production-ready output. Portfolio is structured around florals, greeting cards, holiday, and novelty categories — standard licensing-facing organisation.

## Markets and Clients

Primarily licensing-oriented. Maintains a dedicated licensing catalog available by email request. Represented by Brenda Manley Designs. Selected placement images suggest stationery, greeting card, and giftable product applications. Full catalog available on request; the website shows a partial sampling only.

## Career Path

Pursued postgraduate degrees in English Literature and Journalism before returning to art. Discovered surface pattern design through a class taken during maternity leave — a turning point with, in her words, "no looking back." Self-taught path into SPD; now represented by an established licensing agency.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns confirm a strong floral and botanical core — vibrant, layered compositions with a hand-drawn illustrative character. Catalog organised around florals, greeting cards, holiday, and novelty themes, with botanical motifs drawn from garden observation and Indian saree textile traditions.
