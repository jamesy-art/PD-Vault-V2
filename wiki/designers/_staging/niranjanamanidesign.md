## Overview

Niranjana Mani (Nira) is a US-based licensed surface pattern designer and self-taught watercolour artist. By day an engineer, she has focused on surface pattern design since early 2023, combining hand-painted watercolour and hand-drawn digital illustration to create patterns for wallpaper, fabric, and paper goods. Her work is positioned for licensing enquiries.

## Style and Aesthetic

Her practice blends the soft, organic feel of watercolour with digital illustration tools, working with classic repeat layout styles reimagined with a personal perspective. Patterns are designed to bring calm, playfulness, and storybook charm into interiors — cosy, imaginative, and artfully layered.

## Techniques and Tools

Hand-painted watercolour and hand-drawn digital illustration, often combined in a single design. Works with classic repeat structures and reinterprets them with fresh composition and colour. Presents work via Adobe Portfolio.

## Markets and Clients

Wallpaper, fabric, and paper goods. Open to licensing enquiries via Instagram DM or email. Separate art account (@_nira_m_) for personal creative work.

## Career Path

Professional engineer with a creative practice in surface pattern design developed since early 2023. Self-taught in both watercolour and digital illustration. Focused on building a licensing presence across wallpaper, fabric, and paper goods.

## Pattern Focus

Botanical and nature-inspired patterns rendered in watercolour and digital illustration — calm, storybook-inflected compositions suited to wallpaper and fabric. Classic layout structures reimagined with a handmade warmth.
