## Overview

Manali Dixit is a London-based textile designer graduating from London Metropolitan University with a BA Hons in Textile Design in 2025. Her graduate project explores the intersection of nature and urban architecture, drawing on a visit to Vienna and the philosophy of Friedensreich Hundertwasser. She works under the Instagram handle @empatterns with specialisms across Textiles for Interiors, Print, and broader Textiles.

## Style and Aesthetic

Manali's practice is conceptually grounded in the tension between organic natural growth and structured urban architecture. Her visual language references Hundertwasser's playful, colourful approach to built form — climbing vines, mismatched tiles, curved organic forms — translated into textile surface design. Selected Instagram images (5 approved patterns) suggest bold, colourful surface compositions. Selected placement images (2) suggest interior textile application contexts.

## Techniques and Tools

Trained specialisms: Textiles for Interiors, Textiles – Print. The graduate project methodology blends digital embroidery, print, texture, and colour to explore conceptual themes about design coexisting with the natural world. The project brief references a multi-technique approach rather than a single medium.

## Markets and Clients

Textiles for Interiors specialism positions her toward home furnishing, upholstery, and interior textile markets. No documented clients — Manali is an early-career designer with a clearly articulated conceptual framework and graduate-level project work as her primary evidence base.
