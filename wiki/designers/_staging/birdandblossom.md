  Hello, my name is Andrea...
  This is a small glimpse into what’s currently unfolding in my studio—a space where ideas are constantly evolving and new directions are always taking shape. My practice moves fluidly between digital illustration and gouache painting, and I often alternate between the two. Gouache allows for rich, tactile expression and hand-drawn nuance, while digital work gives me the freedom to refine, layer, and develop pieces with precision. Together, they create a balance between spontaneity and control that defines my style.

  I’m especially drawn to the challenge of transforming artwork into seamless repeating patterns. There’s something incredibly satisfying about taking a single illustration and expanding it into a rhythm that can live across fabric, wallpaper, stationery, and homewares. It opens up endless possibilities—turning a moment or motif into something immersive and functional.

  Pattern design is something I discovered during my studies in graphic design, and it completely shifted the way I think about composition. Learning how to build a repeat was a turning point, but learning how to translate my own illustrations into that format took it to another level. It pushed me to think not just about the artwork itself, but how it interacts, flows, and tells a story across a surface.

  I naturally gravitate towards designing in collections, with each piece thoughtfully considered as part of a bigger picture. I think about how colours, motifs, and scale work together across different applications—whether that’s a hero print, coordinating patterns, or complementary colourways. This approach allows for versatility in the end product, making it easy to translate designs across a range of items while maintaining a cohesive and recognisable aesthetic.

## Overview

Andrea Hurley is a surface pattern designer and illustrator based in regional Victoria, Australia, operating as Bird & Blossom Design Studio. She creates colourful, illustration-led designs for licensing across textiles, stationery, and homewares, and is represented by Illustrate It Agency. Her work is stocked across eleven Australian independent retailers, with original products available through her own studio shop.

## Style and Aesthetic

Bird & Blossom's work is warm, expressive, and grounded in hand-made character. The studio describes its positioning as "environmentally inspired art," and selected Instagram pattern images suggest nature-led compositions — florals, botanicals, and organic motifs — with rich, painterly colour handling. The aesthetic bridges hand-drawn warmth and refined digital execution, making it well-suited to fabric, homewares, and stationery applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Hurley works fluidly between gouache painting and digital illustration in Procreate, often alternating between the two within a single project. Gouache provides tactile expressiveness and hand-drawn nuance; digital work enables precision layering and repeat development. Together, they create what she describes as "a balance between spontaneity and control" — the defining quality of the studio's output.

## Markets and Clients

Bird & Blossom targets textiles, stationery, home décor, and womenswear markets. The studio is represented by Illustrate It Agency for licensing. Retail distribution spans eleven Australian stockists including Hattie and the Wolf (Ballarat), The Presentation Place (Williamstown), Arthouse Industries (Port Macquarie), Bohemian Sundays (Peregian Beach), and Wildfire Blooms (Alice Springs). Selected placement images from Instagram suggest work appearing on fashion apparel, tote bags, and printed goods.

## Career Path

Hurley discovered pattern design through graphic design studies. Learning repeat construction was an early turning point, but translating her own illustration style into that format deepened the practice — pushing her to consider how artwork "interacts, flows, and tells a story across a surface." She built Bird & Blossom Design Studio from this foundation, developing a collection-based design model suited to multi-product licensing.

## Pattern Focus

Hurley works in collections rather than individual prints — each set structured around a hero print, coordinating patterns, and complementary colourways. This approach is designed for versatility across product categories while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest floral and botanical motifs with warm, painterly colour handling. Target substrates include fabric, wallpaper, stationery, and homewares.
