## Overview

Thuany Gabrielle Figueiredo is a print designer and illustrator based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Fashion Design and has eight years of experience in the Brazilian fashion market. She currently works as Creative Coordinator at Studio Paraizo, overseeing moodboards, collection concepts, colour palettes, trend research, and print coordination.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected images suggest tropical, botanical, and illustrative print work consistent with Brazilian womenswear aesthetics — vibrant, colour-forward designs suited to resort and summer collections. Work shows command of both placement and all-over repeat compositions, with an expressive, decorative quality informed by her illustration practice.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital print design with a full fashion-industry workflow: moodboards, collection concepts, colour palettes, trend research, and print coordination. Background in fashion design informs understanding of garment application, commercial briefs, and seasonal collection structure.

## Markets and Clients

Brazilian fashion market, womenswear. Documented client collaborations include Farm, Fábula, Enjoy, and My Place. Studio projects include Espaço Love, Aventurina, and Korean Textil for Studio Paraizo; White Closet and Zinzane for Anna Mendes Design. Selected placement image suggests familiarity with fashion application contexts.

## Career Path

Entered the fashion industry in 2016. Built print design experience across a range of Brazilian womenswear brands before joining Studio Paraizo, where she now serves as Creative Coordinator. Available for freelance commissions via Instagram DM.

## Pattern Focus

Tropical, botanical, and illustrative prints suited to womenswear and resort collections. Selected patterns suggest confident use of organic motifs, decorative composition, and colour-driven storytelling across a range of scales and structures.
