## Overview

Natália Fischer is a São Paulo-based graphic designer and print studio founder working as Branco Design. She established the studio with a focus on prints and stationery, later expanding into physical product design (playing cards, picture frames, toiletry bags, posters) and visual identity work. The studio has over 22,000 Instagram followers and is a verified account with 2,500+ posts, giving it strong Brazilian digital presence.

## Style and Aesthetic

Eight selected pattern images and four placement images suggest a decorative print practice oriented toward stationery and lifestyle products. The about text describes an aesthetic of "delicacy, love, and design." The studio name Branco (Portuguese for "white" or "blank") alludes to the blank page as the founding creative inspiration. Selected placements suggest product application in the stationery and lifestyle category.

## Markets and Clients

Primary markets: fashion (print supply), decoration, surfaces, and stationery. The studio also develops visual identity and logos for emerging brands.
