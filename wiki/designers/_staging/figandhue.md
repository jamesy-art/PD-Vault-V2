## Overview

Emma Sowerby is a Liverpool-based freelance print and pattern designer operating under the studio name Fig and Hue. With over a decade of professional experience since graduating from Leeds Arts University in 2012, she works across home interiors, children's print, and stationery. All work is designed and printed in the UK.

## Style and Aesthetic

Sowerby is best known for delicate watercolour florals with an organic, textured quality. Her portfolio extends to illustrative children's print and stationery styles. A naturally organic aesthetic and love of texture remain central to her creative process. Selected patterns confirm loose watercolour compositions — florals with soft edges and layered colour work.

## Techniques and Tools

Primarily hand-painted, with digital elements now incorporated into the process. Skills include painting, pattern design, colour theory, market adaptation, and client collaboration. 10+ years of professional freelance experience.

## Markets and Clients

Works with UK brands and some international clients (specific names not documented). Markets served: home interiors, children's print, and stationery. Selected placement images suggest application to printed goods and textile contexts.

## Career Path

Sowerby graduated from Leeds Arts University in 2012 and began her freelance career immediately, building a UK and international client base over the following decade. She works from Liverpool.

## Pattern Focus

Watercolour florals form her signature output — loose, organic, multi-petal compositions in soft colour palettes. Secondary capability in illustrative children's print and stationery patterns. Selected patterns confirm a consistent watercolour-led style across floral and nature motifs in both flat and tonal applications.
