## Overview

Anela's Design Studio is the trading name of Olena Kloeppner, a Germany-based surface pattern designer and placement print illustrator. The studio produces illustrated seamless patterns and placement prints for gift cards, gift wrap, stationery, fabric, and wallpaper. Kloeppner is represented by @illustrateit_agency and sells collections via Spoonflower. Her work is characterised by bright, highly detailed, whimsical illustrations executed in a Procreate gouache style.

## Style and Aesthetic

Kloeppner works in a bright, vibrant, highly textural style — her own description is "joyful and playful." Her collections span seasonal and holiday themes (Halloween, St. Patrick's Day, Christmas, Italian travel) alongside nature-based designs including hydrangeas, woodland motifs, and japandi-influenced florals. Selected Instagram images suggest densely worked, colourful compositions with rich surface detail. Placement prints frequently incorporate hand lettering and puns suited to greeting card and gift packaging contexts.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital workflow using Procreate, with a stated aim to recreate a realistic hand-painted gouache effect. Produces hand lettering integrated into placement print designs. Offers licensing, custom project work, and a full licensing catalogue available on request.

## Markets and Clients

Primary markets are stationery and gift (greeting cards, gift wrap, gift bags), fabric (via Spoonflower), and wallpaper. Named licensed collections include Bella Italia Fabric Collection (2025), Japandi Farmhouse Wallpaper & Fabric Collection (2025), Grandmillennial Collection (2024), Cute and Spooky Halloween Collection (2025), Limelight Green Hydrangeas (2025), and multiple seasonal holiday titles. Accepts licensing enquiries directly or via @illustrateit_agency.
