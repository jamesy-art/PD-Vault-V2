## Overview

Katrine Hesselberg is a Norwegian graphic designer and illustrator specialising in floral and botanical artwork. Trained as a graphic designer, she redirected her practice toward intricate flower painting and decorative illustration—driven by a desire to make beautiful things for the joy of it. She is based in Norway and takes commissions via Instagram.

## Style and Aesthetic

Katrine's work centres on detailed floral painting with an emphasis on decorative finish and botanical observation. Selected Instagram patterns suggest intricate flower and foliage compositions—expressive mark-making combined with careful, layered detail. Her palette follows naturalistic flower tones. She is known for her painted floral letter series.

## Techniques and Tools

Painting is her primary medium, with a practice that also includes paper art and three-dimensional paper flowers. Her graphic design training informs compositional structure. She works directly from observation and personal inspiration rather than commercial briefs.

## Markets and Clients

No documented commercial clients. Takes commissions via Instagram DM. Her decorative, hand-crafted output is suited to licensing applications in stationery, gifting, and home decor, though no confirmed market relationships are documented.

## Career Path

Trained graphic designer who pivoted toward personal creative practice after recognising that client work rarely left room for the intricate floral work she wanted to make. She began building a practice around floral illustration—painting flowers, letters, and paper art—and now takes commissions. She describes her journey as one of recovering creativity after burnout.

## Pattern Focus

Intricate floral and botanical compositions with a decorative, painted quality. Selected patterns suggest elaborate flower arrangements with layered detail. Paper art and dimensional flower work also feature. Output is decorative rather than structured repeat-pattern oriented.
