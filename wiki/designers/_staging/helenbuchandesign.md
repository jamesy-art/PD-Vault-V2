## Overview

Helen Buchan is a surface pattern designer and illustrator based in the Midlands of England, with many years of professional experience across in-house and freelance design roles. She studied at the Royal College of Art, London, and specialises in children's print with the animal world as her primary source of inspiration.

## Style and Aesthetic

Buchan's visual signature centres on the animal world, drawn with a playful, character-driven quality that balances warmth with illustrative precision. Selected Instagram images suggest repeat patterns featuring animals and nature in an accessible, commercially legible children's aesthetic. Her Instagram bio lists Fashion, Home, Gift, Books, and Stationery as sectors served, indicating breadth beyond core kidswear.
