## Overview

Mónica Muñoz is a Madrid-based surface pattern designer and illustrator operating under the Moniquilla studio. Originally from Salamanca, she holds a Fine Arts degree from the Universidad de Salamanca. Her practice spans fashion, editorial, food and beverage, ceramic, and packaging applications across national and international markets. She has over 131,000 Instagram followers and holds a verified account — one of the more prominent Spanish pattern designers on the platform.

## Style and Aesthetic

Muñoz's work draws on watercolour, markers, and wax crayons, giving her patterns a warm, expressive hand-crafted quality. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest a colourful, illustrative vocabulary — florals, decorative motifs, and figure-informed surface work applied to all-over print formats. Her palette tends warm and vivid. The visual language is personal and playful while remaining commercially applicable to fashion and kidswear.

## Techniques and Tools

Muñoz works across watercolour, markers, and wax crayons, building a diverse technical range that she has applied to client collections over many years. She teaches illustration and pattern design at the Instituto Europeo del Diseño (IED) Madrid and at the Universidad de Salamanca, indicating a formal understanding of both studio practice and industry-facing methodology.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion (womenswear, kidswear), editorial, food and beverage, and packaging. Documented clients include Zara Home, Women'Secret, Urban Outfitters, Zara Baby & Kids (spring/summer 2024 floral collection), and French brand Ikks Paris (stars and lightning motifs, winter 2024 childrenswear). Previously served as Art Director at Fun&Basics, overseeing all graphic and print creation.

## Career Path

Fine Arts graduate from the Universidad de Salamanca. Took on the role of Art Director at Fun&Basics, directing the graphic and print output of the brand. Has since built an independent freelance practice for national and international clients across fashion, editorial, and food sectors. Featured in the international Print & Pattern book and publications including Harper's Bazaar and Elle. Selected by Metrópoli / El Mundo as one of ten rising young design talents. Has shown at El Ego during Madrid Fashion Week on multiple occasions. Teaches illustration and pattern design at IED Madrid and the Universidad de Salamanca.
