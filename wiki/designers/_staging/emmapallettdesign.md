## Overview

Emma Pallett is a UK-based freelance print designer and illustrator based in the Midlands. She graduated with a Textiles for Fashion degree and has since worked across in-house design studios and freelance roles in the fashion and greeting card industries. Her practice spans print design, character illustration, and graphic design — ranging from cute and character-led to elegant and modern — using a mix of hand-drawn and digital techniques.
