## Overview

Nalani Jones is a Midwest surface design studio operated by Alana Lemke, specialising in intricate colorful botanicals for wallpaper, fabric, and home products. With a background in graphic communication and prior career as a graphic designer at a marketing agency, Lemke has transitioned fully into surface design. The studio positions itself toward brands and licensing partners seeking vibrant, botanically detailed design.

## Style and Aesthetic

Lush, maximalist botanicals — intricate arrangements of leaves, flowers, and organic forms with bold, saturated colour palettes. Selected Instagram images suggest detailed all-over botanical repeat patterns with a confident, layered quality. The studio describes its work as "bold and contemporary designs with captivating color palettes" and emphasises designs that "breathe new life into interiors, textiles and products."

## Techniques and Tools

Background in graphic communication. Professional software-based repeat construction workflow implied by the studio's print-on-demand platform presence and licensing positioning. Specific digital tools not documented.

## Markets and Clients

Designs distributed across wallpaper and fabric products. Available for licensing and custom commission work. Instagram bio confirms: "Designer creating botanical artwork for brands. Licensing & custom designs." Selected placement images suggest fabric and product mockup contexts indicating direct-to-brand and POD platform sales. Target categories include kidswear and home decor markets.
