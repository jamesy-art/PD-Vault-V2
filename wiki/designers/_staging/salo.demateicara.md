## Overview

Salome Dematei Cara (working as Salo Dematei Cara) is an artist, illustrator, and textile designer based in Mercedes, Buenos Aires, Argentina. She studied for several years at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes in Buenos Aires, complementing her formal education with courses and self-directed practice. She worked two years at Pehuen, a textile studio, collaborating with national and international brands, before moving into a freelance role at a print studio. Alongside this she develops her own project of hand-painted and digitally produced tapestries on gabardine.

## Style and Aesthetic

Defined by a combination of technique, texture, and colour in pursuit of visual harmony. Her practice moves fluidly between painterly hand-worked surfaces and digital illustration. Nature and everyday life are primary sources of inspiration. Selected Instagram images — particularly the portfolio and print series — suggest richly textured, expressive compositions suited to both decorative textile and surface pattern applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Works with oil pastel, acrylic, and watercolour for hand-painted work; creates digital illustrations daily. Comfortable across traditional and digital media. Portfolio is organised into distinct sections: hand-painted works (obras pintadas a mano), digital illustrations, textile deco, and prints.

## Markets and Clients

Two years at Pehuen working with national and international brands on textile and illustration briefs. Currently freelancing at a print studio. Develops and sells hand-painted tapestries on gabardine independently. Open to freelance illustration and pattern design projects.

## Career Path

Studied at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes, Buenos Aires. Specialised as textile designer and illustrator at Pehuen (two years). Now freelancing at a print studio while building an independent tapestry and pattern practice.

Expressive, texture-rich surface work spanning hand-painted and digital print. Selected patterns suggest painterly, mixed-media compositions with strong colour.
