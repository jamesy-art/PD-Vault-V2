## Overview

Applied Arts is a Brooklyn-based textile design studio founded by Anjuli Bernstein, specialising in bespoke handwoven textiles for interior design projects and design consulting for industry professionals. Woven prototypes and limited yardages are produced on wooden looms in the Brooklyn studio, with natural materials and distinctive weave structures as defining priorities.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images reflect a woven textile aesthetic — structure-led compositions where weave construction is the primary design vehicle. The studio emphasis on natural materials and unique weave structures suggests an earth-toned, tactile visual palette with an architectural sensibility, positioned for premium interior contexts.

## Techniques and Tools

Handwoven production on wooden looms in a Brooklyn studio. Output is prototypes and limited yardage — not large-scale print runs. Natural materials are a consistent emphasis. Design consulting is offered alongside bespoke production, positioning the studio for direct collaboration with interior designers and architects.

## Markets and Clients

Interior design sector — architects, designers, and residential or hospitality clients requiring bespoke woven solutions. Selected placement images suggest high-end interior applications. Design consulting is a documented service for industry professionals.

Sole-founder studio based in Brooklyn. Practice documented from at least 2017 (earliest Instagram content). Combines bespoke handwoven production with design consulting for interior industry professionals.

Selected pattern images reflect handwoven textile structures rather than printed surface pattern. The design vocabulary is structural and tactile — weave-derived geometrics and texture-based compositions in natural material palettes, distinct from digital print surface design.
