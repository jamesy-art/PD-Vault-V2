## Overview

Eva Jansen is a Netherlands-based freelance illustrator, animator, and live artist working from The Hague. With over 500 Instagram posts and a broad client base spanning educational, corporate, and cultural sectors, she produces illustrations and animations for books, posters, websites, social media banners, and live event coverage. Her practice bridges paper-based and digital production, with a preference for work that carries a recognisable personal signature.

## Style and Aesthetic

Warm, humorous, and attentive to people and environment — her stated aesthetic combines sensitivity with a touch of irony. Selected Instagram images suggest figurative and editorial illustration with expressive mark-making and narrative clarity. The work is communicative and character-driven rather than decorative repeat.

## Techniques and Tools

Works on paper using pencil, coloured pencil, fineliner, markers, watercolour, gouache, and acrylic paint. Digitally produces work in Adobe Illustrator, Procreate, and Adobe Fresco. Animations are produced in Adobe After Effects. Also practises live art — drawing visual minutes of meetings and events in-person and remotely, and creating discussion charts and visualisations.

## Markets and Clients

Education, corporate communications, publishing, and events. Documented output spans animated explainer films, storyboards, editorial illustration, book illustration, posters, print production management, and live graphic facilitation. Available for commissions and freelance illustration assignments.
