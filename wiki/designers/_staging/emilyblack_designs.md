## Overview

Emily Black is a US-based artist selling hand-drawn botanical wall art prints via her own website and Instagram. Her work is inspired by contrast and the beauty of natural form, executed through controlled line-work. She ships to the USA, Canada, and the UK.

## Style and Aesthetic

Emily's prints are refined botanical studies in muted, considered palettes — dusty mauve, mushroom beige, muted slate blue, muted sage, terracotta, sandstone, and olive green. Subjects include tropicals (bird of paradise, heliconia, hibiscus, protea), roses, and foliage. The work is spare and controlled, oriented toward considered interior decor markets rather than bold pattern design.

## Techniques and Tools

Each piece begins as a hand-drawn study of natural form, printed on carefully selected paper for a matte, timeless finish. No digital-only production tools mentioned — the hand-drawn origin is core to the brand positioning.

## Markets and Clients

Consumer-facing wall art print retail is her sole documented market. All prints are priced at $30.00 as matte art prints. Her stated positioning — "for spaces that feel both refined and lived in" — targets interior home decor buyers rather than trade or licensing clients.
