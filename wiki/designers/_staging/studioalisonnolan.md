## Overview

Allie Nolan is a British illustrator and hand lettering artist based in Zürich, Switzerland, working under the name Studio Alison Nolan. She creates colourful, textured digital designs spanning illustrated lettering, seamless surface patterns, and character illustration, and is available for commission and collaboration.

## Style and Aesthetic

Nolan's work is characterised by rich texture, warm colour palettes, and a decorative sensibility that draws equally on lettering tradition and botanical illustration. Selected Instagram patterns suggest floral motifs with layered detail, confident use of colour, and a handcrafted quality despite being executed digitally. Her output bridges illustrated gift design and surface pattern.

## Techniques and Tools

Nolan works digitally, producing colourful textured designs that reference traditional illustration and lettering methods. Her skills include repeat construction, colour theory, and market adaptation alongside illustration and hand lettering.

## Markets and Clients

Nolan positions her work for home decor, stationery, and gifting markets. She is available for commissions and collaborations. Selected placement images suggest application to printed stationery and home goods.

## Career Path

Before moving into illustration and surface pattern design, Nolan worked as a commercial Interior Designer in architecture practices, designing office spaces for large corporate clients. She describes her shift to graphic-based work as fitting her life more comfortably as a mother of two. She is British and has been based in Zürich for an extended period.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns indicate floral and botanical work with illustrated character, textured repeats, and warm layered colour. Selected placement images suggest application to printed stationery and home product contexts.
