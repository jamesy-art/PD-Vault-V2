## Overview

Rosita Uricchio is an Italian illustrator and graphic designer originally from Southern Italy, currently based in Portugal. She studied Visual Arts at the Brera Academy in Milan and holds a Master's degree in Editorial Illustration from ISIA in Urbino. Over thirteen years she has lived and worked across Hungary (Pécs), Germany (Berlin), South America (Cusco, Peru), and Portugal — a peripatetic path that informs the cultural range of her practice. She works remotely with international clients and has two published illustrated books.

## Style and Aesthetic

Poetic, metaphorical, and colourful. Uricchio describes her work as translating ideas and emotions through impactful visual language. Selected Instagram images suggest an expressive, character-driven illustrative practice with botanical and figurative compositions. Her work has consistent thematic preoccupations: nature, plant life, sustainability, and collective awareness. The Instagram identity "illustrator & plant seeker" signals a direct connection between personal interest and visual subject matter.

## Techniques and Tools

Editorial illustration practice spanning books, animation, logotypes, and surface work. Remote production for a global client base. Work documented across editorial, book illustration, and animation contexts.

## Markets and Clients

Clients include Unicef, Corriere della Sera, Awid, The Greats, FineActs, zeroCO2, and Wine52. Featured in Cicada Wheels, Picame mag, Curated by Girls, Farma 282, and Travel on Art. Published books: *In viaggio con le mamitas* (Gutenberg Edizioni, 2022) and *El Ojo, 20 preguntas sobre la maravillosa capacidad de ver* (Delirio Ediciones, 2025). Selected placement images suggest editorial and product surface applications.
