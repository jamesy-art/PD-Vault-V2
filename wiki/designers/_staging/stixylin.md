## Overview

Dominique Collins is a Brisbane-based graphic artist and illustrator known online as Stixylin. Her work is colourful, expressive, bold, and feminine — spanning surface patterns, illustration, and prints. As of the time of this profile, her website pattern shop is closed and she is not available for new commissions.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest bright, expressive patterns with bold colour application and illustrative mark-making. The work is consistently vivid and high-energy, sitting within the colourful, character-influenced aesthetic typical of Australian contemporary illustrators working across surface design and print.

## Techniques and Tools

Graphic art and illustration applied to surface pattern design. Background in illustration and pattern-making. Prior website referenced custom surface pattern capability and artist collaboration services.

## Markets and Clients

No active market positioning as of current profile status. Prior to closure, the studio referenced surface pattern, textile, and custom pattern applications. Custom commissions were previously available.
