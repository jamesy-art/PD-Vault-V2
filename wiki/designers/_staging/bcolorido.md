## Overview

Nell Simböck is an Austrian surface designer based in Ronda, Andalucía, Spain, working under the brand bienvenido colorido. She creates joyful, colourful patterns for fabric, wallpaper, T-shirts, and surface applications — available for licensing and collaboration. She holds a Spoonflower competition win and documented licensed work.

Joyful, vibrant, and whimsical — the brand name ("welcome, colourful") signals an explicit commitment to bold chromatic expression.
