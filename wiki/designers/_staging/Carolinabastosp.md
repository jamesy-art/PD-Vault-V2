## Overview

Carolina Bastos is a Brazilian designer and illustrator from Rio de Janeiro, currently based in Barcelona where she completed a Master's in illustration at BAU Barcelona. Her work spans surface pattern design, tropical and botanical illustration, and fashion illustration, with a portfolio of client collaborations across Brazilian stationery and lifestyle brands.

## Style and Aesthetic

Bastos's work is characterised by vivid colour, nature-inspired motifs, and range — from tropical botanical prints to cosmic and celestial themes. Her fashion illustration work (Chloé SS2025 project at BAU) shows a surrealistic, soft-toned feminine register. Selected Instagram patterns suggest structured, colourful surface designs suited to stationery and apparel applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Works in digital illustration. BAU coursework documentation references sketchbook development before final digital execution. Fashion illustration and surface pattern design are her two primary output modes, approached with an academic illustration foundation.

## Markets and Clients

Documented clients include Cicero Papelaria (recipe book 2022, Coleção Astral 2022, the garden collection 2022, baby book 2022), Fato Surfwear (2024), Victoria Sayeg — Cosmic Illustrations (2024), Vntta — visual identity (2023), AVLE — tropical pattern (2022), Cultura Inglesa — Book Club (2020). Selected placements (4 images) suggest stationery product and womenswear garment contexts.
