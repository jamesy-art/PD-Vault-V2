## Overview

Michel Massih is a Brazilian multidisciplinary art director and designer based in Brazil, with over 15 years of professional experience. He operates a creative studio at michelmassih.com.br and maintains a parallel art and gallery practice at @galeria.massih. Surface design (estamparia) is one of eight documented service areas alongside branding, packaging, muralism, illustration, social media, UI/UX, and product design.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a broad visual practice spanning decorative surface patterns, murals, and identity work. His aesthetic is described as rooted in brasilidade — work that breathes Brazilian culture, nature, vitality, and joy, expressed through colour, movement, and emotion. The palette across selected pattern images suggests bold, energetic colour use with organic and cultural motifs.

## Techniques and Tools

Graphic design and market adaptation are the documented core skills. The studio operates across digital design, illustration, surface design, muralism, and packaging — indicating a comprehensive commercial design toolset. Self-described as a designer multidisciplinar by nature, driven by curiosity across techniques and languages.
