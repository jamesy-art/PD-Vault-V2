## Overview

Malika Buttinger is a surface pattern designer and illustrator based in Leiderdorp, the Netherlands. Her work is illustration-led, covering quirky animals, bold florals, and narrative-driven scenes. With a background in visual communication and a self-taught illustration practice, she offers art licensing, commissions, and print-on-demand through Spoonflower and Happywall. Documented collaborators include PicCollage, Unieke Stoffen, Happywall, Pink Pig Sketchbooks, and The Pattern Paper.

## Style and Aesthetic

Buttinger's work operates across two modes: clean vector patterns and more textured, expressive illustration. Inspiration comes from the animal kingdom, nature, childhood nostalgia, surrealism, and the visual language of the 1990s — filtered through soft, vibrant colour and gentle storytelling. Named collections include *Midnight Garden* (Halloween; botanical and celestial motifs with soft vintage tones) and *Cosmic Woods* (developed for The Pattern Challenge by Mélanie Johnsson). Selected Instagram pattern images suggest diverse subject matter — animals, botanicals, and narrative scenes — in a warm, playful, consistent palette.

## Techniques and Tools

Clean vector illustration and more textured expressive approaches. Background in visual communication. Distributes through Spoonflower and Happywall for print-on-demand.

## Markets and Clients

Kidswear, home decor, stationery, seasonal (Halloween, celebration). Documented clients and collaborators: PicCollage, Unieke Stoffen, Pink Pig Sketchbooks, Happywall, The Pattern Paper. Available for licensing and commissioned design projects.
