## Overview

Francesca Querin is a surface pattern and textile designer operating under the studio name Fran.illus. Born and raised in Caracas, Venezuela, and now based in Spain, she launched her independent studio in 2017 after a fashion design background introduced her to pattern work for textiles and surfaces. She works with international brands on exclusive and commercial collections and is open for licensing and collaborations.

## Style and Aesthetic

A watercolour-led approach informed by a fashion design foundation — designs show sensitivity to garment application, print placement, and trend-aware aesthetics. Selected pattern images suggest floral and decorative repeat compositions with a clean, contemporary quality suited to womenswear and kidswear markets.

## Techniques and Tools

Watercolour is the primary medium — discovered during fashion design training and retained as the signature technique. Works across pattern design for textiles, surfaces, and exclusive brand collections. Portfolio spans womenswear fashion design, surface pattern, and babywear categories.

## Markets and Clients

Womenswear and kidswear (babywear) are the primary declared categories. Has worked with unnamed international brands on exclusive collections. Positioned for licensing and direct client collaboration. Open for enquiries via hello@franillus.com.

## Career Path

Fashion designer by training, born in Caracas, Venezuela. Discovered watercolour illustration while studying. Transitioned from fashion industry employment to freelance practice in 2017, establishing Fran.illus as a surface pattern design studio. Has collaborated with international brands across textile, surface, and non-textile categories.

## Pattern Focus

Watercolour-driven floral and decorative repeat patterns, primarily aimed at womenswear and babywear. Selected patterns suggest delicate compositions suited to fabric print and surface application. Collections span fashion print, pattern design, and babywear per the declared portfolio structure.
