## Overview

Roberta Paolini is a Brazilian surface pattern designer and artist based in Brazil. She holds a degree in Architecture and Urbanism from USP (University of São Paulo) and has worked across graphic design and scenography for clients in Brazil and internationally. Her current practice is rooted in an authorial body of work built around the image of trees and roots — a guiding thread connecting memory, trauma, and healing — alongside commercial surface pattern design.

## Style and Aesthetic

Organic and architecturally informed. The tree and root motif functions as a conceptual anchor across her authorial practice. Declared influences span architecture (spatial and compositional thinking), scenic design, and lived experience across multiple cities and cultural contexts. Selected pattern images suggest a range including textural surface works and structured repeat compositions. The practice spans the decorative and the expressive.

## Techniques and Tools

Combined manual and digital process: drawing, printmaking (gravura/etching), and digital pattern construction. Architectural training shapes her approach to composition, space, and visual narrative. Named techniques: drawing, printmaking, surface pattern design (estamparia). Applications range from interior design through to fashion.

## Markets and Clients

Interior design and fashion are her declared application markets. Also active in graphic design and scenography. No specific commercial clients documented. Portfolio includes licensable patterns (visible under AVAILABLE FOR LICENSING on her website) alongside artwork.

## Career Path

Architecture and Urbanism graduate (USP, São Paulo). Practised graphic design and scenography for years, with clients in Brazil and internationally. Relocated across multiple cities and cultural contexts — an experience she describes as directly expanding her creative perspective. Transitioned into surface design alongside an authorial fine art practice.

## Pattern Focus

Nature-derived surface work with conceptual framing: tree forms, roots, organic structure. Selected pattern images include both repeat-oriented compositions and textural artwork. Named pattern collections suggest range across structural and organic styles (Play on the Street, Forest Protectors, Village, Waves on Clouds, Chita on the Wall, Rock Texture, Root). Selected Instagram images suggest confident, painterly mark-making applied to repeat contexts.
