## Overview

Tammy Gerlach is a Melbourne-based digital lettering artist and illustrator known online as Gerlacta, operating within the "bookish art" niche. With over 9,000 Instagram followers, she creates whimsical typography-led illustrations that combine hand lettering with flora, fauna, and fungi motifs — a distinctive visual identity suited to art licensing, book cover design, and decorative print markets. She is available for commissioned work and art licensing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Whimsical and enchanting, built on the integration of lettering and illustration. Nature — animals, plants, fungi — forms the core subject matter, rendered with a decorative, folksy character. Selected patterns suggest compositions where hand-lettered typography and botanical elements intertwine, producing a readable, emotionally warm aesthetic with strong appeal to book-loving and gift markets.

## Techniques and Tools

Works digitally in Procreate. Combines typography and illustration skills to produce lettering-led pattern and decorative art. Also creates custom Procreate brushes available commercially. A farm childhood in rural Australia is cited as the foundation of her deep engagement with animals and nature as subject matter.

## Markets and Clients

Art licensing, book cover design, and commissioned illustration. The "bookish" niche — decorative art for readers — positions the work for gifting, stationery, and homeware markets alongside publishing-adjacent licensing. Selected placement images suggest styled product and editorial contexts.

## Pattern Focus

Typography-integrated botanical illustration — lettering combined with floral, animal, and fungi motifs. Selected patterns suggest decorative, lettering-led compositions suited to gifting, stationery, and book-adjacent product licensing. Pattern work is characteristically decorative and text-led rather than strict repeating surface pattern.
