## Overview

Emilie Simpson is a Canadian artist and illustrator based in Bowmanville, Ontario, working in watercolour and gouache. Her work spans art prints, greeting cards, shoes, textiles, and editorial illustration, with placement across major Canadian lifestyle publications and international fashion and travel brands. She is open for custom pet and home portrait commissions.

## Style and Aesthetic

Emilie's style is charming and whimsical, rooted in the natural world, nostalgia, and vintage botanical illustration. She cites old children's book illustration and vintage botanical art as key influences. Selected Instagram images suggest botanical and animal compositions with a warmly illustrative quality — detailed, soft, and suited to gift product and editorial placement.

## Techniques and Tools

Primary media are watercolour and gouache. No digital-only production methods documented. Her handmade approach is integral to the warmth and character of her work.

## Markets and Clients

Emilie has documented commercial relationships across fashion, lifestyle, travel, and editorial markets. Named clients include Pink Chicken New York, Birdies, American Airlines, LCBO, Style at Home Magazine, Canadian Gardening Magazine, Canadian Living Magazine, and Elle Canada. Her work has also appeared on shoes and textiles.

## Career Path

Emilie has built a multi-market illustration career primarily in Canada, with work placed across major Canadian lifestyle publications and international fashion and travel brands. She accepts custom portrait commissions (pets and homes) alongside her commercial illustration practice.

Selected Instagram images suggest botanical motifs and animal-centred compositions suited to textile and gift product application.
