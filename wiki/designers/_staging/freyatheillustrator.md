## Overview

Freya Symonds is a UK-based illustrator and surface pattern designer operating as Freya the Illustrator. Based in the Forest of Dean, she recently graduated from the University of South Wales and is building an early-career portfolio spanning repeating patterns, book cover artworks, stationery design, and decorative illustration. Her work is primarily digital with some traditional practice and is openly available for commissions and collaborations.

## Style and Aesthetic

Nature-inspired illustration with detailed linework, vibrant colour, and textural depth. Work is inspired by the intricate structures found in plants, animals, and environments — flora and fauna are the primary motifs. Selected pattern images suggest structured repeat compositions with rich natural detail suited to stationery, product design, and home goods.

## Techniques and Tools

Primarily digital, with traditional work (lino printing) also practised. Detailed linework is a declared signature. Portfolio categories include literature illustration, pattern design, product design, TCG labels, and decorative illustration. A lino print was produced for and sold at the Printed Festival in 2024.

## Markets and Clients

Stationery is the primary declared market. World Illustration Awards entries include insect stationery, wildflower stationery, fungi stationery, and greeting cards. A selected placement image suggests product packaging or applied illustration context. Open for commissions across illustration, pattern design, and decorative work.

## Career Path

Recently graduated from the University of South Wales. Based in the Forest of Dean, UK. Early-career portfolio developed through academic projects (Macbeth and Jane Eyre book covers as Final Major Project work) and competition entries (World Illustration Awards, Printed Festival 2024). Currently building independent commission work.

## Pattern Focus

Nature-themed repeating patterns with botanical and entomological motifs — flowers, fungi, and insects are the declared subject categories from stationery design competition entries. Selected patterns suggest structured all-over compositions with colourful, textured linework suited to stationery and home products.
