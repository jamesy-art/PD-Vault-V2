## Overview

Surface pattern designer based in Portugal, formerly based in Brooklyn, New York. Over 20 years of professional experience, beginning with sleepwear and home textile design. Now focused on surface pattern design under the tagline "Mindful beauty for the spaces you cherish." Soft, feminine aesthetic drawing on vintage, natural, and coastal sources. 159 posts; 297 followers (business account).

## Style and Aesthetic

Soft femininity with delicate detail — her own description. Draws inspiration from historical artifacts, vintage treasures, seashells, and wildflowers. Selected patterns suggest careful, refined surface compositions with a timeless quality. Work born from play and curiosity rather than trend-led briefs.
