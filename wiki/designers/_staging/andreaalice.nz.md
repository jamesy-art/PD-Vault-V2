## Overview

Wellington-based commercial illustrator and surface pattern designer specialising in bright seamless florals and tropical prints. Andrea Muller licenses artwork to brands worldwide for product packaging, fabrics, fashion apparel, home decor, and editorial, and distributes direct through print-on-demand platforms including Spoonflower, Redbubble, and Society6.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram patterns suggest bold, high-saturation florals with tropical influence — hibiscus blooms, lush botanical arrangements, and maximalist repeat compositions. Colourways lean vibrant and market-forward, consistent with a broadly commercial tropical-floral positioning.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital design with a dual degree in Graphic Design and Textile Design. Delivers print-ready high-resolution seamless repeats and layered editable motif files, ready to apply to fabrics, wallpaper, product packaging, and editorial across a range of substrates.

## Markets and Clients

International brands across product packaging, fashion apparel, home decor, and editorial. Offers art licensing and bespoke commissions. Selected placement images suggest designs applied to fabric yardage and home textiles.

## Career Path

Holds a degree in Graphic Design and Textile Design, providing production knowledge from brief through to print on a range of substrates. Based in Wellington, New Zealand, working with international clients via licensing and commission. Available for freelance projects.

## Pattern Focus

Specialises in seamless florals and tropical prints. Hibiscus, botanical, and lush floral compositions appear consistently across selected images. Designs are available for licensing as seamless repeats or layered files, with immediate turnaround for licensed work.
