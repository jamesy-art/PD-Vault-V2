## Overview

Charisse Marei is a Pennsylvania-based surface pattern designer, eco-conscious interior designer, and published author. Her practice brings together decades of interior design experience — informed by building biology, feng shui, and sustainable design principles — with a surface pattern design catalogue available for licensing across home décor, stationery, and fabric. She also runs an interior design consulting practice and is an active speaker and media contributor.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram pattern images reveal a range spanning florals, stripes, geometric grids, and seasonal holiday collections. The work carries what Charisse describes as "a feminine flair," with colour stories including coastal blues and whites, soft pinks, classic neutrals, and targeted Christmas palettes. Pattern designs are named and positioned for commercial use — titles include Modern Candy Ribbon Floral Loops, Sweet Heart Blossoms & Stripes, Retro Geometric Square Grid, and Whimsical Christmas Stripes — suggesting a market-aware, licensing-oriented approach.

## Techniques and Tools

Charisse describes her work as handmade, with patterns stemming from a passion-driven creative process intended to be "felt in the resulting work." Specific digital software is not documented. Her interior design background — formal study in building biology, feng shui, and sustainable practices — directly informs the conceptual framing of each collection around well-being, balance, and eco-conscious living.

## Markets and Clients

Available for licensing across home décor, stationery, and fabric. Licensing options include non-exclusive (limited period, specific product) and exclusive (reserved single-brand) arrangements. Product applications include linens, accent pillows, wallpaper, stationery, gift wrap, notecards, stickers, kitchenware, and dog bandanas. Interior design consulting is offered separately for residential and commercial clients.

## Career Path

Charisse holds a BS in Interior Design and additional certifications from the Institute for Building Biology & Ecology (BBEC), the NY School of Feng Shui, Green Irene, and Graceful Lifestyles. Her career began in interior design, including aviation interior work for private and corporate jets. A bicycle accident became a turning point: the carefully designed, clutter-free environment she had built helped her recovery, deepening her conviction that designed spaces directly support well-being. She has since published three books and built a pattern design practice grounded in the same principles.

## Pattern Focus

The confirmed pattern range spans florals (Sweet Heart Blossoms & Stripes, Modern Candy Ribbon Floral Loops), stripes (Modern Chic Vertical Stripes, Classic Timeless Stripes), geometric grids (Retro Geometric Square Grid), and seasonal/holiday designs (Merry Berry Kisses & Holly Sprigs, Whimsical Christmas Stripes, Modern Christmas Stars & Geometric Starbursts). Colour stories are predominantly feminine, coastal, and classic, with distinct holiday collections. All 8 selected Instagram images are confirmed patterns — no placements in the approved set.
