## Overview

Emma Lawrence is an award-winning illustrator, author, and designer based in Shropshire, UK, working under the Emma Lawrence Designs® brand. Her pen-and-mixed-media style — clean line-work, gentle humour, and popular rural themes — has been licensed across greeting cards, gifts, stationery, kitchenware, bags, mugs, and children's ranges. She is represented by MHG Licensing and has clients including the NHS and UK charities. A former Clinical Neuropsychologist, she transitioned to full-time illustration and won industry awards in her first years of trading.

## Style and Aesthetic

Emma's work is characterised by clean pen line-work, a warm British countryside sensibility, and good-natured humour. Recurring themes include animals in characterful scenarios (dogs in wellies), wildflowers, rural landscape subjects, and celebratory imagery. Selected Instagram images (8 approved patterns) suggest illustrated surface designs across seasonal, animal, and botanical motif categories. The style sits in a well-loved British illustration tradition — accessible, heart-warming, and commercially proven in the gifting sector.

## Techniques and Tools

Pen and mixed media illustration, adapted to print and licensed product formats. Also produces branding and logo work for commercial clients, with applications on building signage, banners, and business cards. The Little Nature Explorers® children's book series extends her practice into educational publishing and character-based illustration for young readers.
