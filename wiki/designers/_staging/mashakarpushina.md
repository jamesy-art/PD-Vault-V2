## Overview

Masha Karpushina is a Moscow-born, London-raised illustrator and mural artist with a practice spanning illustration, art direction, and collaborative work with fashion and lifestyle brands. Her visual world is built from observation of the real and surreal — shaped by nature, human storytelling, and the multicultural fabric of London. Documented collaborations include a fashion collection with LABRUM and a 2017 calendar project with SIRIN. Based in London.

## Style and Aesthetic

Karpushina's work is defined by playful, colourful compositions with careful detailing, handwritten type, and expressive use of ink and watercolour. The visual register bridges figurative illustration and decorative surface work. Selected Instagram images suggest illustrated patterns with a warm, hand-led character across ornamental and figurative themes.

## Techniques and Tools

Works primarily in ink and watercolour. Experienced in art direction and project-based collaboration across fashion and lifestyle clients. Mural work and large-format painting are established parts of the practice.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion and lifestyle collaboration documented. MK x LABRUM is the documented fashion client. Portfolio spans illustration, art direction, food and drink, fashion, and calendar publishing. Open to commissions. No surface pattern licensing clients documented at this time.

## Career Path

Moscow born, raised and based in London. Built a practice as an illustrator, art director, and muralist. Documented collaboration: SIRIN x MK 2017 Calendar; MK x LABRUM fashion collection.

## Pattern Focus

Selected Instagram images suggest illustrated surface patterns with botanical, figurative, and ornamental motifs. The work has a handmade character suited to fashion, editorial, and lifestyle product applications.
