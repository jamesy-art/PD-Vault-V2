Fabric Drawer is the work of Melbourne designer Beck Ng I’m a surface designer and maker living in Melbourne, Australia, who has a love of creating patterns. Whilst working as a graphic designer for many years and yearning to create something more tactile, I enrolled into a screen-printing course, which led to start a textiles course and discover the world of repeating patterns. I started Fabric Drawer in 2012 after travelling and then living in London, spending many hours wondering how to follow my creative passions. Things started very small and organically, by being involved in a few small craft markets selling products I’d designed and made. Not deterred by the minimal sales, I continued to create my patterns and products and have been part of many design markets in Melbourne. As well as making my own range of products, I also worked with an art agent in America, licensing my artwork on a range gift/party ware and greeting cards. Since flying solo, I’ve collaborated with a range of local and international companies. Outside of Fabric Drawer, I’m a mum to two daughters, enjoy running and still work as a graphic designer.   I’m a surface designer and maker living in Melbourne, Australia, who has a love of creating patterns.

  Whilst working as a graphic designer for many years and yearning to create something more tactile, I enrolled into a screen-printing course, which led to start a textiles course and discover the world of repeating patterns.

  I started Fabric Drawer in 2012 after travelling and then living in London, spending many hours wondering how to follow my creative passions.

  Things started very small and organically, by being involved in a few small craft markets selling products I’d designed and made. Not deterred by the minimal sales, I continued to create my patterns and products and have been part of many design markets in Melbourne. As well as making my own range of products, I also worked with an art agent in America, licensing my artwork on a range gift/party ware and greeting cards. Since flying solo, I’ve collaborated with a range of local and international companies.

  Outside of Fabric Drawer, I’m a mum to two daughters, enjoy running and still work as a graphic designer.

  Thanks for reading my journey so far.

  x Beck

## Overview

Beck Ng is a Melbourne-based surface designer and maker operating as Fabric Drawer since 2012. She designs and produces a direct-to-consumer range of patterned goods — fabrics, stationery, homewares — and licenses her work to Australian and international brands. She also continues to work as a graphic designer outside of Fabric Drawer.

## Style and Aesthetic

Ng's work is inspired by life, travel, and nature, with a bright and vibrant colour sensibility aimed at bringing joy into everyday objects. Selected Instagram images suggest nature-inspired motifs — botanical and organic forms — rendered with warmth and graphic clarity across repeat compositions.

## Techniques and Tools

Ng trained as a graphic designer and later undertook screen-printing and textiles courses to develop her pattern practice. She produces small-run handmade goods from her Melbourne studio with a focus on quality over volume. Skills include illustration, graphic design, pattern design, colour theory, market adaptation, and licensing.

## Markets and Clients

Licensed and collaborated with: IZIMINI, AHD Paper Co., Nalgene, Miquel Rius, Spotlight, Cloud 9 Fabrics, JumJum Nappies, Art x Scrubs, Bravery Co., Apiary Made, The Green Collective, Monster Threads, Wili Heat Bags, Nerida Hansen Fabrics, Iseult, Swan Coastal Press, Moonpig. Markets span children's apparel, fabrics, stationery, and kitchen and dining goods. Licensing and wholesale are available. Selected placement images confirm product application across fabric and children's goods.

Press coverage includes Frankie Magazine, Country Style Magazine, Uppercase Magazine, Print & Pattern Blog, Flow Magazine, and Pattern Pulse (Rachael King). Winner, We Are Explorers × Nalgene Custom Water Bottle Competition 2025. Best Australian Textile Designer, APAC Insider 2022.

## Career Path

Ng worked as a graphic designer for many years before enrolling in a screen-printing course, which led to a textiles course and the discovery of repeating patterns. She founded Fabric Drawer in 2012 after living in London. She initially sold at Melbourne craft markets and worked with an American art agent licensing artwork on gift and greeting card ranges. She has since collaborated with a range of local and international companies.

## Pattern Focus

Nature and travel-inspired repeat patterns with vivid, everyday appeal. Selected patterns suggest organic botanical motifs suited to fabric, children's goods, and lifestyle markets. Work is illustrative rather than geometric, with a focus on warmth and accessibility across a range of product applications.
