## Overview

Clara Martí Aguasca is a Barcelona-based artist, illustrator, and pattern designer working under the name Clara Cottonets. With a Fine Arts degree from the University of Barcelona (2005) and specialisation in Drawing and Printmaking, her practice spans painting, printmaking, children's illustration, and surface pattern design. She launched the Cottonets children's accessories brand in 2013, with an online store since spring 2018, and maintains an active illustration and pattern practice alongside it.

## Style and Aesthetic

Aguasca's work is rooted in fine arts traditions — drawing, printmaking, and painting — informed by doctoral-level Land Art study and a humanist interest in storytelling. Her Instagram references Barcelona's Modernism scene as a contextual touchstone. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest a decorative surface pattern practice with a handmade, art-led quality, drawing on organic and illustrative motifs.

## Techniques and Tools

Drawing and printmaking as primary disciplines; digital adaptation for pattern and product design. Background in traditional illustration media including gouache and paint. Experience across painting, children's illustration, land art, and commercial pattern work.

## Markets and Clients

Children's accessories and lifestyle products via the Cottonets brand. Published children's book illustration: "The Tales of ITA" (2000, Editorial Eiunsa, San Sebastián). Collaboration with NGO Youbari Asso on a charitable project. Awards: Silver Medal, Montblanc Painting Competition 2006; Second Prize for Drawing, Cercle Artístic de Sant Lluc 2005.

## Career Path

Fine Arts graduate, University of Barcelona, 2005; specialisation in Drawing and Printmaking. Doctoral studies in Land Art project drawing. Secondary school teacher (post-graduation). Illustrated "The Tales of ITA" (2000). International art fairs and group exhibitions 2005–2006. 2013: launched Cottonets children's accessories brand. 2018: Cottonets online store. Ongoing NGO collaboration.

## Pattern Focus

Decorative surface patterns informed by fine arts traditions and a humanist creative perspective. Selected Instagram images suggest organic, nature-referenced motifs with a handcrafted illustration quality. Practice spans children's market products and independent artistic work.
