## Overview

Cassidy Rae Marietta is a visual content curator, illustrator, and painter based in Columbus, Ohio. Her practice spans painting, printmaking, and ornate figurative work, drawing on botany and psychedelia to produce colour-saturated imagery. She has maintained an active fine art exhibition career since at least 2014, placing with international galleries in the US and Australia and building a following of over 20,000 on Instagram.

## Style and Aesthetic

Marietta's signature is expressive figurative work centred on the modern woman, layered with botanical motifs and psychedelic visual language. Velvety colour palettes, ornate pattern details, and recurring imagery — bones, flora, eyes — distinguish her hand. Recurring themes across her practice include censorship, social normality, and the raw characteristics of human nature. Her Instagram bio, "Botanica ✷ Bones," distils her dual fascinations. Selected Instagram images suggest the same maximalist botanical-figurative language carries through her pattern and decorative work.

## Techniques and Tools

Marietta works primarily in original painting on wood round panels and canvas, screenprinting, and embellished and standard giclée inkjet printing. Hand-painted nesting sets — finished with high-gloss protective coating — are a signature product format. Die-cut stickers are a secondary output. Her practice is predominantly hand-made; no digital-only workflow is documented.

## Markets and Clients

Marietta's work has placed with international galleries and curatorial platforms: UHF Gallery (Venice, CA), Outré Gallery (Melbourne, VANGUARD 2024), SPOKE ART (San Francisco), BLOCKFORT Gallery (Columbus), and WOWxWOW (multiple group shows, 2019–2024). She sells original work, prints, and stickers direct-to-consumer via her website, and is open to commissions and client collaborations.

## Career Path

Marietta has been exhibiting professionally since at least 2014, with early shows at Denmark Gallery Columbus (solo), CS Gallery (Staff Pick), WWA Gallery Santa Monica, and SBC Gallery Palos Verdes. Solo exhibitions followed at BLOCKFORT Columbus (Unseen Splendor, 2019) and The Gallery at Gateway Film Center (Babe with the Power, 2017). She entered the international figurative underground through the Suggestivism shows curated by Nathan Spoor at Nucleus Gallery Portland (2019) and SPOKE ART San Francisco (2020), then built a sustained WOWxWOW presence from 2019 through 2024.

## Pattern Focus

Selected Instagram images suggest dense botanical illustration with ornate figurative composition — flowers, organic forms, and expressive mark-making. The work bridges fine art and decorative design. Repeating-pattern applications appear primarily in sticker, print, and applied decorative formats rather than standalone repeat designs.
