## Overview

Julieta Besse is an Argentine fashion designer and textile print designer working under Julieta Besse Studio. Based in Argentina, she works with brands and creative projects on fashion collection development, textile prints, embroidery, and international production. Her website references textile printing in India, indicating international supply chain experience. She targets womenswear and positions her practice around bespoke collection development and brand identity.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a fashion-forward print vocabulary with a contemporary approach. The work sits within womenswear territory, combining print design with garment context. Embroidery is positioned alongside print as a distinct offering, indicating facility with both surface approaches.

## Techniques and Tools

Julieta uses Procreate for digital illustration and print design (listed in skills). Her practice spans textile print design, embroidery design, and fashion collection development — with a stated focus on producing prints and collections with a strong brand identity.
