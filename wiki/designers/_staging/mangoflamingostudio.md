## Overview

Swati Chopra is the founder and creative director of Mango Flamingo Studio, an India-based print design studio supplying trend-led prints to fashion and lifestyle brands globally. With a background in graphic and fashion design, she founded the studio to combine both disciplines in a print-focused practice. The studio offers exclusive prints, non-exclusive library prints, bespoke commissions, and illustration services, delivered in vector, PSD, or client-specified formats.

## Style and Aesthetic

Mango Flamingo's prints are travel-inspired and nature-drawn, reflecting Chopra's personal observations from her travels. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest a vibrant, illustrated aesthetic with florals, botanicals, and decorative motifs well-positioned for resort-wear and lifestyle applications. The studio produces all-over and placement prints across a wide colour range.

## Techniques and Tools

Prints created by hand or digitally painted. Services include mood board development, colour palette creation, repeat construction, and illustration-to-repeat conversion. Finished files delivered in vector and PSD formats.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion (resort-wear, womenswear), home decor, gifting, stationery, textiles. Documented clients include Nautanky, The Print Pickle, Deepika Nagpal, Joge Lingerie, Jayanti Reddy, Affairs Label, Peixoto Wear, The Pink Elephant, and Africa-based clients. Products stocked through @theprintpickle.

## Career Path

Swati Chopra worked as a graphic and fashion designer before founding Mango Flamingo Studio to pursue print design as a combined art form. The studio now operates as a trend-informed design service for global clients.

## Pattern Focus

All-over and placement prints with a travel and nature-inspired palette. Resort-wear and fashion-forward florals and botanicals. Selected Instagram images suggest decorative illustrative motifs at multiple scales. Placement images show prints applied to garments and lifestyle product contexts.
