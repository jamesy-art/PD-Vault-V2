## Overview

Georgie Taylor is an artist, pattern designer, and graphic designer based in Australia (Perth), available for freelance pattern design, art licensing, graphic design, and commissions. Her work spans joyful, colourful compositions with a self-described "human-made feel" — from textiles and surface patterns to brand identity and custom painted artworks. She has documented experience designing for kidswear and textiles clients, and sells prints and greeting cards directly via her website.

## Style and Aesthetic

Colourful and expressive, with a hand-made quality — work intended to invite touch. Selected pattern images suggest vivid, painterly compositions with bold colour and an illustrative, non-geometric sensibility. The aesthetic bridges decorative pattern and graphic illustration, suited to apparel, lifestyle accessories, and brand-adjacent applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Works across custom painted artwork and digital graphic design. Skills include illustration, painting, graphic design, and pattern design. Comfortable with brand identity deliverables (custom logo fonts, icons, brand assets) alongside print collections and direct surface pattern applications. Produces prints and greeting cards for direct retail.

## Markets and Clients

Documented work includes: a textiles print collection designed for Fair Space; surface pattern design for phone cases; digital and print design for luxury beauty and menswear brands; and brand identity work for a premium sparkling water company and a yoga/pilates instructor. Open to pattern licensing, freelance graphic design, and custom commissions.
