## Overview

Laura Ortiz is a freelance print and graphic designer based in Valencia, Spain, with over eight years of experience working in the fashion industry. She operates from a home studio and has previously held in-house roles at fashion brands, suppliers, and print studios.

## Style and Aesthetic

Ortiz's visual signature is built around vivid, pop-influenced colour palettes with a strong affinity for florals and conversational prints. She describes "funny conversationals and colorful florals" as her core output. Travel—particularly to Asia—feeds her colour sensibility and compositional energy. Selected Instagram images suggest bold, saturated pattern work with expressive, energetic motifs.

## Techniques and Tools

Uses both hand-painted and digital techniques. Trained initially in PR, then completed a Design & Illustration Master Degree. Positions colour theory and detail-focused execution as key professional strengths.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion is the primary market, with womenswear as the stated category focus. Has worked with brands, suppliers, and print studios across her eight-year career. Offers bespoke print commissions and positions work as commercially effective for client sales performance.

## Career Path

A fashion brand internship approximately nine years ago identified print and pattern as her professional direction. Built an in-house career at fashion companies before transitioning to freelance. Now operates as a full-time freelancer from Valencia, often working while travelling—particularly in Asia.

## Pattern Focus

Selected Instagram images suggest bold floral repeats and conversational prints characterised by energetic, expressive colour. Work targets fashion applications, with womenswear as the core end market.
