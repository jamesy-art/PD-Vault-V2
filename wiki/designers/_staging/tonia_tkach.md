## Overview

Tonia Tkach is an illustrator and print designer originally from Ukraine, now based in Seattle, WA. She works across children's book illustration, seamless pattern design, commissioned projects, and stock illustration. Her Instagram following of 194,635 is notably large for an independent pattern designer, indicating substantial reach in the illustration and surface design community. Artwork is available via Creative Market, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, and Stocksy.

## Style and Aesthetic

Tkach's stated aesthetic interests are fine details, botanical beauty, and vintage charm — these are consistent with the types of seamless pattern work she describes. Selected Instagram images suggest richly detailed botanical and natural history subjects. The website bio confirms an orientation toward refined, decorative illustration as distinct from bold graphic pattern.

## Techniques and Tools

Works in both traditional and digital media. Traditional: watercolour, ink, and mixed media. Digital: vector and raster illustration. This dual capability positions her for both editorial illustration and commercial textile/surface pattern licensing. Portfolio separated into: Illustration, Patterns, and Black & White sections.

## Markets and Clients

Children's books: "Beans" (Polyandria publisher) and "Poems about Flowers" (MIF / Mann, Ivanov and Ferber). Stock illustration: Creative Market, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Stocksy. Takes commissioned projects. No commercial surface pattern licensing clients documented beyond stock platforms and book publishing.
