## Overview

Lanecia Prints (LANECIA STUDIO) is an artisanal surface design studio specialising in bespoke wallpaper and textile design for commercial spaces worldwide. The studio is founded by Vanessa Muñoz (@la.vanecia) alongside @paulaedg, and focuses on hand-crafted prints for restaurants, hotels, and other commercial clients. The Instagram account carries a verified badge with over 5,000 followers.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a portfolio spanning botanical illustration, geometric abstraction, and Victorian-inspired decorative motifs. Pattern names — FANTASIAS BOTANICAS, ROBLE VICTORIANO, LP VICTORIA — signal a signature blend of organic botanical themes and historical decorative reference. Work is characterised by tactile, handcrafted quality and strong visual presence suited to interior applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Prints are described as crafted by hand for a touch of uniqueness. Products are produced on premium non-woven wallpaper using water-based eco-solvent pigments. Standard roll format: 0.53 × 10m, covering 5sqm. Applied via glue-primer system.

## Markets and Clients

Primary market is commercial interior design — restaurants, hotels, and commercial spaces. The studio offers bespoke brand-DNA-to-art translation services for commercial clients. Worldwide project capability is documented. Products are sold at $250–$300 per pattern roll.
