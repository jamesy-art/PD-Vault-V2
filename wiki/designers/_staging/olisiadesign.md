## Overview

Alessandra Medeiros is the founder of Olisia Design, a Brazilian surface pattern studio specialising in print development for the fashion industry. With over 10 years of experience, she offers briefing development, pattern creation, colourway proposals, print-ready files, and surface techniques as a design service to fashion brands. Trained in Fashion Design at IFSC and post-graduated in Fashion Business Management and Marketing at SENAC, Brazil.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected images suggest a vibrant, fashion-forward aesthetic — pattern work calibrated for womenswear and contemporary fashion collections. Colour is central: the studio positions itself around "creating emotions in colours and prints."

## Techniques and Tools

Full print development pipeline: briefing interpretation, pattern creation, colourway proposals, and print-ready technical files. Available on [[Patternbank]] and Five Points Graphics for direct licensing. Ambassador for Audaces, the Brazilian fashion technology platform.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion industry — primarily womenswear. Studio services available for brand-level pattern development from briefing through to print-ready output. Patterns accessible for licensing via Patternbank and Five Points Graphics.

## Career Path

Graduated in Fashion Design from IFSC. Post-graduated in Fashion Business Management and Marketing at SENAC, Brazil. Founded Olisia Design as a dedicated surface pattern studio after 10+ years of industry experience.

## Pattern Focus

Fashion print development — patterns designed for apparel with attention to colourway, print readiness, and surface technique. Studio offers both licensing and bespoke development services to fashion brands.
