## Overview

Mini Magique Studio is a UK-based print and illustration studio specialising in the baby and children's design market, led by Genine Delahaye. Based in Dumfries and Galloway, Scotland, the studio operates across two strands: commissioned print and illustration work under Genine Delahaye's name, and Mini Magique Studio, which offers pre-designed prints available for immediate purchase. The studio has worked with high street and global retailers across more than 15 years.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a children's print vocabulary: playful character-driven illustrations, pattern-based prints, and product applications typical of the baby and younger markets. The aesthetic is commercial, age-appropriate, and market-aware, covering multiple product categories within the children's sector.

## Techniques and Tools

Illustration, graphic design, pattern design, trend analysis, and mood board preparation. Trend direction is a documented service alongside print and illustration, reflecting a full-service offering for children's market clients.

## Markets and Clients

Documented market coverage: clothing/fashion, kids books, gift and greeting, bolt fabric, and home and nursery — all within the baby and younger markets. The website references "a multitude of highstreet and global retailers" as past clients; specific names are not disclosed on the public site.

## Career Path

Genine Delahaye has worked as a freelance children's illustrator and print designer for over 15 years, serving the children's industry across multiple product categories. Mini Magique Studio was developed as a secondary channel offering pre-designed prints for immediate licensing purchase.

## Pattern Focus

Selected Instagram pattern images suggest colourful, character-led children's prints suitable for apparel and product applications. Selected placement images show designs applied to garments and products consistent with the baby and kidswear market.
