## Overview

Anna Jones is an Edinburgh-based illustrator working as Miss Jones Draws, represented by The Bright Agency. She studied Illustration and Graphic Design in Liverpool, graduating in 1999, before working as an in-house designer at a London greetings card company and subsequently going freelance. Her client list spans major children's publishing houses in the UK and US, and her work extends into greetings cards and craft fairs alongside editorial and licensed publishing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Jones works in a colourful, character-driven illustrative style suited to children's publishing and licensed products. Selected Instagram images (4 patterns, 2 placements) suggest playful, warm visual language — animated animal characters and seasonal or nature-themed compositions consistent with children's book and greetings card formats.

## Techniques and Tools

Illustration and graphic design background. Works in digital illustration for publishing, licensing, and greetings card production. Craft fair attendance also documented alongside online sales.

## Markets and Clients

Magazine: Highlights. Greetings cards: Paperlink, Egmont. Additional: craft fairs and online sales. Represented by The Bright Agency.
