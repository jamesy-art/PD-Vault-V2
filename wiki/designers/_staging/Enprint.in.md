## Overview

Enprint is an India-based textile and surface pattern design studio founded by Kalyani A, offering cloud-based design services to brands, manufacturers, and exporters in the textile industry. The studio creates custom patterns across wallpaper, home furnishings, garments, and fabrics, and is a documented featured artist partner with Luxe Walls — a leading Australian removable wallpaper company.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images (8 approved patterns) suggest a commercially oriented surface pattern practice spanning traditional and contemporary motifs. The website references a Paisley Collection and a Prickly Collection, indicating range across heritage-influenced and graphic contemporary print styles. Output is market-ready and production-focused.

## Techniques and Tools

Cloud-based digital design production. The studio positions itself as a professional design service converting client briefs into production-ready artwork, with a team of designers. Enprint offers a freelance service alongside its product collections, and the Luxe Walls collaboration confirms wallpaper-format repeat design capability.

## Markets and Clients

Documented clients: Luxe Walls (Australia — removable wallpaper, featured artist at luxewalls.com.au), Arden Home and Living (home furnishing), La Terzi (fabric/apparel), and unnamed garment companies. Market coverage spans wallpaper, home furnishing, apparel, and fabrics. The B2B positioning serves brands, manufacturers, and exporters directly.

Enprint was established as an independent textile design studio offering cloud-based design services to the trade. Instagram activity confirms an ongoing freelance services practice with active collaboration invitations.

## Pattern Focus

Eight selected Instagram images confirm a surface and textile pattern practice spanning multiple categories. The studio offers named collections (Paisley, Prickly) alongside bespoke client services. Pattern work is commercially oriented and production-ready rather than experimental.
