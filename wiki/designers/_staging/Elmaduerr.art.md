## Overview

Elma Duerr (known as Edie) is a Minneapolis-based illustrator and map artist operating under TartStudio LLC. She specialises in whimsical illustrated maps of cities and places, alongside decorative illustration. Commissions are available for personalised map artwork; she also sells posters, prints, and banners from her website.

## Style and Aesthetic

Elma's work is warmly illustrative and character-led — hand-drawn maps of cities (Minneapolis, Italy) rendered with charm and detail, alongside decorative animal and domestic scene pieces. Selected placement images suggest colourful illustrated townscapes, black-and-white swan illustration, and cat-in-chair decorative prints. The aesthetic is whimsical and personal rather than production-pattern-oriented.

## Techniques and Tools

Elma works primarily on iPad in Procreate, and also draws on paper. Her website cites "personalized watercolor & gouache map illustration commissions" as a specialty alongside digital Procreate work.

## Markets and Clients

Her primary market is commissioned personalised map art — city maps, travel souvenirs, and gift prints. She also produces standalone decorative illustration for print-and-poster retail. Selected placements suggest framed art print and poster formats.

## Career Path

Elma works as an independent illustrator and map artist under TartStudio LLC in Minneapolis. No formal education history documented. Her self-described inspiration comes from "surroundings, daydreams, and the occasional talking swan."
