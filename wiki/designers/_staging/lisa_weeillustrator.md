## Overview

Lisa Wee is a Singapore-based illustrator and children's book creator, represented by Amy Miligan at Illo Agency. Her practice centres on inclusive, diversity-focused illustration for children, drawing on a multicultural background spanning Penang, Singapore, and Kenya. She has received multiple international illustration awards and is open for commissions.

## Style and Aesthetic

Vibrant, inclusive, and character-driven. The stated creative identity — "Inclusive stories, joyful colors, and kids who belong everywhere" — defines both the visual and thematic tone. Work is described as "quint, quirky and vibrant," with a strong diversity and inclusivity focus. Selected Instagram patterns suggest bold, graphic, high-chroma illustration work with a playful character. Selected placement images indicate work in children's book and editorial contexts.
