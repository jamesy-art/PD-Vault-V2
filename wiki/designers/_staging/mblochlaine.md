## Overview

Marie Bloch-Lainé is a French-born illustrator and surface pattern designer based in Chicago, with career experience across Montreal and Chicago. Her practice draws on a background in illustration and graphic design alongside a deep affinity for traditional print-making techniques. Named collections include Autumn in the Backyard, Court of Hearts, Merry Reverie, Wild Wonders of the Midwest, Bold Florals, and Imperfect Beauties. A full pattern and illustration directory is available for licensing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Bloch-Lainé's aesthetic is bohemian, nostalgic, and characterised by rich textures, spontaneous brushstrokes, and earthy colour palettes. Selected Instagram images suggest surface patterns with an organic, handmade quality — loose, imperfect mark-making over clean digital precision. The work is described as "modern and approachable," with a clear commercial fit for stationery and home goods.

## Techniques and Tools

Rooted in traditional print-making sensibilities. Works as both illustrator and graphic designer. Available for projects, collaborations, custom work, and licensing. Full illustration and pattern directory available on request.

## Markets and Clients

Primary markets: stationery and home goods. Available for licensing, custom commissions, and collaborations. No documented brand clients at this time.
