## Overview

Aneri Jariwala is an artist, illustrator, and surface designer from Gujarat, India, currently pursuing a BFA in Illustration at the Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD), expected graduation 2027. Based in Savannah, GA, she works across digital and traditional media, drawing on cultural heritage, personal experience, and natural observation as primary sources.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a vibrant, detail-rich style that blends cultural motifs with botanical and organic forms. Colour palettes appear warm and expressive. Her work draws on South Asian visual traditions alongside observational work from the natural world, resulting in designs that feel both culturally rooted and broadly accessible.

## Techniques and Tools

Works across digital and traditional media, adapting the medium to the project. Comfortable with a range from hand-rendered to digital processes; specific software not documented in available sources.

## Markets and Clients

Open to freelance, art licensing, and in-house collaborations. As a current SCAD student, no documented commercial clients at time of enrichment. Positioned for entry-level licensing and surface design work on graduation.
