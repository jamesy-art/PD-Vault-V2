## Overview

Santiago-based illustrator and graphic designer working in digital botanical illustration. Born in Colombia, Andrea Muñoz relocated to Chile over six years ago, where the country's flora and landscape have shaped her practice. She creates illustrations for brand packaging, campaigns, products, patterns, and visual identity.

## Style and Aesthetic

Soft, clean botanical illustration with a nature-led palette. Selected Instagram patterns suggest detailed floral compositions in muted, warm colourways — a visual language described as "calm and beautiful." Style sits in the space between botanical fine art and commercial brand illustration.

## Techniques and Tools

Primarily digital illustration; also works in pencil. Process begins with close observation of flowers and natural forms, developed into digital compositions. Delivers illustration for packaging, campaigns, products, patterns, and brand identity.

## Markets and Clients

Independent brands seeking botanical illustration for packaging, campaigns, and visual identity. Open to commissions and collaborations. Selected placement images suggest product mock-up and branding applications.

## Portfolio and Presence

- Website: andreamu.com
- Instagram: @Andrea.muart (707 followers, 185 posts)

## Career Path

Trained in graphic design in Colombia before relocating to Chile over six years ago. Developed an independent illustration practice, exploring botanical illustration as a way to connect art with everyday life. Works on freelance commissions and personal projects from Santiago.

## Pattern Focus

Botanical and floral illustration with pattern applications. Motifs draw directly from flowers and natural forms observed in Chilean flora. Selected patterns suggest all-over floral compositions; pattern work is positioned as one output of a broader brand illustration practice.
