## Overview

Jelly Bean Studio is Aysha Zreika's Barcelona-based print and illustration studio. Originally from Lebanon and raised in Barcelona, Aysha brings over a decade of fashion industry experience to her studio practice, including collaborations with fashion brands and the launch of her own womenswear label. The studio produces bold, playful, colour-forward print designs for licensing and custom development across apparel, accessories, and home décor.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest vibrant, character-driven surface patterns with a strong illustrative sensibility. The studio's positioning — "playful prints for creative brands / Kidswear & Lifestyle" — is reflected in bold colour choices and narrative visual content. Placement images from the review set indicate product applications in apparel or accessories contexts. The work is designed for commercial use across multiple categories.

## Techniques and Tools

Aysha studied both digital and traditional illustration formally in art school. Her studio practice draws on this dual training to produce prints suited to physical and digital reproduction. Custom print development is available from single prints to full collections, with colour and scale adjustment for client needs.

## Markets and Clients

Primary markets: kidswear, womenswear, menswear, home décor, and lifestyle products. Named collaborator: The Animals Observatory (Barcelona-based childrenswear brand). The studio offers three service modes: non-exclusive catalogue licensing, custom print development, and brand collaboration. A password-protected online catalogue is available to trade buyers on request.

## Career Path

Aysha Zreika has drawn since childhood and studied digital and traditional illustration formally. She worked in the fashion industry for over 10 years, collaborating with brands including The Animals Observatory and launching her own womenswear label. She founded Jelly Bean Studio to channel this experience into a dedicated print and illustration licensing operation.
