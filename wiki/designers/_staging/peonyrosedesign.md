Nicola Allen is a UK-based illustrator and designer working as Peony Rose Design, based on the Sussex Coast. She holds a BA (Hons) in Graphic Design and Advertising and has built a commercially published portfolio spanning greetings cards, surface patterns, and fine art. Her work has been published worldwide and she is represented by Advocate Art.
