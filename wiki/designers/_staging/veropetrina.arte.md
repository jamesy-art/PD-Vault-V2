## Overview

Veronica Petrina is a Buenos Aires-based watercolor artist, illustrator, and teacher working under the handle @veropetrina.arte. With a background in clothing design and visual art, she has built a recognised practice in botanical watercolor with a following of over 34,000 on Instagram (verified). She teaches via recorded courses and live Zoom classes, sells original paintings and art supply kits, and is open to brand collaboration and print/pattern licensing.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram patterns suggest a botanical and floral focus — delicate watercolor compositions featuring flowers, plants, and natural motifs. The palette tends to the soft, luminous, and botanically detailed, consistent with a classical watercolor approach applied to surface pattern and illustration. Work reflects careful observation of natural forms and a fine art sensibility grounded in direct botanical study.

## Techniques and Tools

Watercolor on 100% cotton Canson paper (documented). Teaches from beginner technique through botanical composition, colour theory, and abstract painting. Annual guided course structure indicates consistent, long-form teaching practice. Original paintings are worked on imported cotton paper; courses cover watercolor fundamentals, botanical floral work, and mixed technique.
