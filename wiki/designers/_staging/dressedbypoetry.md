## Overview

Caroline Falasca is an Auckland-based illustrator and multidisciplinary artist working under the name Dressed by Poetry. The project is a tactile extension of her illustration practice — handcrafted objects including illustrated garments, bookmarks, resin pieces, and painted textiles made primarily from reclaimed and second-hand materials. It sits at the boundary of fine art and wearable object-making rather than commercial surface pattern.

## Style and Aesthetic

Quiet, literary, and memory-driven. Work blends digital illustration, hand lettering, and material experimentation, with natural forms, emotional narrative, and imperfection as recurring themes. Selected Instagram placements (4) suggest intimate, object-based applications — illustrated items and wearable pieces with a handcrafted presence. One selected pattern image suggests a surface-applicable illustrated composition.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital illustration, hand lettering, resin casting, and painting on fabric and reclaimed materials. Works almost exclusively with second-hand and repurposed items. Skills include illustration, painting, hand lettering, and graphic design.

## Markets and Clients

Primarily direct-to-consumer and commission-based via Instagram. Not currently positioned for trade licensing. Target audience is buyers of original handcrafted work and bespoke commissions.

## Portfolio and Presence

- Website: carolinefalasca.com/dressed-by-poetry
- Instagram: @dressedbypoetry (17 posts, ~92 followers, Auckland NZ)

## Career Path
