## Overview

Lhais Munhoz is a textile designer and illustrator based in São Paulo, Brazil. Most of her career has been developed in-house at Cia Hering, one of Brazil's largest clothing brands and retailers, in the Kidswear Division. Her practice centres on storytelling through drawing — described in her own words as telling "stories through drawings" and creating "imaginary worlds to dress kids."

## Style and Aesthetic

Narrative and character-led. Selected Instagram images suggest diverse visual languages explored across Hering Kidswear seasons — illustrative motifs, character-based designs, and pattern compositions suited to children's apparel. The aesthetic is story-driven, expressive, and varied in register.

Pattern design and illustration. Specific tools not documented.
