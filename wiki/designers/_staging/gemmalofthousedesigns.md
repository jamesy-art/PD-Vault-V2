## Overview

Gemma Helm is a UK-based (Cornwall) freelance surface pattern, textile, and tile designer operating as Gemma Lofthouse Designs. She works across commissions, collaborations, design team support, and her own GLD Pattern Library — a catalogue of ready-to-purchase exclusive pattern designs. Services span surface pattern, textile design and material sourcing (print, embroidery, wovens), tile design, trend research and colour analysis, and creative direction for brands in the homewares and interiors sector.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images suggest a commercially versatile design range positioned for the interiors and homewares market — trend-led or brand-led, tailored per client brief. The studio's stated positioning emphasises innovation within a client-responsive framework: delivering designs suited to homewares, stationery, fashion, and tile applications across a range of aesthetics.

## Techniques and Tools

Designs for print, embroidery, and wovens. Experienced in tile formats — printed, hand-painted, encaustic, ceramic, and porcelain — across small and large formats including border tiles, repeating wallpaper tiles, and floor tiles. Textile work includes material sourcing from UK, European, and Asian suppliers and mills, with documented experience in upholstery product design and sourcing. Skills include painting, colour theory, trend awareness, and client collaboration.

## Markets and Clients

Primary focus: homewares, interiors, and textiles. Secondary: apparel, stationery, and tile design. Offers design team support on a temporary or ongoing basis for studios and in-house teams. Non-exclusive designs available for smaller businesses via Etsy; exclusive designs through the GLD Pattern Library. Open to creative direction and consultation for new brand launches and collection development.
