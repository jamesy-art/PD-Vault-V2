## Overview

Anuja Dhawale is a self-taught surface pattern designer based in Canada, working under the brand Color Voyage Designs. With roots in India, she creates hand-painted patterns for home and lifestyle markets, working across acrylic, watercolour, and digital illustration. Her catalogue spans wallpaper and fabric, available through her website and [[Spoonflower]].

## Style and Aesthetic

Anuja's work blends classic elegance with modern charm through delicate florals, loose geometrics, and carefully curated colour palettes. Selected Instagram patterns suggest painterly qualities — organic motif placement and visible hand-worked character translated into repeat structures. Selected placement images indicate fabric and home textile applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Multi-media practice spanning acrylic painting, watercolour, and digital illustration. Physical and digital media are both integrated into her working process.

## Markets and Clients

Home decor — wallpaper and fabric. Seeks collaboration with brands creating distinctive, timeless home and lifestyle products. Also active on [[Spoonflower]] for direct consumer sales.

## Career Path

Self-taught, with a creative background originating in India and developing in Canada. Her practice has moved through multiple media — acrylic, watercolour, digital illustration — converging on surface pattern design for the home market.

## Pattern Focus

Delicate florals and loose geometrics with a hand-painted quality. Selected Instagram patterns suggest a range from painterly botanical repeats to structured geometric compositions, unified by warm, thoughtfully curated colour palettes.
