## Overview

Daniela Vargas is a Colombian illustrator and surface pattern designer specialising in botanical and tropical motifs. She creates seamless patterns and illustration work for licensing, with applications including stickers, notebooks, totes, wall art, fabric, and print. Her work is available for licensing and direct collaboration.

## Style and Aesthetic

Daniela's practice centres on natural, colourful imagery — botanical and tropical forms rendered with a distinctive, confident visual style. Her Instagram bio describes her specialism as pattern and surface design with a natural and colourful focus. Selected Instagram patterns suggest vibrant, detail-rich botanical and tropical repeats with rich, expressive colour palettes.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital illustration, with a specialism in botanical and tropical subject matter. Her website frames the practice as illustration-led — spanning pattern design, book covers, prints, and editorial projects.

## Markets and Clients

Licensing and collaboration targeting brands producing stickers, notebooks, totes, wall art, fabric, and decorative products. Her website includes a licensing portfolio with direct contact for inquiries.
