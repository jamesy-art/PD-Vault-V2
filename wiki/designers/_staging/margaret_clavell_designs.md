## Overview

Margaret Clavell is a surface pattern designer based in Gloucestershire, UK. She designs florals and graphic prints for licensing, selling through Spoonflower and her own website. Her work covers wallpaper, fabric, stationery, and accessories, with licensing enquiries welcomed.

## Style and Aesthetic

Clavell's work draws primarily from nature and is openly influenced by [[William Morris]]. Florals dominate her portfolio — stylised flowers and leaves in tossed repeat arrangements across both muted and bright colourways. Named collections include Fern Leaf, Buttercup (scallop and standard), Water Lily, and Bluebell wallpapers. Selected Instagram pattern images suggest well-constructed floral repeats with botanical motifs at varying scales. Alongside her florals, she produces a more graphic print style suited to stationery and gift wrap.

## Techniques and Tools

Adobe Illustrator and Procreate. She also experiments with other media. A keen landscape photographer, her observational practice informs her approach to natural subjects.

## Markets and Clients

Wallpaper, fabric, home decor, stationery (notebooks, greeting cards, gift wrap), accessories. Prints available for licensing through Spoonflower; finished accessories on Etsy. Open to direct licensing discussions.
