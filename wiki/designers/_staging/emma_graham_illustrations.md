## Overview

Emma Graham is an illustrator based outside Ipswich, Suffolk, UK, working across children's book illustration, pattern, and decorative print. She takes commissions from worldwide clients and describes her work as hand-drawn in acrylic inks, collage, and some digital work. Her style has been compared to Beatrix Potter in its whimsical, nature-led character.

## Style and Aesthetic

Emma's visual signature is warm, narrative, and rooted in the English countryside — animals, flora, and pastoral scenes rendered with a hand-crafted, textured quality. Selected Instagram images suggest illustrative surface patterns with a storybook aesthetic — layered, texture-rich compositions consistent with her acrylic ink and collage methods. The work is nostalgic and charming in character.

## Techniques and Tools

Emma works primarily in hand-drawn acrylic inks and collage, with some digital work supplementing the handmade core. Her personal interests — beachcombing, mudlarking, tree hugging, wild swimming — directly inform the nature-led content of her illustrations.

## Markets and Clients

Emma works with worldwide clients across illustration and design commissions. Her primary output categories are children's book illustration and decorative pattern. She is open to commissions and can be contacted directly via her website or phone.
