## Overview

Andrea Barrientos is the founder of Seesu Patterns, a pattern licensing platform offering exclusive and semi-exclusive prints for brands and product developers. The platform targets fashion brands, textile manufacturers, industrial designers, and entrepreneurs seeking distinctive, non-commercial prints. Patterns are available for digital download via seesupatterns.com, with licensing structured in semi-exclusive (3 downloads) and exclusive (1 download) tiers.

## Style and Aesthetic

Selected Instagram images suggest a commercially varied print library. Named patterns include Mint Drift, Nude Flowers, Sweet Butterflies, Olive Tiger, Terra Dots, Sacred Roots, Painted Clay, and Goldie Leopard — spanning florals, animal prints (tiger, leopard), butterfly motifs, abstract dot compositions, and earth-toned organic designs. The range targets womenswear, swimwear, and lifestyle product applications.

## Techniques and Tools

Digital pattern design with print-ready file delivery. Patterns supplied as ZIP downloads formatted for direct printing. Seesu documents fabric sublimation samples — including silk sublimation for pyjamas — as proof-of-product demonstrations for buyers.

## Markets and Clients

Fashion brands (apparel, swimwear, dresses, pants), textile industry (retail and wholesale fabric), industrial designers, and independent product brands. Applications include clothing, stationery, sportswear, planners, backpacks, and t-shirts. Global payments via Tilopay platform (VISA, Mastercard, Amex). Licensing in semi-exclusive and exclusive tiers.

## Portfolio and Presence

- Website: seesupatterns.com
- Instagram: @see.su.patterns

## Career Path

Andrea began as a retail entrepreneur in 2014. In 2018, she launched a luxury sleepwear brand, teaching herself fashion design, sewing, and textile management. The need to source unique, exclusive fabrics for the sleepwear line led to founding Seesu as a dedicated pattern licensing platform. Pattern design became her primary business.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns cover a broad commercial range: florals (Nude Flowers), animal prints (Olive Tiger, Goldie Leopard), insects (Sweet Butterflies), abstract (Mint Drift, Terra Dots, Painted Clay), and botanical (Sacred Roots). Positioned for womenswear, swimwear, and lifestyle product buyers seeking exclusive or semi-exclusive print rights.
