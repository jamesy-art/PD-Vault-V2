## Overview

Laura Brumana is a Milan-based print and fashion designer with a background in fine art painting. She graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts in Milan and built a career designing embroideries and prints for Italian and international fashion brands, with a focus on feminine and minimal womenswear collections. Her documented client list spans multiple seasons across Italian and international houses.

## Style and Aesthetic

Brumana's practice centres on feminine, minimal print design for womenswear and accessories. Selected Instagram images suggest geometric and abstract pattern directions, with possible influence from her stated admiration for Sonia Delaunay — bold colour blocking and structured compositions. A 2023 portfolio project explicitly cites Delaunay as inspiration. Work has been applied to total look womenswear campaigns.

## Techniques and Tools

Trained in painting at the Academy of Fine Arts, Milan. Career work covers embroidery design, print design, fabric development, jacquards, laces, and embellishments. Experience with leather, technical fabrics, and outerwear materials alongside traditional womenswear. Proficient in illustration and painting; Adobe Suite implied by professional practice.

## Markets and Clients

Primary market is women's fashion. Documented clients include Caractère (A/W 2011, S/S 2011, A/W 2012), GAS Jeans, Baldinini, Max Mara, DESA, BREKKA, ADD Milano, Gheef, Massimo Dutti, and Guy Laroche. Focus on Italian and international fashion houses.

## Career Path

Brumana studied painting at the Academy of Fine Arts in Milan, then moved into fashion print design. She built her career designing embroideries and prints for Italian and international companies, developing expertise in feminine, minimal womenswear and outerwear fabric development. Portfolio documents seasonal work for multiple prestigious clients.

## Pattern Focus

Documented output spans womenswear total look collections across multiple seasons. A 2023 project inspired by Sonia Delaunay confirms continuing interest in bold geometric and colour-field abstraction. Work encompasses embroidery, lace, and jacquard development alongside flat surface prints.
