## Overview

Tracy Schifeling is a print and pattern designer based in Denver, CO, working under her own name. She designs colourful stationery and home goods — greeting cards, patterned tea towels, napkins, serving trays, and original pattern art — sold directly through her website. A distinctive process signature: all patterns begin as hand-cut paper collages and hand-painted compositions before being resolved into final designs. Instagram bio: "Print and pattern artist designing colorful stationery and home goods."

## Style and Aesthetic

Schifeling's work is characterised by bold, graphic colour and a hand-crafted starting point. Selected Instagram images confirm a rich palette — florals, lattice structures, geometric vases, and folk-influenced motifs rendered with layered colour and hand-made texture. Recurring colour signatures include golden yellow, navy, orchid, periwinkle, sage green, and olive. The visual language bridges hand-made warmth with graphic pattern clarity, suited to stationery and home goods.

## Techniques and Tools

Distinctive process: all patterns originate from paper cutout collages and hand-painting. Card designs include hand-cut paper shapes arranged "petal by petal" before resolution. This analogue starting point is the stated core of her design identity. No digital tools specified in available sources.
