## Overview

Shelby Cornwell is a Midwest US-based surface pattern designer and founder of Feral Flower Design Studio. Her practice focuses on vintage-inspired fabric prints, wallpaper patterns, and illustrated textile designs. She is a certified surface pattern designer and graduate of Bonnie Christine's Immersion course, and is open to licensing and brand collaborations.

## Style and Aesthetic

Cornwell's work centres on soft, painterly surface patterns inspired by vintage florals, nostalgia, and the quiet beauty of domestic life. Her aesthetic is described as timeless and heirloom-in-quality — a cottagecore sensibility grounded in careful craft. Checked image table entries (all pattern-selected) suggest floral compositions with a hand-painted, romantic quality and restrained, warm colour palettes.

## Techniques and Tools

Painterly surface pattern techniques with soft colour palettes and a handmade touch. Certified surface pattern designer; graduate of Bonnie Christine's Immersion programme. Work spans fabric, wallpaper, and illustrated textile formats. Licensing basics listed as a declared skill.

## Markets and Clients

Home décor is the primary target market. Open to licensing opportunities, brand collaborations, and commissions. Portfolio collections include named groups: In Her Garden, For Her Always, Framed in Rose, Linger Leaf, Trellis Bud, Rosewood Bloom, and a Twilight series — suggesting a coordinated collections approach suitable for licensing.

Midwest-based designer who trained through Bonnie Christine's Immersion programme and established Feral Flower Design Studio as her independent practice. Her studio is oriented toward building a licensing-ready collection portfolio.

Vintage floral and botanical repeat patterns with a painterly, soft-colour aesthetic. Work is positioned toward fabric, wallpaper, and home textile markets. Coordinated collection groupings indicate a developed approach to licensing-ready portfolio building.
