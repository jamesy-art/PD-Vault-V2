## Overview

Anis is a Germany-based surface pattern designer and illustrator specialising in botanical prints. She has worked in pattern design for seven years, building a portfolio of hundreds of seamless botanical designs aimed at home, gift, and occasion markets. Her practice is described as merging painting and botany — a childhood passion for plants converted into a commercial surface design career.

## Style and Aesthetic

Anis works in a soft, nature-driven palette of pastels and neutrals, consistently anchored by bold black line work for contrast. Selected Instagram images suggest detailed floral and botanical patterns with a painterly, organic quality. The combination of warm pastel grounds with structured black outlines gives her work legible appeal suited to decorative product applications.
