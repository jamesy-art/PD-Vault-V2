## Overview

Guy Megides Studio is an Israeli textile design studio founded in 2024, creating handmade colourful products for the home and everyday life. Founded by textile designer and illustrator Guy Megides — a Shenkar College of Arts graduate and former co-founder of Studio KipKop — the studio is built around printed polyester mesh as a signature material, producing tote bags, table runners, and accessories. Geffen Layhani works alongside Guy as creative and management consultant. The studio has shown at Fresh Paint Art Fair 2025 and received coverage from Designboom, Ynet, El-Al Atmosphere Magazine, and Design Museum Holon.

## Style and Aesthetic

Vibrant, playful, and optimistic. The studio sees "colour as a way of experiencing and shaping the world — vibrant yet gentle, thoughtful yet spontaneous." Selected pattern images suggest bold, graphic compositions with strong colour contrast and rhythmic repetition — a contemporary character rooted in illustration and drawing. Work balances joyful expression with precision and considered production.

## Techniques and Tools

Products are designed and tested by hand. The studio's signature material is printed polyester mesh — valued for its transparency, lightness, and durability, and cited as the basis of its distinctive product character. Each piece originates as a print or drawing and evolves through experimentation and play before finalisation. All goods are handmade in-studio.

## Markets and Clients

Home and everyday lifestyle accessories sold direct-to-consumer via an e-commerce storefront. Current product range: tote bags (named collections including Bauhaus, Michelle, Maya, Sarah, Lemon), table runners, fish charm keychains, fish-shaped bags, and origami boat charms. Ships worldwide; pricing in Israeli NIS. Selected placement images suggest lifestyle and product styling contexts.

## Career Path

Guy Megides studied Textile Design at Shenkar College of Arts in Israel. She co-founded Studio KipKop with artist Tamar Yogev, working on large-scale public art projects — murals in Tel Aviv's Neve Shaanan neighbourhood and immersive installations at the Imperial Hotel. After years of designing for clients and brands, she founded Guy Megides Studio in 2024, focused on her own handmade product label.

## Pattern Focus

Colourful, graphic repeat prints applied to transparent polyester mesh textiles. Selected patterns suggest bold, rhythmic compositions with strong colour play — consistent with a studio practice rooted in illustration and drawing. The design language bridges fine art printmaking and decorative textile, applied to lifestyle accessories and home products.
