## Overview

Saeideh Ghanbari is a surface pattern designer based in Düsseldorf, Germany, specialising in seamless print design for fashion, textiles, and home decor. She describes her creative process as rooted in curiosity and experimentation across pattern, colour, and composition, with a deep inspiration from nature. Her collections are positioned for commercial licensing and collaboration.

## Style and Aesthetic

Nature-inspired, with a modern and production-ready sensibility. Work is described as having balanced colours and strong commercial appeal. Selected Instagram images suggest a clean, structured approach to print design with natural motifs — consistent with her stated focus on seamless prints for fashion and textile. The portfolio spans textile surface patterns and wallpaper designs.

## Techniques and Tools

Trained as a textile designer with a focus on print design. Works digitally. Output includes fashion-facing textile prints and wallpaper-ready surface patterns.

## Markets and Clients

Positioned for fashion, textiles, home decor, and wallpaper markets. Collections available for licensing and collaboration. Based in Germany; open to international enquiry.

## Career Path

Trained as a textile designer with a print specialisation. Now based in Düsseldorf, Germany, building a commercial licensing portfolio across fashion, textile, home decor, and wallpaper.

## Pattern Focus

Seamless prints informed by nature and an experimental approach to colour and composition. Modern, commercially oriented output. Selected patterns suggest considered colour harmony and structured repeat construction. Wallpaper and fashion/textile both documented as target applications.
