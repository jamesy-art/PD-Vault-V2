## Overview

Bloomery Prints is a Netherlands-based surface pattern and textile design studio serving global women’s wear, swimwear, and home décor brands. The studio specialises in sophisticated, feminine prints with a vintage and bohemian luxe character, releasing new designs weekly as seamless, production-ready patterns available on both exclusive and non-exclusive terms.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio’s aesthetic centres on a vintage and bohemian luxe sensibility. The collection spans hand-painted florals, intricate paisley motifs, watercolour tropical compositions, digital collages, and abstract artwork — a range positioned for brands seeking artisanal and distinctive print work with a rich, feminine character.

## Markets and Specialties

Women’s wear, swimwear, and home décor. Both exclusive and non-exclusive licensing options are offered.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Bloomery Prints works across multiple media including hand-painted florals, watercolour tropicals, digital collage, and abstract digital artwork. All patterns are supplied as seamless, production-ready files. The shop is updated weekly with new designs.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio sells directly via its own print shop (also branded as Saltbox Printhouse) with exclusive and non-exclusive purchasing options. No specific client names are disclosed.

## Studio Background

Bloomery Prints is based in the Netherlands and operates as an independent boutique print studio. The studio has built a consistent print vocabulary around vintage, bohemian, and artisanal design traditions.

## Pattern Focus

Hand-painted florals, paisley, watercolour tropicals, digital collages, and abstract prints, all delivered as seamless production-ready files. Women’s wear, swimwear, and home décor are the primary markets.
