## Overview

SOGÉ STUDIO is a Brooklyn, NY based textile design studio specializing in apparel, home, and accessories prints. Founded by Brooklyn-born, Fashion Institute of Technology alumna Stephanie Ogé, the studio is led by an ethnically diverse in-house team and has been featured in WGSN multiple times.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The portfolio shows a consistent identity built on painterly, gestural florals — loose brushstroke botanicals, watercolor-style abstract blooms, and layered silhouette-and-foliage motifs — alongside more structured work such as an ornate, tile-bordered floral panel and a small-scale ditsy floral. Colorways range from soft pastel and blush tones to jewel-toned emerald, sapphire, and gold, consistent with the studio's stated "color intuition" and "fluidity of artwork."

## Markets and Specialties

- home
- apparel
- accessories

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Pattern imagery reflects a mix of hand-painted/gestural brushwork, watercolor florals, and digitally rendered silhouette and mosaic-style motifs, consistent with the studio's description of blending "mixed media, hand illustration and digital techniques."

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

SOGÉ STUDIO positions itself as a premium, trend-setting print source for apparel, home, and accessories brands, citing repeated features in WGSN as evidence of trade recognition.

## Studio Background

Founded by Stephanie Ogé, a Brooklyn-born, Fashion Institute of Technology and Polimoda (Florence) alumna who worked for nearly a decade as a sportswear fashion designer in New York City before founding the studio. A trip to Jaipur, India, exposing her to indigenous natural dyeing and block-printing techniques, is credited as a turning point that drove her to pursue textile design full time. She leads a small in-house team of designers.

## Pattern Focus

Painterly and watercolor florals, gestural brushstroke botanicals, and silhouette-based floral motifs, spanning soft pastel to jewel-toned colorways, with occasional ornate tile/mosaic-bordered and ditsy-scale florals.
