## Overview

Found is a London print design studio based in Hackney Wick, led by Sophie Dakin, who brings 16 years of international sales and trend-direction experience to the studio's seasonal print and colour direction. A team of in-house designers produces a rotating, weekly slate of exclusive designs for the womenswear, menswear, kids, active, and swim markets. Fabric artwork is printed in the UK.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The portfolio shows a deliberately broad stylistic range rather than one house look: bold, linework-driven tropical botanicals in saturated colour blocking; loose, traditional hand-painted florals in soft archive-style palettes; tribal-influenced maximalist florals layered over geometric motifs; ditsy and abstract dot-scatter repeats; and an engineered, jacquard-style placement floral suited to eveningwear. The range reflects a studio built to serve multiple seasonal briefs across several markets rather than a single signature aesthetic.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected images span hand-painted botanical work alongside precise, engineered placement and geometric repeats, consistent with the studio's description of designers combining "a variety of different skills and techniques."

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Found licenses original, exclusive print designs on a weekly basis to womenswear, menswear, kids, active, and swim brands; artwork is produced and printed to order in the UK. No specific clients are documented.
