## Overview

Le Studio Copenhagen is a Copenhagen-based studio made up of an international design team, with a stated focus on colour and quality. Its designs range from cool, urban styles to intricate bohemian work, with a trend-oriented perspective.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images support the studio's own "cool urban to intricate bohemian" description: the range spans painterly abstract brushstroke prints, damask/ikat-style motifs in saturated pink-orange-and-cream, ornate Moroccan-tile geometric patchwork, large-scale romantic florals, plaid and stripe combinations, and dense paisley/bandana-style patchwork in muted heritage tones. Colorways run from soft pastel florals to high-saturation jewel tones.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion

Selected pattern images are shown almost entirely on apparel (dresses, tops, swimwear, resort wear), indicating fashion as the primary market.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The portfolio shows a mix of painterly, hand-drawn work (brushstroke abstracts, watercolor florals) and more graphic, digitally constructed motifs (tile-pattern geometrics, cross-stitch-style borders), suggesting the studio works across both hand-drawn and digital techniques.
