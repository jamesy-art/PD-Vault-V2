## Overview

KATE x KASEY is a US boutique print studio founded by Kate Aurelia and Kasey Free, producing exclusive prints for home and fashion clients worldwide. The studio is recognised for distinctive colour palettes, original motifs, and trend-aware design that elevates brand ranges across both markets.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio's output is characterised by unique colour combinations and strong motif work developed through traditional painting, collage, and digital design. The portfolio features a broad design range suited to home décor and fashion applications, with an emphasis on distinctive, brand-elevating aesthetics.

## Markets and Specialties

Home and fashion, with clients served globally. The studio offers both an exclusive print shop (seamless repeat, production-ready, immediate download) and a bespoke commission and collaboration service.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Prints are created using a combination of traditional painting, collage, and digital design. The founders describe their process as drawing on extensive experience across each medium to produce work tailored to specific client briefs.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio sells exclusive prints via its online shop (immediate download, seamless repeat, production-ready files) and accepts commissions and collaborations. No specific client names are disclosed.
