## Overview

Bay & Brown is a London-based textile print studio founded in 2005 by Tracey Brown, specialising in sophisticated hand-painted print designs for women's apparel, swimwear, children's, and home collections. The studio releases new designs weekly and operates a global sales network serving both high street and high-end clients.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio is positioned on the quality and sophistication of its hand-painted approach. The collection updates weekly, with themes visible on the website including tropical, floral, and decorative directions. The breadth of market coverage — from high street to high-end, from swimwear to home — reflects a versatile commercial range anchored by hand-painted technique.

## Markets and Specialties

Women's apparel, swimwear, children's, and home. Bay & Brown serves both high-end and high street clients globally, with bespoke services for smaller brands requiring closer collaborative guidance.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Bay & Brown's core positioning is hand-painted textile design, produced in-house by the studio team and released continuously into the collection. No other production methods are documented.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Bay & Brown's designs have featured in collections across high street and high-end stores worldwide; specific brand names are not disclosed. A dedicated global sales team and network of agents support direct client meetings. Bespoke design services are available to smaller brands.

## Studio Background

Tracey Brown founded Bay & Brown in 2005 following experience as a fashion textile designer. Based in Hackney Wick, London, the studio has grown to include a global sales infrastructure and The Design School by Bay and Brown — an educational arm offering creative programs, digital courses, and in-studio workshops for both beginners and working designers.

## Pattern Focus

Sophisticated hand-painted prints for women's apparel, swimwear, children's, and home. The collection updates weekly and reflects ongoing seasonal trends across tropical, floral, and decorative directions.
