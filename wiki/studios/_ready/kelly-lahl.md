## Overview

Kelly Lahl Design LLC is a US-based print design practice selling exclusive hand-painted artwork to fashion brands and manufacturers, with a stated focus on apparel, swim, resort, and nightwear.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The collection has an artistic, contemporary hand with dye effects, abstracts, and animal print alongside vintage-inspired modern florals and ornamentals. Selected pattern images bear this out: loose gouache/watercolor florals (peonies, roses, daisies), paper-marbled abstracts, a fabric-draped floral repeat, an animal-print ground, and Western-themed conversational motifs (horses, riders, cacti).

## Markets and Specialties

Apparel, swim, resort, and nightwear, per the studio's stated focus, created for contemporary fashion and lifestyle brands.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Most designs begin as hand-painted or hand-drawn studies on paper using analog media, then are digitized, refined, and developed into production-ready designs in Adobe Photoshop. The portfolio shows painted studies, color-swatch reference sheets, and painting materials consistent with this hand-to-digital process.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Kelly Lahl Design LLC sells exclusive prints directly to brands and manufacturers, informed by ongoing cultural, runway, and market trend research.

Kelly Lahl Design LLC is a US-based print design practice built around a hand-painting-led process — paintings and drawings on paper in a range of analog media, translated into production-ready digital artwork.

## Pattern Focus

Hand-painted florals with dye-effect washes, abstract marbling, animal print, and vintage-inspired conversational motifs (Western/equestrian imagery), developed for the swim, resort, and apparel markets.
