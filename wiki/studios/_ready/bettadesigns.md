## Overview

Betta Design is a contemporary textile design studio based in Sydney, Australia, producing exclusive prints for the fashion market. The studio specialises in colourful prints, graphics, and embroideries inspired by nature, art, and world culture, interpreting lifestyle trends through a combination of digital and hand-drawn/painted techniques.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Betta Design's output is colourful and nature-informed, with a design vocabulary drawing from art and global culture. The portfolio features expressive, trend-responsive work suited to fashion applications with a bold colour approach.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio works with a combination of digital and hand-drawn/painted techniques. Every design is described as uniquely crafted through this dual process, producing prints, graphics, and embroideries.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Betta Design supplies exclusive prints to the fashion market. No specific client names are disclosed.
