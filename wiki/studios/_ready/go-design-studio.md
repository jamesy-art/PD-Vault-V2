## Overview

Go Design Studio is a US-based trend-directed print studio operating as a collective of designers from around the world. The studio is directed and owned by Pattern Curator, a trend and concept studio for colour, print, and pattern. Designs span apparel, interior, and social-experience products and are offered on both exclusive and non-exclusive terms to industry professionals.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Go Design Studio describes its output as trend-directed and artistically inclined. The studio's collective model draws on diverse designers worldwide to produce a broad, forward-thinking print range with a strong production-conscious ethos.

## Markets and Specialties

Apparel, interiors, and social experience products. Both exclusive and non-exclusive designs are offered, with custom CAD, bespoke design development, and recolouring services available.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio's collective of designers works in handcrafted and digitally rendered methods, producing prints structured for easy transition to manufacture. Collection appointments are available in person or via Zoom. Custom CAD and recolouring services are offered by inquiry.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Go Design Studio serves industry professionals, offering a registered exclusive collection and a separate non-exclusive collection for limited end-use purposes. Bespoke print design, development, and recolouring services are available via the studio's design team.
