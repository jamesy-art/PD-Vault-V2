## Overview

Gather No Moss is a UK-based multidisciplinary print design studio with over thirty-five years of experience in textiles and fashion. Founded in London in 1987 by Tom Bambridge-Sutton and James Hartley, the studio has grown into a globally recognised operation with a skilled international design team, a dynamic sales function, and a continuously refreshed online collection available to buyers around the clock.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Gather No Moss produces original, trend-driven prints across a broad aesthetic range. The Womenswear collection alone spans Scenic, Ethnic, Ditsy, Camouflage, Tropical, Floral, Photographic, Catwalk Reaction, Tie Dye, Conversational, Geometric, Abstract, Boho, and Paisley directions. Selected images and placements reflect output suited to diverse market segments from womenswear and menswear to swim and gifting.

## Markets and Specialties

Womenswear, menswear, kidswear, swimwear, home décor, and gifting. Seasonal collections cover Womens, Mens, Kids, Gift, Swim, Girls, and Boys categories. Design services include bespoke artwork, co-ordinating prints, vintage reworks, digital archive curation, and custom commission.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The design team — led by Art Director Fiona White (twenty-four years experience) — produces contemporary original prints rooted in hand-rendered illustration. A globally diverse team of designers provides broad trend perspective and a wide range of handwriting styles. All artwork is supplied as high-quality digital files in production-ready formats.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Gather No Moss operates as a full-service print design partner, offering bespoke briefing, co-ordinating print development, and access to a vast digital archive. The sales team is managed by Charlotte Baxter (fourteen years experience) and supported by international agents. The online collection is available 24/7 for direct browsing and purchase. No specific client names are disclosed.

## Studio Background

Founded in London in 1987 by Tom Bambridge-Sutton and James Hartley, Gather No Moss has operated for over thirty-five years in the textile and fashion print industry. The studio has an international agent network and describes itself as one of the world's leading textile design studios.

## Pattern Focus

A broad, seasonally refreshed collection spanning womenswear, menswear, kidswear, swim, and gifting. Design directions range from hand-rendered botanical and floral work to abstract, geometric, catwalk-reactive, tie-dye, and vintage rework categories.
