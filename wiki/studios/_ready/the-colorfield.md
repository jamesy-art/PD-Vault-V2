## Overview

The Colorfield is an established print design studio founded in New York in 1994 by Adam Read, with a London studio added in 2005 employing a team of 15 print designers. The studio has built a reputation over more than 20 years serving an international client base spanning contemporary labels, major retailers, and emerging designers.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern work spans a wide stylistic range rather than a single signature look: ditsy floral repeats, painterly watercolor florals, dense botanical and tropical florals in saturated jewel tones, graphic art-deco-style linear leaf and fan motifs, abstract tie-dye/camouflage-style blends, and geometric chevron and dot-based prints. Colour is treated as the unifying device across these varied motifs, consistent with the studio's stated practice of defining a directional colour palette each season.
