## Overview

Plinto Studio is a US design studio specializing in original hand-painted prints and graphics, creating custom textile designs and artwork for children's apparel, home furnishings, and paper goods. Designs have been featured by Janie and Jack, Gymboree, and Tea Collection.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The 8 selected_patterns are consistently hand-painted floral and botanical work: dense coordinating floral flat-lays (a swatch spread of complementary ditsy, tossed, and Provençal-style florals in blue/pink/coral palettes), a pen-and-ink block-print-style medallion motif in navy and pink (shown mid-drawing with a dip pen), an ombré petal/blossom gradient study, a purple hollyhock botanical, a muted sage-and-blue leaf repeat, a moody navy-ground magnolia floral, a grey berry-and-leaf repeat, and an olive branch botanical shown mid-painting. The reviewed sample skews toward florals and botanicals rather than the tropical or conversational prints mentioned in the studio's own site copy.

## Markets and Specialties

Children's apparel, home furnishings, and paper goods.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Hand-painted originals — watercolor botanical studies and pen-and-ink work are both visible in progress across the selected pattern images (brush and dip-pen shown mid-stroke), consistent with the studio's own description of itself as "hand-painted prints and graphics."

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Designs have been featured by Janie and Jack, Gymboree, and Tea Collection, per the studio's own website.
