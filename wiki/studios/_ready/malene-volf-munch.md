## Overview

Malene Volf Munch is a Danish pattern designer and illustrator recognised for a refined ornamental style, expressive line work, and sophisticated colour sense. Rooted in North Zealand and drawing on the surrounding sea and nature, she creates fashion and lifestyle patterns informed by historical design traditions and decorative arts, available for licensing to international brands.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns confirm a refined, ornament-led aesthetic: a celestial sun/moon-face motif rendered in fine linework, a Byzantine/Ottoman-influenced medallion print in teal and navy, and several geometric repeats (checkerboard, harlequin diamonds) in confident, decorative colorways. The work favors graphic, historically-informed motifs over loose or painterly florals.

## Studio Background

Malene Volf Munch is a Danish pattern designer and illustrator based in North Zealand, a graduate of the Danish Design School (KADK), with more than two decades of experience in the fashion and lifestyle industries.

## Pattern Focus

Ornamental and decorative-arts-informed motifs: celestial imagery, historical medallion patterns, and geometric repeats (checkerboard, harlequin), reflecting the stated influence of nature, historical design traditions, and decorative arts.
