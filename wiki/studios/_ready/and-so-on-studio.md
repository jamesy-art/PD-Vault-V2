## Overview

& So On is a London-based print design studio producing on-trend exclusive collections for fashion and textile clients worldwide. The studio operates with a small team across a range of design skills, maintaining both an online print collection and a global sales representative network.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio describes its output as fresh, innovative, and on-trend. The portfolio features a commercially focused range suited to women's fashion apparel with strong colour and seasonal relevance. No specific motif signature is documented; the studio emphasises trend currency and exclusive availability.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion and textiles. & So On supplies exclusive designs to global clients, with designs stated to appear in leading fashion brands. The studio also offers design services to support in-house teams with colour separation, repeat work, and design administration.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio provides original print design, colour separation, and repeat work as client services, in addition to its online print collection. A global sales representative network complements direct online sales.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

& So On prints are described as appearing in "some of the world's leading fashion brands," though specific names are not disclosed. Designs are offered exclusively, with bespoke services available to smaller labels. The studio is actively seeking freelance collaborators.
