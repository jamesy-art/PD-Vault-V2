## Overview

Harmony Print Studio is a Melbourne-based print and pattern design studio with over a decade of industry experience. Founded by Rose and Vanessa — who first worked together at a design company before reuniting to launch the studio — Harmony operates from a conviction that great design combines commercial awareness and creative vision in equal measure.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio's print range runs from hand-crafted florals to bold abstracts and sportswear patterns. Selected patterns reflect a diverse, trend-informed vocabulary designed for broad commercial application across fashion and activewear markets.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion, including sportswear and activewear. The studio offers an exclusive print library alongside a bespoke design service.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Harmony Print Studio grounds its practice in hand-drawn skills combined with trend forecasting and digital production. The studio offers both a ready-to-buy exclusive print library and a bespoke design development service for tailored client briefs.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Harmony positions itself as a print partner for fashion and activewear brands, offering a retail-ready exclusive print library and a bespoke collection service. No specific client names are disclosed.

## Studio Background

Rose and Vanessa founded Harmony Print Studio after meeting at a design company approximately ten years ago, combining experience working with high-end and high-street clients. The studio is based in Melbourne, Australia.

## Pattern Focus

A diverse collection spanning hand-crafted florals to bold abstracts and sportswear prints, delivered from a growing print library with bespoke design capability.
