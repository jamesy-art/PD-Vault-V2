## Overview

ALXNDR is a New Zealand-based multi-disciplinary print studio led by artist and illustrator Bron Alexander. The studio creates unique prints and patterns for fashion, stationery, and home decor, with each design positioned as a visual narrative that adds a distinctive, art-driven character to products.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio is described as "led by a love of art," with Bron Alexander working across illustration and pattern design. The portfolio features a strong illustrative sensibility with painterly or hand-rendered qualities. Selected placements indicate work applied across apparel and stationery products.
