## Overview

Owens & Kim is a London-based textile design studio founded in 1997 by Helen Owens and Kim Stockton, who combined their backgrounds in commercial textile design. The studio positions itself as "the premier textile design studio for young women's fashion," selling designs with usage rights directly through its website to a self-described international client base of fashion houses, retailers, and fabric manufacturers.

[Contact us](https://www.owensandkim.com/contact-us/) to make an appointment.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The portfolio shows a fashion-forward, print-led range built on bold color and painterly texture: ikat-blurred brushstroke abstracts in soft multicolor palettes, an animal-print-inspired scribble abstract, ornate paisley/bandana-style medallions in warm jewel tones, a dense multicolor ditsy floral on a navy ground, a blue-and-cream photographic floral, a geometric ikat/houndstooth abstract paired with a tonal animal-print stripe, and a maximalist tropical scenic print with toucans, palms, and a panther silhouette. The range spans painterly abstraction, paisley, ditsy floral, and tropical novelty rather than a single house look.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Per the studio's own description, designs combine hand-drawn imagery with digital production, with each pattern delivered as a digitally-printed silk sample alongside a layered digital artwork file — consistent with the mix of painterly brushwork and cleaner engineered/geometric repeats seen among the selected patterns.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Owens & Kim operates a direct licensing model: designs are available for purchase with usage rights via the studio's website, with new work uploaded daily and buyer registration required to search, preview, and download.
