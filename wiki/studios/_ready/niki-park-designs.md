## Overview

Niki Park Designs is a solo surface pattern design studio run by Nicole ("Niki") Park from Portsmouth, Virginia. Niki began working in surface pattern design in May 2020, drawing on photography, abstract painting, and everyday life for inspiration. Selected pattern work spans whimsical, conversational novelty prints alongside soft ditsy florals.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The portfolio shows two consistent directions: character-driven conversational novelty prints (line-art cats and yarn, bicycles and typewriters with yarn balls, a bison-and-daisy motif) rendered in clean, high-contrast line work on solid grounds, and softer ditsy florals (a cream floral-and-polka-dot print, a white blossom-and-branch print in ivory, burgundy, navy, and gold). A dark-navy scattered-bloom print and an all-over abstract dot texture round out the range. This favors clean, market-ready repeats over painterly or heavily textured rendering.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The portfolio features clean digital/vector line work for the conversational novelty prints and softer digital rendering for the floral repeats *(unverified whether any work is hand-painted)*.

## Studio Background

Niki Park (Nicole) is based in Portsmouth, Virginia. She discovered surface pattern design in May 2020 and has worked as a solo designer since, describing her practice as rooted in curiosity and everyday inspiration — coffee shops, nature, and family life.

## Pattern Focus

Conversational novelty prints (cats, yarn, bicycles, typewriters, bison) and ditsy florals, plus one abstract dot/texture print.
