## Overview

Phyllis Orzalli is a freelance textile designer and watercolor artist based in the Northern California foothills, creating hand-painted floral surface patterns for women's contemporary fashion. Her practice translates original watercolor paintings into seamless, production-ready repeats, combining hand-painted artistry with trend awareness.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The 8 selected_patterns are consistently floral and botanical, rendered from watercolor originals into finished repeats: dense wildflower bouquets in soft pink/coral/burgundy, an autumn floral with oak leaves and acorns, a directional blue floral stripe on a damask ground, a graphic black silhouette floral on cream, a bold jewel-tone floral with black accents, a saturated red-ground nasturtium botanical, a dusty ditsy rose floral, and a peony floral on a black ground. Palettes range from soft pastel to deep jewel-tone and black-ground colorways, unified by naturalistic flower forms and visible watercolor texture even where digitally finished (a "painted petals" logo watermark appears on several pattern files).

## Markets and Specialties

Women's contemporary fashion.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Hand-painted watercolor originals, using wet-on-wet and wet-on-dry techniques, later digitized into seamless repeats. Notably, the 4 selected_placements are not product mockups but original watercolor studies (an iris botanical study, a peony bouquet, a rose bouquet, and a white rose/floral spray) — direct evidence of the hand-painted source material behind the finished patterns.

## Studio Background

Phyllis Orzalli has painted in watercolor for 25+ years, since taking a class at her local college. She works from her studio in the Northern California foothills, drawing inspiration from her garden, nearby forest, and travel.

## Pattern Focus

Hand-painted floral and botanical repeats for women's fashion, spanning wildflower bouquets, garden roses and peonies, autumn foliage motifs, and directional floral stripes.
