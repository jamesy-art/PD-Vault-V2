## Overview

Parchment and Pixel is a boutique print studio founded by artist Annie Havlicek, who brings over seventeen years of experience in the luxury fashion industry. The studio is known for hand-painted floral prints for fashion and home decor clients, positioned as fine-art-grade design work rather than commodity print.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns confirm a consistently hand-painted, fine-art floral aesthetic across several registers: loose acrylic/gouache bouquet florals in saturated multicolor (peonies, ranunculus, anemones) on white and cornflower-blue grounds; a fine-line watercolor botanical of peonies, nasturtiums, and tulips on a periwinkle ground in an antique herbarium style; an old-master-style tulip-and-peony bouquet print with trailing blue ribbon on soft yellow; a monochrome blush-and-dusty-blue rose repeat; an all-white peony repeat on sky blue; a jewel-toned garden floral with bees and butterflies on cream; and a maximalist rococo-style scroll print combining tropical florals, a perched bird, and butterflies. The range spans delicate botanical illustration to bold painterly maximalism, unified by fine-art execution rather than flat vector design.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Patterns are hand-painted (acrylic/gouache florals, fine-line watercolor botanicals), consistent with the studio's description of prints as "fine art creations" rather than digitally-generated design.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Per the studio's own description, Havlicek's client list includes Marchesa, Pamella Roland, Theia, and Halston, with prints regularly featured at Saks Fifth Avenue, Neiman Marcus, Nordstrom, and Bergdorf Goodman — positioning the studio at the luxury/occasion end of fashion print, consistent with the bridal- and eveningwear-adjacent tone of the selected pattern images.
