## Overview

Tara Lilly grew up on family farmland in rural Northern California, surrounded by redwood forest, open meadows, and wildflowers — an upbringing that remains her primary creative touchstone. Her work draws on nature, folk art, antique textiles, and mid-century design, expressed through floral illustration, charming characters, and colour. Illustrations appear across fabric, apparel, games, home and garden decor, and paper goods; she is represented by Lilla Rogers.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns are gouache-painted, densely packed florals in bold, saturated palettes — coral, lime, mustard, magenta, cobalt, and blush pink recur across the set. Several repeats work whimsical conversational elements (ladybugs, butterflies, a dragonfly) directly into the floral field, and one studio wall/portfolio image extends the same folk-illustration style to charming characters (a dog portrait, a bear in a dress, a woman with floral hair) and home-goods illustration (a floral teapot, a floral sofa with a cat) — consistent with the studio's own description of painting "charming characters" alongside florals.
