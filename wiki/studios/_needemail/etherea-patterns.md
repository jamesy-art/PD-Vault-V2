## Overview

Etherea Patterns is a US-based print design studio producing nature-inspired patterns for textile and paper products, drawn from real and imagined natural subjects.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images are consistently botanical: ditsy watercolor florals in violet and pink, rose/peony motifs in a cream colorway, a red-wildflower print with dragonflies, and a peony print on grey. All are all-over floral repeats on light or white grounds, matching the studio's stated focus on nature-based imagery.

## Markets and Specialties

Textile and paper products *(per studio description; no further market detail is provided in the source text)*.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Pattern imagery mixes looser watercolor florals with more graphic, flat-rendered floral repeats, suggesting the studio works in both painterly and digital illustration styles *(unverified)*.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio offers both exclusive and non-exclusive pattern licensing: non-exclusive designs are priced lower and positioned as secondary or accent patterns (e.g. borders) rather than primary motifs. The studio states it incorporates client feedback into its designs.
