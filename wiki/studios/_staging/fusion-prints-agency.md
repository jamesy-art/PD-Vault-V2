## Overview

Fusion Prints is an Australian-based sales agency representing leading UK exclusive print and design studios, receiving and distributing new collections from these studios monthly.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Not applicable — Fusion Prints distributes collections produced by its partner studios rather than designing its own patterns. No reviewed images are available for this page (all Pattern Cloud and website images are unchecked).

## Markets and Specialties

Not documented on this page; markets vary by the individual studios represented (see Connections).

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Not applicable — artwork is produced by the represented studios, not by Fusion Prints itself.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Fusion Prints operates as the Australian sales channel for six named UK print and design studios — Amanda Kelly, Owens & Kim, Fawcett + Co, Whiston & Wright, Bay & Brown, and Fairbairn & Wolf — receiving new collections monthly. No end-client names are documented for Fusion Prints itself.
