## Overview

Niki Park Designs is a solo surface pattern design studio run by Nicole ("Niki") Park from Portsmouth, Virginia. Niki began working in surface pattern design in May 2020, drawing on photography, abstract painting, and everyday life for inspiration. Selected pattern work spans whimsical, conversational novelty prints alongside soft ditsy florals.

## Niki Park

Surface Pattern Designer based in Portsmouth, Virginia

I'm a wife and homeschool mom to a teenage daughter. I love my family and care very much for my community.

A bit of an introvert and an old soul, I mostly enjoy the quiet things in life. I love spending time in coffee shops. Spending time in nature also makes me happy. I'm always up for getting out of town when the opportunity arises!

I discovered Surface Pattern Design in May of 2020, and I became absolutely hooked! Creating repeat patterns makes me feel like a kid again. 

I hope you enjoy my work as much as I enjoy creating it!

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show two consistent directions: character-driven conversational novelty prints (line-art cats and yarn, bicycles and typewriters with yarn balls, a bison-and-daisy motif) rendered in clean, high-contrast line work on solid grounds, and softer ditsy florals (a cream floral-and-polka-dot print, a white blossom-and-branch print in ivory, burgundy, navy, and gold). A dark-navy scattered-bloom print and an all-over abstract dot texture round out the range. This favors clean, market-ready repeats over painterly or heavily textured rendering.

Niki Park (Nicole) is based in Portsmouth, Virginia. She discovered surface pattern design in May 2020 and has worked as a solo designer since, describing her practice as rooted in curiosity and everyday inspiration — coffee shops, nature, and family life.
