## Overview

Paradox (Paradox NYC) is a New York-based vintage textile and print design archive and showroom, holding a collection of over 6,000 categorized vintage textiles and prints organized by decade and technique. Rather than producing original seasonal collections from scratch, the studio's core offering is access to this historical archive as a resource for contemporary print and embellishment design, updated seasonally to track current trends.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected images reflect the studio's archival, multi-decade remit rather than a single house style: a hand-beaded and sequinned embroidery sample worked in a wave motif (gold, green, pink, and red on black), a graphic abstract animal-print scribble with red and yellow accent spots, a bold tropical hibiscus-leaf print in red, yellow, and grey, a retro Hawaiian-style floral stripe print in saturated yellow, pink, and blue, a green-and-white scrolling silhouette floral reminiscent of chinoiserie/jacquard work, a loose watercolor-style rose print in teal, pink, and orange, a graphic mustard tropical floral with black-and-teal fronds, and a pastel chinoiserie-style tiger-and-bamboo print. Together these point to a vintage-led, decade-spanning range across retro florals, tropical/Hawaiian motifs, animal prints, and embellishment techniques.

## Studio Background

Described as "New York's premier source for trendsetting vintage prints, silhouettes, and embellishment techniques," Paradox states that it builds its archive by selecting and traveling to source unique vintage textiles and garments from the world's most influential fashion capitals.

## Pattern Focus

The archive spans retro/vintage florals (Hawaiian tropical, chinoiserie-style scroll and tiger motifs), graphic animal-print abstracts, and hand embellishment (sequin and bead) techniques — reflecting the studio's role as a historical reference resource rather than a single-aesthetic design house.
