## Overview

Popcreate is a London-based textile design studio founded in 2020 by Faye Angel. The studio is staffed by a team of textile and fashion designers with prior retailer-side experience, and operates a ready-to-buy digital print collection alongside bespoke commissions and colour/print trend analysis for fashion and interiors clients.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show dense, all-over florals in two registers: saturated, high-contrast colourways (frequently set on black grounds) and softer, dusty-pastel recolours of the same motifs (lilac, powder blue, sage). The set also includes archival scarf-style prints — a novelty fruit/vegetable seed-packet motif, a cherry-and-stripe combination, and a paisley floral border — indicating a range that extends beyond core florals into vintage-inspired conversational and archival prints.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion

A selected placement image shows a floral-and-leopard motif applied to womenswear separates, consistent with the studio's stated fashion focus.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Popcreate digitises vintage archive pieces and original photography into hand-rendered, full industry-repeat artwork, and offers free re-colours — visible in the selected patterns, where several florals recur across multiple distinct colourways.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

No specific client names are disclosed (unverified). The studio positions itself around three services: a ready-to-buy digital print collection, bespoke client commissions, and seasonal colour/print trend analysis.
