## Overview

Made in Brighton is a long-established UK print design studio producing original artwork for the fashion industry and adjacent lifestyle categories, working closely with clients on collection development.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images span a deliberately wide range rather than one signature look: hand-painted watercolor florals in saturated wildflower and thistle motifs, a bold jewel-tone large-scale floral on a dark ground, flat vector botanicals (tropical foliage, a desert cactus scene), a traditional ornamental tile-style floral, and an indigo fish repeat with a coastal, craft-print feel.
