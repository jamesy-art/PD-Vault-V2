## Overview

December Design Box is a small studio focused on developing contemporary print design for fashion, resort, and swim.

The studio develops ready-to-print repeat artworks, placement artworks, and colorway, with consideration to scale and real product application.

If you’re looking for something specific to your own idea, or an integration of your direction in an artwork, I’m happy to chat. Just reach through email or website.
