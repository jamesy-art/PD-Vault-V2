## Overview

Fated Print Agency is a New York-based print agency founded in 2018 by principals with a background in London print studios and a decade spent selling print work internationally before settling in NYC. The agency represents a portfolio of print studios rather than producing an in-house collection itself.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Fated Print Agency positions itself as a multi-studio agency, representing Camilla Frances Prints, BAY & BROWN, FAWCETT & CO, Fated Print Collection, Zisser Textile Design, and PS London under its own umbrella. Stated regular clients include Karl Lagerfeld, Calvin Klein, Macy's, Primark, Express, Ann Taylor, Monique Lhuillier, GAP, and Victoria's Secret.
