## Overview

Prints of Orange is a textile design studio based in Connecticut, US, representing multiple designers from around the world. The studio produces hand-painted and digital-print artwork for fashion, home furnishing, accessories, and paper goods markets, and takes on custom designs, re-colourings, and commissioned projects.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show a deliberately wide stylistic range, consistent with a multi-designer studio: a saturated neon-digital tropical palm-leaf abstraction; a tonal sage-green illustrated lichen/topiary novelty print; a vivid multicolour firework-style wildflower illustration on cream; a soft watercolour-and-ink wildflower botanical; and a naturalistic vintage-style botanical illustration of passionflower and tropical blooms. The range spans bold digital abstraction to traditional hand-painted/illustrated botanicals.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio works across hand-painted and digital artwork, presented on both paper and fabric, and offers custom design, re-colouring, and commissioned work.
