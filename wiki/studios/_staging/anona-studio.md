## Overview

ANONA Studio is a US-based creative studio founded in 2011 by Sarah Leaman and Renée Shortell, specialising in exclusive artwork for the apparel and home industries. The studio is known for hand-painted original work, a timeless design sensibility, and particular strength in children's design. ANONA presents collections at Première Vision New York and Première Vision Paris.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

ANONA's approach is defined by hand-painted artwork, clever conceptual references, and a timeless editorial sensibility described as finding "delight in the unexpected." Selected patterns suggest a range spanning decorative, botanical, and narrative print directions. The studio's strength in children's design points to an illustrative, playful dimension within its broader output.

## Markets and Specialties

Apparel and home, with a noted focus on children's design. Première Vision NY and Paris presence positions the studio within the premium international print sourcing market.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Hand-painted original artwork is central to the studio's identity. Services include: Original Print Design from the archive, Bespoke Print Design to client brief, CAD Services (colour matching, colourways, colour separation/indexing, motif changes, repeats and reworks), and Vintage rework (digitising and updating vintage prints to production standards).

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

ANONA presents at Première Vision NY and Paris, indicating a client base in international apparel and home industries. No specific client names are documented. Both archive designs and bespoke commissioned work are available.
