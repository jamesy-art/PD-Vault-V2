## Overview

Aquamarine Studio is a US-based print design practice run by Janet Soja Carlson, a fashion design graduate with over twenty-five years of hands-on experience in the Miami garment industry as a fashion designer and textile buyer. The studio applies deep industry knowledge of fabric selection, panels, and border prints to fresh, distinctive surface design for the fashion market.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Carlson describes being most inspired by colour and texture pairings, with a goal of bringing fresh, distinctive designs to the textile space. Selected images suggest a range including structured repeats and more expressive compositions. The studio has recently been exploring hand-made collage as an additional medium.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion, with specialist knowledge of panels and border prints developed during Carlson’s career in the Miami garment industry.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio works in both vector and raster formats, with repeat pattern skills developed through Pattern Observer’s Textile Design Lab (2016–2021). Carlson’s background includes hand-made collage and a lifelong practice in quilting, both informing her approach to colour and texture.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Aquamarine Studio operates as an independent print design practice. No specific client names or commercial relationships are documented in available sources.

## Studio Background

Janet Soja Carlson holds a Fashion Design degree and spent over twenty-five years in the Miami garment industry before transitioning to textile design. She studied graphic arts in 2005 and completed specialist training at Pattern Observer’s Textile Design Lab (2016–2021). The studio draws on Carlson’s combined background as a practising fashion designer and textile buyer.

## Pattern Focus

Fashion-oriented repeat patterns emphasising colour, texture, and fresh distinctive compositions. Panel and border print experience is a documented specialty. Selected images suggest a range including structured and expressive repeat work.
