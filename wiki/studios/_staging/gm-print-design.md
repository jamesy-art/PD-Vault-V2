## Overview

GM Print Design is a London-based print studio led by Georgina Mockett, producing trend-driven, production-ready prints for the global fashion industry. With over fifteen years of fashion and production experience and a global team of designers, the studio supplies exclusive prints to established fashion brands and ambitious startups worldwide.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio's collection spans from florals to abstract animal prints, with an emphasis on originality, trend relevance, and commercial impact. The output is designed for fast-moving seasonal application across fashion brands.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion. The studio works with buyers, designers, and creative directors at established brands and fashion startups globally.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

GM Print Design produces fully production-ready files: layered PSD files in seamless, industry-standard sized repeats, colour-separated and supplied in two colourways. All designs are sold exclusively, copyright included, and ready to send to manufacturer. Trend forecasting is also offered as a service.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio works directly with buyers, designers, and creative directors across established brands and ambitious startups worldwide. All designs are sold on an exclusive basis with copyright included. No specific client names are disclosed.
