## Overview

Studio Olive is a textile and surface design studio based in northern Italy, led by founder Amanda Oliveira, with over 10 years of experience creating exclusive prints. The studio positions itself around transforming trends and consumer insights into designs adapted to each client's collection.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show a Mediterranean-inspired range: watercolour coastal scenes (an Amalfi-style hillside village, a blue watercolour palm-tree and beach scene), cross-stitch-textured folk florals and ornamental borders (sage-green foliage, blue-and-white ornamental motifs, magenta florals), ikat-style abstract florals, a red-and-white jacquard-style paisley, a pale-blue line-art floral with a bird silhouette, and one bold colour-blocked geometric abstract in coral, blue, and burgundy. The coastal watercolours and folk cross-stitch textures align with the studio's stated Italian-Brazilian heritage.

## Studio Background

Founded and led by Amanda Oliveira, of Italian-Brazilian heritage, based in northern Italy. She holds a degree in Fashion and a postgraduate specialisation in Innovation and Technology in Print Design from SENAI CETIQT (Rio de Janeiro, Brazil), and has over a decade of experience in the fashion industry with a focus on print and surface design.

## Pattern Focus

Mediterranean and coastal watercolour scenes and folk-inspired cross-stitch-textured florals form the studio's most distinctive throughline, alongside ikat abstracts, paisley/jacquard prints, and occasional bold geometric abstraction.
