## Overview

Studio Noir is a Brazilian multidisciplinary print studio founded in 2017, evolving from a dedicated print studio into a broader creative practice applying original patterns across fashion, accessories, home goods, and decorative objects for both B2B clients and its own product line.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns centre on tropical and botanical leaf motifs (palm and fern silhouettes in olive, brown, and cream), alongside animal prints (leopard, zebra-adjacent stripe) and colour-blocked Art Deco-style geometric abstracts in jewel tones (rust, brown, blue, pink, mustard). A hand-painted cobalt-blue botanical linework study and a set of hand-painted ceramic tableware — striped, checked, and leaf-motif plates and vessels in terracotta, navy, and olive — show the studio's pattern work extending beyond textiles into product surfaces.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion
- accessories
- home goods

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Evidence spans hand-painted work (a blue ink/gouache botanical study, hand-painted ceramic tableware) and printed textile application on finished garments (a tropical-leaf shirt and swimwear set), indicating both traditional and print-production techniques within the studio's process.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio operates a B2B print licensing/commission business alongside its own branded product line. One selected pattern image is tagged @stonefoxswim, indicating an application or collaboration with the swimwear brand Stone Fox Swim (unverified beyond the tag visible in the image). Primary market focus is fashion, per the studio's own description of its origins.

## Studio Background

Founded in 2017. Source material describes the founding both as a two-person collaboration and, elsewhere, credits Édipo Furtado individually with starting the studio's focus on printmaking for the fashion market (unverified which framing is authoritative). What began as a dedicated print studio has expanded into a multidisciplinary creative practice spanning fashion, accessories, and home goods.

## Pattern Focus

Tropical/botanical leaf motifs are the studio's most consistent throughline, supported by animal prints and Art Deco-style colour-blocked abstracts; pattern work is applied across apparel, swimwear, and hand-painted ceramic tableware.
