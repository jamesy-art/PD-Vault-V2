## Overview

Nomad Studio is a Brazilian print design studio founded in 2015 by Daniel Ayres (graphic design) and Daniela Gontijo (fashion design), producing exclusive prints for fashion brands nationally and internationally. The studio pairs Ayres's technical printmaking background with Gontijo's fashion-design perspective and maintains an active trade-show presence in Brazil and at Premiere Vision in Paris.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show a wide, trend-forward range: animal-print/spot patterns styled in runway-editorial fashion imagery; saturated tropical and botanical florals (palms, hibiscus, iris) in warm coral, peach, and green; a "Wild Bloom" watercolor floral collection in magenta and coral; a graphic black-and-cream abstract zebra-stripe print; an art-deco-inspired stained-glass geometric print; ornate paisley and jacquard-style florals; and an illustrative Mediterranean/coastal-themed collection (sardines, citrus, seaside imagery). The range reflects a studio built around seasonal collection drops rather than one fixed house style.

## Markets and Specialties

- Fashion — women's apparel, shown in runway/editorial styling in selected pattern imagery
- International fashion trade, per the studio's stated trade-show presence in Brazil and at Premiere Vision, Paris

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected pattern imagery spans painterly watercolor florals, vector-based botanical and abstract prints, and photographic/illustrative collage-style collections. The studio combines a graphic-design-trained co-founder (Daniel Ayres) with a fashion-design-trained co-founder (Daniela Gontijo).

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Nomad Studio states it collaborates "with renowned brands both nationally and internationally" *(unverified — no specific client names documented)*. The studio describes a "strong presence at important industry events," including trade shows in Brazil and Premiere Vision in Paris, one of the industry's major fashion trade fairs. Selected pattern imagery includes promotional material for an "Estampar" trade event (Sept 30–Oct 1, Edifício Oswald de Andrade), consistent with this trade-show positioning.

## Studio Background

Founded in 2015 by Daniel Ayres and Daniela Gontijo. Ayres, a graphic design graduate, contributes technical and creative printmaking expertise; Gontijo, with a background in fashion design, shapes the studio's styling and functionality. Based in Brazil.

## Pattern Focus

Animal/spot prints, tropical and botanical florals, watercolor florals, graphic abstract/geometric prints, paisley and jacquard-style motifs, and illustrative coastal/Mediterranean themes.
