## Overview

Saltbox Print (Saltbox Printhouse) is a boutique textile print studio based in Scheveningen, near The Hague, Netherlands, a few steps from the beach. Per the studio's own description, it deliberately chooses quality over quantity, pairing fast-paced fashion-industry know-how with a distinct, trend-aware but not trend-led aesthetic.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show a hand-painted, artist-led practice rather than a purely commercial trend-chasing one: soft watercolor animal print (grey/yellow leopard spots on blush), a bold jewel-tone abstract marbled/collage composition (magenta, teal, brown, peach), indigo shibori-style tie-dye, a linocut-style botanical floral in blue/black/gold, a painted daisy floral on pale blue, a mustard-ground ditsy floral with green/pink/navy blooms, a climbing botanical vine print in ivory/black/gold, and a bold graphic tiger-stripe animal print. The range spans delicate watercolor work to bold graphic prints, consistent with the studio's stated aim to "stay true to our own aesthetics" while tracking commercial colour direction.

## Studio Background

Small boutique studio located in Scheveningen (The Hague), Netherlands. Per its own description, it maintains the production discipline of the fast-paced fashion industry while staying connected to its own artistic voice rather than following trends wholesale.

## Pattern Focus

Watercolor and hand-painted animal prints (leopard, tiger stripe), botanical and ditsy florals, and bold abstract marbled/collage motifs, spanning soft pastel to jewel-tone colorways — a range built to serve swim, active, fashion, and home markets alike.
