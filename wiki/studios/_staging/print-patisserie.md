## Overview

Print Patisserie is a surface design studio creating exclusive, art-driven prints for fashion and interior brands worldwide. Founded in 2019 by Margarita Saplala, the studio was born in Brooklyn and is now based in Northern California, blending hand-created artwork with digital design across concepts that are both trend-driven and culturally inspired.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns range across several distinct registers: retro-inspired abstract swirl florals in saturated orange/pink/green and olive/brown colourways; dense, romantic all-over florals set on black, burgundy, and rust grounds; a watercolour peacock-and-floral border/stripe print in blue tones; an archival paisley/floral scarf-style print; and an animal-print ikat blend developed from hand-drawn leopard croquis sketches. The range spans decorative repeat florals and paisleys through to fashion-forward placement and animal-print artwork.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion and interior brands, per the studio's own positioning. The selected placement image shows the leopard-ikat print applied to a womenswear button-up blouse, evidencing apparel use; the paisley and floral border motifs are consistent with a home/interiors application as well.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

The studio combines hand-painted and hand-drawn source art — visible in the leopard/jaguar croquis sketches among the selected patterns — with digital colourway and repeat development. The collection mixes retro-inspired abstract motifs with traditional florals and paisleys, described as balancing artistry with commercial relevance.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

No current Print Patisserie clients are named in the source text. Every design is sold exclusively rather than through an open print-shop model, positioning the studio at a higher-touch, bespoke tier. Founder Margarita Saplala's prior career included work with Halston and Anthropologie, and in 2005 she launched an eponymous womenswear collection carried by Barneys New York, Beams, and Tomorrowland — background for the founder rather than disclosed clients of the studio itself.

## Studio Background

Founded in 2019 by Margarita Saplala, Print Patisserie originated in Brooklyn and is now based in Northern California. Saplala brings over two decades of fashion and textile design experience, including work with Halston and Anthropologie and her own womenswear label. She leads a global team of artists producing exclusive collections.

## Pattern Focus

Retro-inspired abstract florals, romantic all-over florals, paisley and archival scarf motifs, and animal-print/ikat development including hand-drawn croquis — spanning both apparel placement prints and repeat patterns for interiors.
