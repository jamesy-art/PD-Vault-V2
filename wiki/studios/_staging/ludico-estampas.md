## Overview

Lúdico Estampas is a Brazilian print design studio founded by creative director Carina Prieto, creating exclusive prints for brands worldwide with the aim of helping them develop a distinct visual identity.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show vivid tropical and botanical illustration in saturated jewel tones — pink, purple, orange, and red florals with gold outline detailing, large-scale layered leaf motifs in burgundy and lilac, and a scenic coastal/village illustration (palm trees, colonial architecture) used as a border-print motif. The aesthetic favors bold, colorful illustration over the geometric side of the studio's stated range.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion

Selected pattern images are shown applied to womenswear (dresses, jumpsuits, resort-style pieces), indicating fashion as the primary application.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected pattern images show digitally illustrated botanical and scenic motifs with fine outline detail and gold accenting, consistent with the studio's description of offering both geometric patterns and colorful illustrations.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

No specific clients are documented. The studio positions itself as a creative-direction partner helping brands "develop their DNA" through exclusive prints.
