## Overview

The Scoop In Design is a US-based textile art agency, founded in 2003 by Nadine, representing a roster of London design studios and supplying trend-driven print designs to the fashion industry.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern imagery spans painterly watercolor florals, boho/ethnic-influenced geometric borders, a dark jewel-toned resort floral, and a whimsical novelty print (rabbits, daisies, carrots) — a broad, trend-forward range consistent with an agency curating work from multiple represented studios rather than one signature house style.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion
- resort *(supported by a "Resort"-branded floral moodboard image)*
- children's/novelty prints *(supported by a whimsical bunny-and-floral pattern)*

## Techniques and Artwork Process

No specific tools, media, or production methods are documented. Selected imagery mixes painterly/watercolor renderings with flatter graphic florals, consistent with sourcing from several different represented studios.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Operates as a textile art agent supplying original, trend-driven print designs to the fashion industry. Studios represented include [[Betta Design]], [[FAWCETT + CO]], [[Gather No Moss]], [[Jack Jones Design]], and [[Print Patisserie]], among others *(per description_patterncloud)*.
