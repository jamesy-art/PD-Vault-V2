## Overview

TWENTY STUDIO LAB is an Italian print design studio founded in May 2018 by Giulia De Salvia and Chiara Guzzon. The studio pairs a drawing-based creative process with backgrounds in the Italian textile industry, developing fashion print collections for brands and companies worldwide.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern imagery ranges from bold, large-scale abstract tropical/botanical placement prints in teal, orange, and pink, to dark-ground fragmented florals paired with finer ditsy floral borders, to classic foulard/paisley motifs. Moodboard and illustration material favors a softer, muted rust, olive, and mauve palette, suggesting a range spanning statement placement prints to smaller-scale classic patterns.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion
- textile industry and capsule-collection consulting *(per existing body text)*

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Described as a drawing-led studio — sketching is presented as the starting point of every project. Stated specialties include pattern variation, customized design variants, and pattern alignment, drawing on over 15 years of textile industry experience.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Collaborates with fashion brands and companies worldwide, offering exclusive designs on request and creative consulting for textile industries and capsule collections. Collections are also distributed through agents worldwide *(no specific clients or agents named)*.
