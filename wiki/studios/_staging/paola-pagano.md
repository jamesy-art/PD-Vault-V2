## Overview

Paola Pagano is a one-woman surface pattern studio based in Milan, Italy, working across paper (watercolor, acrylic) and digital tablet illustration. Pagano studied architecture and illustration and spent time working in New York before establishing her own practice; per her own account, this period gave her "structure" and the push to find her own creative path. The studio produces original prints for Womenswear, Menswear, Active, Swim, and Home clients.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns confirm a hand-painted, gestural aesthetic: an acrylic "making of" study of layered leaf shapes ("Proserpina #2"), a painterly pebble/stone motif with playful polka-dotted fruit shapes, a graphic dotted-ground print with a striding cheetah rendered in flat green, loose brushstroke abstracts in high-contrast colorways (red/blue-grey and orange/navy), a hand-drawn blue ditsy floral sprig, and a set of narrative pop-surrealist prints — leaping cheetahs and birds on a dotted ground, elongated stylised figures, and whimsical flat-color characters (robots, a bird, a house). The work favours saturated, unexpected color pairings over naturalistic palettes, consistent with the studio's own description of "unexpected palettes" and irony.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Per the studio's own description, patterns originate in hand media — watercolor and acrylic painting — as well as digital tablet work. Selected images show both loose painterly brushwork and flatter vector-style illustration, indicating a mixed hand-to-digital process.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Positioned as an independent, single-designer studio rather than a multi-artist agency. Pagano's own description emphasizes originality, color, and irony as creative differentiators rather than trend-forecasting or large-team production capability. ## Studio Background

Pagano studied architecture and illustration and spent time working in New York before establishing her own pattern design practice in Milan. Per her own account, this period abroad shaped her creative direction and gave her the structure to pursue an independent path in textile and pattern design.

## Pattern Focus

Selected patterns point to a recurring visual world of stylised animals (cheetahs, birds) on dotted or patterned grounds, hand-painted organic/pebble motifs, loose gestural brush abstracts, and occasional pop-surrealist narrative scenes, unified by bold, unconventional color pairings.
