## Overview

LD Print Studio is the independent design practice of freelance designer Leonie Dickinson, who divides her time between the UK and Australia. The studio produces exclusive artwork for fashion and interiors, blending hand-drawn illustration with contemporary digital design.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show a range from soft, hand-painted botanical studies (loose gouache-style florals in muted pink and rust) to bold, high-contrast statement florals in saturated colorways — yellow-on-brown, blue-and-yellow, and blue-on-chocolate. Linework tends toward graphic outlines over painterly blending in the bolder pieces, consistent with the studio's description of its prints as "innovative, distinctive, and designed to make a statement."

## Markets and Specialties

- interiors
- fashion

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected pattern images indicate work spans both painted florals and more graphic, digitally finished repeats, matching the studio's stated blend of hand-drawn illustration with digital design.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

No specific clients are documented. The studio is positioned as an independent, freelance-led practice offering exclusive artwork rather than an in-house team.
