## Overview

Fusion CPH is a Copenhagen-based print design studio serving womenswear and interior-textile clients across Europe, the US, and Japan (unverified: figures sourced from third-party directory listings, not Pattern Cloud or the studio's own site). The studio sells exclusive, print-ready designs — all-over prints, placement prints, and embellishments — with full copyright transferred to the buyer, inspired by runway, street-style, and consumer trend analysis. Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Frederiksberg C, Copenhagen (unverified).

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show two distinct threads: animal-print motifs (leopard, tiger, snake, and cow-spot) in wearable, apparel-ready colorways, and floral repeats spanning romantic rose ditsies, soft engineered watercolour florals, and warm painterly botanicals. The mix points to a trend-responsive womenswear studio rather than a single house aesthetic.

## Markets and Specialties

- fashion (womenswear)

## Techniques and Artwork Process

One selected image shows a design in progress — hand-applied paint and dye on paper — consistent with the studio's stated process of starting designs by hand before digitizing them into colour-separated, production-ready files.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Designs are sold outright with full copyright to fashion brands and fabric suppliers, letting buyers place production with any supplier rather than being tied to the studio. No specific named clients are documented.
