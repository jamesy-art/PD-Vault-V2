## Overview

Bethany Hissong Art & Design is a Bell Buckle, Tennessee-based studio bringing over thirty years of professional illustration and design experience to surface pattern work for apparel and home décor. The practice applies a handcraft-led approach — rooted in gouache, watercolour, and digital design — to nature-themed patterns expressed in a classic yet contemporary register.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio favours classic patterns in a modern framing, with nature themes — botanical, floral, and bird subjects — prominent throughout. The hand-rendered quality of the work reflects Hissong's background as a professional illustrator, with a considered approach to colour and motif selection.

## Markets and Specialties

Apparel and home décor.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Bethany Hissong works in gouache and watercolour as well as digital design, describing a continuous exploration of technique and new ways to design. Her illustration background informs a polished, hand-rendered quality across the studio's pattern output.

## Studio Background

Bethany Hissong graduated from the University of Cincinnati's College of Design, Art, Architecture and Planning (DAAP), having also studied in an Honours Programme at the Cincinnati Art Academy. Her professional career has spanned teaching art to K–12 students, working in the creative department of a top national advertising agency, illustrating books for a Christian publishing house, and owning a small art gallery and studio in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. She has been a member of the Portrait Society of America. Hissong is now based in Bell Buckle, Tennessee, where her home studio focuses on book and stationery illustration alongside surface pattern design.

## Pattern Focus

Nature-themed patterns in gouache, watercolour, and digital media, suited to apparel and home décor. Classic repeat structures expressed in a contemporary treatment, informed by a career in professional illustration.
