## Overview

HER Studio London is a female-led print design studio based in East London, launched in 2016 with an ambition to create a platform for independent creatives. The studio produces exclusive artwork for major retailers and contemporary fashion brands worldwide, specialising in womenswear, activewear, and swim, with a collective of designers working new briefs each week.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images support the studio's own description of a "brave approach & celebration of pattern": saturated hand-painted florals (poppies, peonies, dahlias) in warm palettes, tonal blue ink-and-wash botanicals with a folk/chinoiserie feel, a jewel-toned abstract floral wash, colourful checkerboard/madras plaid, an airbrushed abstract swirl print, and a tiger-stripe tie-dye ikat effect — a wide, trend-responsive range rather than one house look.

## Markets and Specialties

- womenswear
- activewear
- swim

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Artwork is produced by hand across tie-dye, gouache, watercolour, ink, and pen-and-pencil detailing — visible directly in a selected process image showing a paintbrush alongside a tie-dye/ikat fabric — then delivered as fully digitised, colour-separated, seamless-repeat PSD files.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Designs are sold exclusively with original copyright to major retailers and fashion brands globally. Documented clients: Ralph Lauren, Topshop, Seafolly, Soul Cycle, Nylon Magazine, Vince Menswear, Anthropologie Home, Rebecca Taylor, DKNY, Calvin Klein, ASOS, Mango, Free People, French Connection, GAP, H&M, Never Fully Dressed, Hugo Boss, Intermix, Joe Fresh, Keds, Kookai, Melissa Odabash, Oysho, Parker NYC, Uniqlo, Victoria's Secret, and Speedo. The studio also states a sustainability goal of reducing waste and shifting clients toward digital sampling over physical fabric.
