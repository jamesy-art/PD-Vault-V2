## Overview

Nechines Print Design Studio is a Paraguay-based textile print studio led by creative director Nechi, producing exclusive, ready-to-print designs for the global fashion market. Operated as a global online studio, it serves women's, men's, and children's apparel with trend-driven, commercially oriented print work.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show a broad, trend-responsive range rather than one fixed signature: hand-painted macro florals in saturated purple, orange, and green; cleaner vector-based floral repeats; tropical hibiscus-and-leaf motifs in swimwear colorways (navy, dusty blue, blush); a striped, ethnic-inspired woven-look print; and an abstract brushstroke texture in cobalt and lime. The spread suggests a studio built around seasonal trend coverage across several aesthetics rather than a single house style.

## Markets and Specialties

- Fashion — women's, men's, and children's apparel
- Swimwear-adjacent prints (tropical/hibiscus motifs shown applied to rash-guard and swim silhouettes in selected pattern imagery)

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected pattern images point to facility with both painterly, hand-rendered florals and cleaner vector-based repeats, alongside woven-look ethnic stripe patterns. Nechi's background is 16 years in graphic design prior to specializing in textile print design.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio describes its mission as delivering trend-driven, high-quality designs that help brands worldwide define strong, distinctive identities through prints. One selected pattern image shows a design applied to a SHEIN X-branded garment, indicating at least one placement with the SHEIN X private-label program *(unverified beyond this image)*.

## Studio Background

Founded and led by Nechi, Creative Director of NECHINES.DESIGN. Nechi brings 16 years of graphic design experience into the studio's textile print practice, running it as a global online studio from Paraguay.

## Pattern Focus

Florals (painterly and vector-based), tropical/hibiscus motifs, ethnic-inspired stripes, and abstract brushstroke textures — spanning apparel-ready prints for multiple market segments.
