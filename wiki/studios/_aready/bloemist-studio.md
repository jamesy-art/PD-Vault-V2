## Overview

Bloemist Studio is a US-based pattern and forecasting studio founded in 2014, drawing inspiration from colour, travel, culture, and developing trends. Since launch, the studio's work has been present in the context of runway seasons across Paris, New York, Milan, London, and Melbourne Fashion Weeks. All prints are supplied in full repeat and colour-separated. The studio offsets its carbon footprint annually.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Bloemist Studio positions itself as a trend-informed design practice with broad international catwalk research as its foundation. Its focus on colour, travel, and culture points to a globally informed aesthetic.

## Markets and Specialties
