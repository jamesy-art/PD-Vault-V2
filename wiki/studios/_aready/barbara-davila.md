## Overview

Barbara d'Avila Print Studio is a São Paulo-based print design practice run by visual artist and print designer Barbara d'Avila. With more than a decade of experience supplying exclusive designs to leading fashion and interior brands, the studio offers a curated collection characterised by a strong colour sensibility and what the studio describes as a distinctive Brazilian aesthetic.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The studio's print collection is organised into four categories — Conversationals, Monochrome, Tropical, and Abstract — reflecting a range that spans narrative, decorative, and colour-driven print traditions. The studio emphasises a "vibrant blend of styles and artistic influences" alongside hands-on craftsmanship.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion and interiors. The studio has supplied exclusive designs to leading brands across both markets, with print collections available for exclusive purchase.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Barbara d'Avila's practice spans ceramics, canvas, fabric, and glass, reflecting a broad materials-based approach to surface and pattern design. Pattern work originates from hands-on artistic practice across multiple media. Specific digital production methods are not documented.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

The studio describes more than a decade of experience with "leading fashion and interior brands." Specific client names are not disclosed. Designs are offered exclusively through the studio's online print collection.

## Studio Background

Barbara d'Avila is a São Paulo-based visual artist who has developed her print design practice across more than ten years of supply to fashion and interior clients. Her artistic background spans multiple materials and surfaces, with the studio's print collection functioning as an extension of her broader creative practice.

## Pattern Focus

Four collection categories: Conversationals, Monochrome, Tropical, and Abstract. A strong colour sensibility and Brazilian aesthetic character are consistent across the range.
