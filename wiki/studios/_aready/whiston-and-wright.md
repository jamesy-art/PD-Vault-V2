## Overview

Whiston & Wright Ltd is a London-based print design studio creating fashion-forward prints that sell to designers worldwide, positioned around blending craftsmanship and innovation to produce trend-driven textiles with timeless appeal.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

The four selected images are trade-show announcement graphics (for New York, Los Angeles, and London show dates) rather than single repeat-print files, but each is a grid of finished prints. Visible work spans hand-painted paisley/foulard scrollwork in jewel tones (burgundy, red-and-black, blue-and-teal), painterly floral wallpaper-style motifs (pink/red blooms on green, blue floral scrollwork, black ink-brush florals on mauve), gouache-style tropical/novelty prints (potted palms with sun graphics, hand-painted citrus and bananas), an illustrated Amalfi-coast street scene, an animal-print abstract, and abstract painterly color-block/stripe panels — a broad, decorative range from classic paisley/foulard to painterly botanicals and travel-inspired scenics.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Imagery shows a hand-painted/gouache-led aesthetic (visible brushwork in the floral, fruit, and stripe motifs) alongside more classic engraved-style paisley/foulard patterns, consistent with the studio's stated blend of "craftsmanship and innovation."
