## Overview

Paul Vogel is a UK woven textile design studio founded in 1991 by Paul Vogel after a two-year internship with Cecci Lido in Florence, Italy. Per the studio's own description, it was one of the first CAD (computer-aided design) textile studios in the UK, specializing in woven textile design for apparel and home textiles.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns confirm a woven-textile specialism built almost entirely around plaid, tartan, and check: madras-style multicolor plaids (pink/blue/burgundy and pink/purple/green/navy colorways), pastel and jewel-tone tartans, a heathered tweed-effect plaid in teal/grey, and photographed woven jacquard-style textile swatches showing basket-weave/patchwork texture in monochrome and gold-on-charcoal. This is a studio built on woven structure and colorway variation rather than printed illustrative motifs.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

CAD-based woven textile design, per the studio's own description as one of the first UK studios to specialize in this process; selected images include what appear to be photographed physical woven swatches alongside digitally-rendered plaid/tartan repeats, consistent with a woven-design (rather than surface-print) practice.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Per the studio's own description, Paul Vogel works with "a diverse range of clients" across apparel and home textiles, produces seasonal collections backed by a large design archive, and is open to bespoke commission work. No specific named clients are confirmed *(unverified)*.

## Studio Background

Founded in 1991 by Paul Vogel following a two-year internship with Cecci Lido in Florence, Italy. The studio describes exhibiting at Première Vision in Paris and Heimtextil in Frankfurt, and travelling internationally to visit clients directly.

## Pattern Focus

Woven plaid, tartan, and check across a wide colorway range — from pastel and heathered tweed effects to jewel-tone madras-style multicolor — plus photographed woven jacquard/patchwork-texture swatches.
