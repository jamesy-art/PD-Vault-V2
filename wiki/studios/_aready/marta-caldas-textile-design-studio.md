## Overview

Marta Caldas Textile Design Studio creates original patterns for the apparel and home decor markets, reflecting Caldas's own perception of nature through hand-drawn, hand-painted, and digital motifs in traditional or contemporary styles. Designs are delivered as layered AI/PSD files in seamless repeat, prepared for production.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show bold, painterly abstraction built from overlapping organic shapes: mirrored blob and pebble-like forms, animal-print-style dot clusters, loopy line-drawn florals, brushy large-scale florals on saturated grounds, and a diagonal ribbon/chevron weave. Color and volume dominate over fine detail, consistent with the studio's own description of a "modern vision of volume and colour."

## Studio Background

Marta Caldas is a painter and photographer born on Spain's northern coast who later moved to the Canary Islands and now lives in Madrid. Her visual arts education spans London's Blake College and London College of Fashion, study in Brighton under Rowena Luke, and further study at ateliers in New York and Madrid, covering painting, sculpture, ceramics, illustration, fashion/textile design, and photography.

## Pattern Focus

Nature-derived abstraction — florals, organic blob/pebble forms, and animal-print-inspired dot clusters — reflecting her stated influences of sea, light, and wildlife from coastal Spain and the Canary Islands.
