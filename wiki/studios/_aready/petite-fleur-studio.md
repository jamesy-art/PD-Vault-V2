## Overview

Petite Fleur Studio is a UK print and pattern studio specialising in prints for children, created by illustrator and surface pattern designer Alice Potter. Alice has close to a decade's experience as an illustrator and licensed artist, with over 30 published children's books, and works across children's books, greeting cards, stationery, and patterns for toys and apparel.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected images show two related visual registers: bold, folk-influenced botanical illustration credited to Alice Potter individually (dark-ground florals with heavy outline work, a safari-animal print), alongside softer studio-branded work under the Petite Fleur Studio name — retro pastel daisy florals, a folk floral border stripe, and playful novelty conversational prints (space and rocket motifs, animal-face repeats, postage-stamp motifs, a woodland harlequin/diamond grid). Colour palettes run from bright and saturated (kids animal and space prints) to soft pastel (retro florals, animal faces). Line work is consistently clean and flat-coloured with bold outlines rather than painterly shading.

## Markets and Specialties

Children's publishing (picture books, activity books), greeting cards, stationery/notebooks, and licensed prints for toys and apparel, per the documented client list below.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected images show flat-colour, bold-outline digital illustration throughout, rather than hand-painted or textured work.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Documented clients: Abacus Card, American Greetings, Auzou, Charlesbridge, Cottage Door Press, Igloo Books, CBeebies Magazine, YoYo Books, Harper Collins US, Papercrafter Magazine, Papyrus Cards, Magi Cards, Raleigh Bikes UK, WH Smith, Quarto Publishing, I Am A Bookworm, Hallmark US, Graphique de France, Picollina Kids, Hollan Publishing, Little Sleepies, UK Greetings, Design Design DDI, Clementoni Toys, Salariya Book Company, Pigment Productions, Greetings Island, Boots Miniclub. Selected placement images corroborate this client mix directly: a greeting card design ("Dance and Twirl / Birthday Girl"), a Halloween card ("Trick or Treat"), a children's book/notebook cover ("Snappy Ideas"), and an educational flashcard-style illustration ("Apple").
