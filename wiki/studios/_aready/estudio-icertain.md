## Overview

Founded in 2012 by siblings Caio and Isabela, Estúdio Icertain is a Brazil-based textile design studio producing exclusive prints for the fashion and home decor markets. The studio has exhibited at Première Vision Paris since 2018 and helps organize ESTAMPAR, São Paulo's leading surface design event, giving it an established presence in both the Brazilian and international print trade.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show a deliberately wide stylistic range rather than a single house look: maximalist jungle prints pairing leopard silhouettes with tropical foliage, ornate botanical paisley scrollwork incorporating pineapples and butterflies, loose watercolor floral vines, blue-and-white chinoiserie-style florals, graphic stripe and scalloped-fan geometrics, and painterly watercolor animal studies. This breadth is consistent with a studio built to serve varied client briefs across markets.

## Markets and Specialties

- home
- fashion

The one selected placement image shows a tropical sunset motif applied to a garment/scarf-style mockup, consistent with resort and vacation-driven apparel alongside the studio's stated home and fashion focus.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Pattern imagery mixes hand-painted watercolor and gouache-style work (florals, animal studies) with flatter, graphic vector-style patterns (stripes, scalloped fans), suggesting the studio produces across both painterly and graphic styles *(unverified which technique is used for final production files)*.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Exhibiting at Première Vision Paris since 2018 signals an established presence on the international fashion trade-show circuit. The studio also works with a network of selected designers to broaden the range of prints it can offer clients.

## Studio Background

Founded in 2012 by brother-and-sister duo Caio and Isabela. The founders have developed their skills through courses including Designing a Fashion T-Shirt Collection and Digital Print on Textiles at Central Saint Martins, and Coolhunting at FAAP.

## Pattern Focus

Exclusive prints for fashion and home decor spanning tropical/animal motifs, botanical paisley, watercolor florals, chinoiserie-style blue-and-white florals, and graphic geometrics.
