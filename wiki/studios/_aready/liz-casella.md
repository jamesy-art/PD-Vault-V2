## Overview

Liz Casella is a print studio producing exclusive artwork for fashion and interior brands, with an in-house team offering a continually evolving print collection across womenswear, menswear, active, swim, and home.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern images show hand-painted work: loose watercolor florals with a soft, blurred quality, saturated red-and-gold botanical washes, a graphic peony rendered in bold outlined color blocks, and abstract horizontal watercolor washes in pink, rust, and sage. The aesthetic favors strong color and a feminine sensibility, consistent with the studio's own description.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected pattern images support the studio's stated hand-worked process — watercolor and gouache washes with pencil/pen detail — spanning soft blurred florals to bolder, more graphic outlined botanicals.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Described as selling globally to "contemporary clients and major retailers," though no specific client names are documented.

## Studio Background

Founder Liz Casella has worked in fashion and textiles for 18 years, with career experience spanning Sydney and Milan before building design studios in Los Angeles and New York over the past 5 years.

## Pattern Focus

Hand-painted florals and abstract washes in watercolor and gouache, ranging from soft blurred botanicals to bolder graphic color-block florals, per selected pattern images.
