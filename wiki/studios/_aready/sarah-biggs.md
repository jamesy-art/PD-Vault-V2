## Overview

Sarah Biggs Design Studio is a UK-based surface pattern studio specializing in hand-painted florals, based in Barnard Castle, County Durham. The studio develops prints for swim, activewear, women's fashion, and home and lifestyle categories, positioning its work as heirloom-quality alternatives to fast-moving trend cycles.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected pattern work spans delicate, ditsy watercolor florals (sprigs, berries, small blooms) to bolder tropical florals on saturated color grounds, alongside a muted abstract geometric composition in pink, teal, and red. The consistent thread across selected images is hand-painted, traditional-media artwork rather than vector or digital construction.

## Markets and Specialties

Swim, activewear, women's fashion, and home and lifestyle categories.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Works in hand-painted traditional media — botanical studies and floral sprigs are painted individually before being composed into repeats. One selected pattern shows a bolder, flat-color abstract composition, indicating the studio also works outside pure florals when the brief calls for it.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Positions itself as a full-service surface design partner, developing complete, market-ready print collections rather than single motifs, for swim, activewear, fashion, and home/lifestyle brands. Specific clients are not disclosed.
