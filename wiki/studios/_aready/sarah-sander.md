## Overview

Sarah Sander Design Studio is a German print design studio founded by Sarah Sander in 2017, combining surface pattern design with further training in 3D fashion design. The studio pairs floral hand-drawn prints with digital photo manipulation, using 3D fashion tools to visualize artwork on garments early in the design process.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Signature style blends hand-drawn floral illustration with digital photo manipulation — a hybrid fashion-print sensibility rather than a purely painterly one. No pattern images have been selected for review, so specific motifs are not yet documented.
