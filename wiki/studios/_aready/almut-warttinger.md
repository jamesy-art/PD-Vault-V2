## Overview

Almut Warttinger is a Berlin-based textile designer creating handcrafted repeat prints for fashion and interiors. With over a decade of experience and a strong award record from early in her career, the studio delivers original drawn designs that are production-ready and distinctive in colour.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Warttinger's work draws energy from music and dance — designs described as carrying rhythm, sometimes bold and expansive, sometimes fine and subtle. Selected Instagram images suggest vivid, original compositions with a handcrafted graphic quality. Collections are positioned as fresh in colour and trend-aware without being generic.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion and interiors. The studio produces repeat prints suited to apparel and interior textiles, delivered in full repeat and ready for production.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Original designs are created entirely by hand and supplied in full repeat. The studio does not document specific software or mixed-media methods; the hand-drawn origin is central to the positioning.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Warttinger presents an online collection of original designs to international trade buyers. No specific client names are documented in available sources.

## Studio Background

Almut Warttinger studied textile and surface design at art school Berlin-Weissensee, graduating with the highest grade. Awards include the Elsa-Neumann-Scholarship (2012), the Career Advancement Prize from the Wilhelm-Lorch Foundation (2012), and first place in the Innovation Award from the German Federation for Textiles and Fashion (2010). She participated in the COME'ON design festival in Como, Italy (2010). Press coverage has appeared in Tagesspiegel and publications of the Wilhelm-Lorch-Stiftung.

## Pattern Focus

Handcrafted repeat prints for fashion and interiors, characterised by original drawn compositions, vivid colour, and a rhythm-driven aesthetic influenced by music and movement.
