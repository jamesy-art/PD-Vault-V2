## Overview

Mary Jones Design is a UK surface pattern studio producing collections for interior and gift products worldwide, available to purchase or license, with collections viewable online or via virtual client meetings.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns show a deliberately varied, home-and-gift-oriented range: a playful line-drawn dog repeat, a traditional ditsy floral with butterflies, painterly rose and daffodil bouquets, a mid-century abstract geometric, an ornate Morris-style damask floral, a formal mirrored floral bouquet, and a cottage-garden watercolor floral stripe. The range confirms the studio's own description of "versatility and variety" over a single house style.

## Markets and Specialties

- home
- gifting

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Selected patterns indicate both hand-painted/watercolor florals and flat vector or digitally-rendered repeats, spanning traditional damask-style and contemporary abstract geometric work.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

No specific clients are confirmed; collections are positioned for home and gift product licensing and direct purchase.

## Studio Background

Established in 1982, Mary Jones Design has produced pattern for home and gift products for over 40 years, with collections continuously updated online.

## Pattern Focus

Broad home and gift focus: traditional ditsy and damask florals, painterly botanical bouquets, novelty conversational prints (dogs), and occasional abstract geometrics.
