## Overview

A3 Design Solutions is a Los Angeles-based print design agency led by founder Angela Arrigo. Established in the early 1990s, the agency has operated for over three decades as a bridge between international print studios and fashion and textile clients seeking curated, trend-relevant designs.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Selected patterns suggest a commercially oriented fashion range. A3 curates designs from international print studios; no single house aesthetic is documented in available sources.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion and textiles. The agency model positions A3 within the sourcing and supply chain layer of the global fashion industry, connecting creative studios to buyers across diverse market segments.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

A3 operates as a design agency rather than a production studio, curating and placing print designs from international creative studios to client briefs. Specific production methods are not documented.

## Studio Background

Founded in Los Angeles in the early 1990s by Angela Arrigo, A3 Design Solutions has operated continuously for over thirty years. Arrigo built the agency around her knowledge of the global textile trade, with a focus on connecting international print studios to fashion industry buyers and infusing innovation into the supply chain.
