## Overview

Esti Studio Inc. is a New York-based textile design agency established in 1990 by Esti, a former textile designer, design director, and stylist for textile converters. The agency is the largest of its kind in New York and represents fifteen prestigious design studios — predominantly European — to an East Coast US clientele across fashion, home furnishing, and paper products.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

As an agency, Esti Studio's aesthetic range is shaped by the fifteen studios it represents. Its offer spans prints, embroideries, and embellishments across fashion and home furnishing categories, sourced from studios recognised as among the most influential in Europe.

## Markets and Specialties

Fashion, home furnishing, and paper products. The agency maintains strong relationships with European design studios and serves East Coast US buyers across these sectors.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Esti Studio operates as a presentation and sales agency for its fifteen partner studios rather than producing designs in-house. The studio sources prints, embroideries, and embellishments from European practices and presents them to US buyers.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

Esti Studio serves buyers across fashion, home furnishing, and paper products, with a focus on East Coast US clientele. Five sales associates manage client relationships. The agency's studio roster includes ANTEPRIMA, BERNINI, BLOEMIST, CAKE CREATIONS, ROBERT VERNET, DR DISEGNI, MADE IN BRIGHTON, MUSTICSTYLE, PATTERN TEXTILE, POP CREATE, SPROUT STORY, and WHISTON & WRIGHT.

Today it operates with five sales associates representing fifteen studios.
