## Overview

LAND is a Midwest US women-owned print design studio founded and run by artists Lindsay and Lily. With over thirteen years of collaboration, the studio produces vintage-inspired, colour-rich prints that begin by hand — painted, drawn, or collaged — before being refined into polished digital artwork for fashion and home markets.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

LAND's design language combines a vintage-inspired sensibility with playful colour and a strong handcraft foundation. The studio's selected patterns reflect a warm, expressive palette that retains artisanal quality while remaining commercially versatile.

## Markets and Specialties

Print design for fashion and home. Services also include Art Direction & Concept and Trend Forecasting.

## Techniques and Artwork Process

Designs originate as hand-painted, drawn, or collaged artwork before development into production-ready digital files. Output is supplied in Adobe formats; physical artwork is available on request.

## Clients and Commercial Positioning

LAND operates as an independent studio offering both design services and direct print sales. No specific client names are disclosed.
