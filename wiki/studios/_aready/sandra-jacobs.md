## Overview

Sandra Jacobs Design is a surface design studio based in the Netherlands, producing patterns for interior textiles, wallpaper, fashion, ceramics, and paper goods. The studio has been operating for more than 25 years and presents its collections at international trade shows in New York, Frankfurt, and Amsterdam.

## Studio Style and Aesthetic

Described as vibrant, trend-led, and contemporary, combining handpainted and digital design methods with an emphasis on inventive compositions and sophisticated color combinations. No pattern images have been selected for review, so specific motifs are not yet documented.

## Markets and Specialties

## Techniques and Artwork Process
